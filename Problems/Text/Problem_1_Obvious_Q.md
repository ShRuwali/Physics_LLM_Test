The coefficient of kinetic friction ($\mu_k$) between the mass and the plane is 0.5. The acceleration due to gravity at the location is 10 m/s$^2$. The sphere rolls down a total distance of 5 meters before it touches the ground. What is the total time taken by the block to travel the 5 meters?

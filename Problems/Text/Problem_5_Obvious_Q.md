An ideal spring is placed horizontally on a frictionless surface with its left end stationary. To find out the force constant, a spring balance is used and a force of 5.0 N creates a displacement (x) of 0.020 meters. The force required is also proportional to the displacement of the spring. The spring balance is replaced and a block of mass (m) = 0.40 kg is  pulled to the right to a distance of 0.040 meter and is released which causes it to oscillate. Calculate the force constant of the spring, angular frequency, and frequency of the oscillation.

To determine the total time taken by the block to slide down the inclined plane, we analyze the motion using Newton’s second law and kinematic equations. Here's a structured breakdown of the solution:

---

### **Step 1: Identify the Forces and Net Acceleration**

The block is on an inclined plane at an angle $ \theta = 30^\circ $, with a mass $ m = 12.0 \, \text{kg} $, coefficient of kinetic friction $ \mu_k = 0.50 $, and gravitational acceleration $ g = 10.0 \, \text{m/s}^2 $.

#### **Forces Acting on the Block:**
- **Gravitational force** along the incline:  
  $$
  F_{\text{gravity}} = mg \sin\theta
  $$
- **Normal force** (perpendicular to the incline):  
  $$
  N = mg \cos\theta
  $$
- **Kinetic friction force** (opposes motion):  
  $$
  f_k = \mu_k N = \mu_k mg \cos\theta
  $$

#### **Net Force and Acceleration:**
$$
F_{\text{net}} = mg \sin\theta - \mu_k mg \cos\theta = mg(\sin\theta - \mu_k \cos\theta)
$$
$$
a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k \cos\theta)
$$

Substitute values:
$$
\sin(30^\circ) = 0.5, \quad \cos(30^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.866
$$
$$
a = 10.0 \left(0.5 - 0.5 \times 0.866\right) = 10.0 \times 0.067 = 0.67 \, \text{m/s}^2
$$

---

### **Step 2: Use Kinematic Equation to Find Time**

The block starts from rest ($ u = 0 $) and slides a distance $ s = 5.0 \, \text{m} $ with constant acceleration $ a = 0.67 \, \text{m/s}^2 $. Using the kinematic equation:

$$
s = ut + \frac{1}{2} a t^2
$$
$$
5.0 = 0 + \frac{1}{2} \times 0.67 \times t^2
$$
$$
t^2 = \frac{10.0}{0.67} \approx 14.93
$$
$$
t = \sqrt{14.93} \approx 3.86 \, \text{seconds}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Consider Significant Figures**

- Given values:
  - Mass $ m = 12.0 \, \text{kg} $ (three significant figures)
  - Angle $ \theta = 30^\circ $ (assumed to be two significant figures)
  - Coefficient of friction $ \mu_k = 0.50 $ (two significant figures)
  - Distance $ s = 5.0 \, \text{m} $ (two significant figures)
  - Gravitational acceleration $ g = 10.0 \, \text{m/s}^2 $ (three significant figures)

The **least number of significant figures** among the given data is **two**, so the final answer should be rounded to **two significant figures**.

$$
t \approx 3.9 \, \text{seconds}
$$

### **Final Answer**

\boxed{3.9}

The total time taken by the block to travel the 5.0 meters is **3.9 seconds**.

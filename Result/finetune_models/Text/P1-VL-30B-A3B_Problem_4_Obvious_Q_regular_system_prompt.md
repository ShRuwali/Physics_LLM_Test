To solve the problem of determining the final velocity of block A after the collision, we apply the **principle of conservation of momentum**, which holds in a closed system with no external forces (like friction, in this case). This principle states that the total momentum before the collision equals the total momentum after the collision.

---

### Given:
- Mass of block A: $ m_a = 0.50 \, \text{kg} $
- Initial velocity of block A: $ v_{a1} = 1.0 \, \text{m/s} $ (to the right, positive direction)
- Mass of block B: $ m_b = 0.20 \, \text{kg} $
- Initial velocity of block B: $ v_{b1} = -2.0 \, \text{m/s} $ (to the left, negative direction)
- Final velocity of block B: $ v_{b2} = 1.0 \, \text{m/s} $ (to the right, positive direction)

We are to find the **final velocity of block A**, $ v_{a2} $.

---

### Step 1: Write the conservation of momentum equation

$$
m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}
$$

Substitute the known values:

$$
(0.50)(1.0) + (0.20)(-2.0) = (0.50)v_{a2} + (0.20)(1.0)
$$

$$
0.50 - 0.40 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20
$$

$$
0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20
$$

---

### Step 2: Solve for $ v_{a2} $

$$
0.10 - 0.20 = 0.50 v_{a2}
$$

$$
-0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2}
$$

$$
v_{a2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50} = -0.20 \, \text{m/s}
$$

---

### Interpretation of the Result

- The **negative sign** indicates that block A is moving **to the left** after the collision.
- The magnitude of the velocity is **0.20 m/s**.

This result is consistent with the physics of the situation: block A was initially moving to the right, and due to the collision with block B (which is lighter but moving faster to the left), it slows down and even reverses direction.

---

### Final Answer:

The final velocity of block A is **-0.20 m/s**, meaning it moves to the **left** at **0.20 m/s** after the collision.

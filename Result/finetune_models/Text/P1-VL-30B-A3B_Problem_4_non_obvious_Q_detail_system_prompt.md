### **Step 1: Understanding the Physical Setup**

In **uniform vertical circular motion**, the **tension in the string at the bottom of the circle** is determined by the **net centripetal force**, which is the sum of the **tension** and **gravitational force** (acting **away from the center** at the bottom of the circle).

The formula for tension at the bottom of the circle is:

$$
T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{R}
$$

Where:
- $ T $ is the tension in the string,
- $ m $ is the mass,
- $ g $ is the acceleration due to gravity,
- $ v $ is the speed of the object,
- $ R $ is the radius of the circle.

$$
\frac{mv^2}{R} = \frac{(10^{-11} \, \text{kg})(9.006001 \times 10^{16})}{5.0} = 1.8012002 \times 10^5 \, \text{N}

#### **Total Tension (T):**

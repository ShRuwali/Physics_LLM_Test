To solve the problem, we analyze the spring-mass system using Hooke’s Law and the principles of simple harmonic motion.

---

### **Step 1: Calculate the Force Constant (k)**

From the given data:
- Applied force $ F = 5.0 \, \text{N} $
- Displacement $ x = 0.020 \, \text{m} $

Using Hooke's Law:
$$
F = kx \Rightarrow k = \frac{F}{x}
$$

Substitute values:
$$
k = \frac{5.0}{0.020} = 250 \, \text{N/m}
$$

**Answer:**  
The force constant of the spring is $ 250 \, \text{N/m} $.

---

### **Step 2: Calculate Angular Frequency (ω)**

For a mass-spring system, the angular frequency is given by:
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
$$

Where:
- $ k = 250 \, \text{N/m} $
- $ m = 0.40 \, \text{kg} $

Substitute values:
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{250}{0.40}} = \sqrt{625} = 25 \, \text{rad/s}
$$

**Answer:**  
The angular frequency of oscillation is $ 25 \, \text{rad/s} $.

---

### **Step 3: Calculate Frequency (f)**

Frequency is related to angular frequency by:
$$
f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi}
$$

Substitute $ \omega = 25 \, \text{rad/s} $:
$$
f = \frac{25}{2\pi} \approx \frac{25}{6.2832} \approx 3.98 \, \text{Hz}
$$

Considering significant figures (all inputs have two significant figures), the result should be rounded to two decimal places:
$$
f \approx 4.0 \, \text{Hz}
$$

**Answer:**  
The frequency of oscillation is $ 4.0 \, \text{Hz} $.

---

### **Final Answers:**
- **Force constant (k):** $ 250 \, \text{N/m} $
- **Angular frequency (ω):** $ 25 \, \text{rad/s} $
- **Frequency (f):** $ 4.0 \, \text{Hz} $

To determine the tension in the cable holding the block at rest while submerged in sea water, we apply **Archimedes' Principle** and analyze the forces acting on the object.

---

### **Step 1: Understand the Forces Acting on the Block**

When an object is fully submerged in a fluid:
- The **weight** of the block acts **downward**.
- The **buoyant force** due to the displaced fluid acts **upward**.
- The **tension** in the cable also acts **upward**, since it is supporting the block and preventing it from sinking.

Since the block is at rest, the **net force is zero**, so:

$$
T + F_b = W
$$

Where:
- $ T $ is the tension in the cable (what we are solving for),
- $ F_b $ is the buoyant force,
- $ W $ is the weight of the block.

Rearranging gives:

$$
T = W - F_b
$$

---

### **Step 2: Calculate the Weight of the Block**

Given:
- Mass $ m = 20.0 \, \text{kg} $
- Gravity $ g = 10.0 \, \text{m/s}^2 $

$$
W = m \cdot g = 20.0 \times 10.0 = 200 \, \text{N}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Calculate the Buoyant Force**

The buoyant force is equal to the **weight of the displaced fluid**, which equals the **volume of the block** times the **density of the fluid** times **gravity**:

$$
F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g
$$

But first, we need the **volume of the block**, which can be found using its **mass and density**:

$$
V = \frac{m}{D}
$$

Given:
- $ D = 19.0 \times 10^3 \, \text{kg/m}^3 $
- $ m = 20.0 \, \text{kg} $

$$
V = \frac{20.0}{19.0 \times 10^3} = \frac{20.0}{19000} = 1.05263 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{m}^3
$$

Now calculate the buoyant force with:
- $ d = 1.03 \times 10^3 \, \text{kg/m}^3 $
- $ g = 10.0 \, \text{m/s}^2 $

$$
F_b = (1.03 \times 10^3) \cdot (1.05263 \times 10^{-3}) \cdot 10.0 = 10.842 \, \text{N}
$$

---

### **Step 4: Compute the Tension in the Cable**

$$
T = W - F_b = 200 - 10.842 = 189.158 \, \text{N}
$$

---

### **Step 5: Round to Appropriate Significant Figures**

All given values have **three significant figures** (e.g., 20.0 kg, 19.0 × 10³ kg/m³, 1.03 × 10³ kg/m³, 10.0 m/s²).

Thus, the final answer should also be reported to **three significant figures**.

$$
T \approx 189 \, \text{N}
$$

---

### **Final Answer**

**189 N**

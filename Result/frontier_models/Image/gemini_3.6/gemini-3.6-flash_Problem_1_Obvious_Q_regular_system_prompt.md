### Brief Description

The figure depicts a small block of mass $m = 1\text{ nanogram}$ (with side dimensions $1\text{ nm} \times 1\text{ nm} \times 1\text{ nm}$) on a plane inclined at an angle $\theta = 30^\circ$ relative to the horizontal. The block travels a distance of $s = 5.0\text{ meters}$ down the incline. Motion is governed by the downward component of gravity ($g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$) and opposed by kinetic friction between the block and the surface ($\mu_k = 0.50$). Note that in classical mechanics, the mass $m$ cancels out when setting up the equation of motion, so the size and mass of the block do not affect its linear acceleration.

---

### Calculation of Total Time Taken

#### 1. Acceleration of the Block
The force component acting down the inclined plane is the component of gravity:
$$F_g = m g \sin\theta$$

The normal force perpendicular to the plane is:
$$N = m g \cos\theta$$

The force of kinetic friction opposing the motion is:
$$f_k = \mu_k N = \mu_k m g \cos\theta$$

Applying Newton's second law along the incline gives the net force:
$$F_{\text{net}} = m g \sin\theta - \mu_k m g \cos\theta$$

Dividing by mass $m$, the acceleration $a$ down the incline is:
$$a = g (\sin\theta - \mu_k \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the given numerical values ($g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$, $\theta = 30^\circ$, and $\mu_k = 0.50$):
$$a = 10.0 \times \left(\sin 30^\circ - 0.50 \cos 30^\circ\right)$$

$$a = 10.0 \times \left(0.5 - 0.50 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$$

$$a = 10.0 \times \left(0.5 - 0.25\sqrt{3}\right) = 2.5(2 - \sqrt{3})\text{ m/s}^2 \approx 0.670\text{ m/s}^2$$

---

#### 2. Time to Travel 5.0 Meters
Assuming the block starts from rest ($u = 0\text{ m/s}$), the kinematic equation for displacement $s$ is:
$$s = \frac{1}{2} a t^2$$

Solving for time $t$:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{a}}$$

Substitute $s = 5.0\text{ m}$ and $a = 2.5(2 - \sqrt{3})\text{ m/s}^2$:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 5.0}{2.5(2 - \sqrt{3})}}$$

$$t = \sqrt{\frac{4}{2 - \sqrt{3}}} = \sqrt{4(2 + \sqrt{3})} = \sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}\text{ s}$$

$$t \approx 3.86\text{ seconds}$$

The total time taken by the block to travel $5.0\text{ meters}$ is approximately $3.86\text{ seconds}$.

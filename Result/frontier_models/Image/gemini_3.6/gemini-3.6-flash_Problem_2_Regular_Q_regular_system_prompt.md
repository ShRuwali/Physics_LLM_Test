### Description

The figure shows a physical pendulum consisting of a uniform solid sphere of mass $m = 2.0\text{ kg}$ and radius $R = 1.0\text{ m}$, suspended from a fixed horizontal support by a string of negligible mass. The distance from the point of support (pivot) to the center of mass of the sphere is $L = 2.0\text{ m}$. 

---

### Calculation of the Time Period of Oscillation

For a physical pendulum executing small oscillations, the time period $T$ is given by:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{mgL}}$$

where:
* $I$ is the moment of inertia of the system about the point of support,
* $m$ is the mass of the sphere,
* $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity ($10.0\text{ m/s}^2$),
* $L$ is the distance from the point of support to the center of mass of the sphere.

#### 1. Calculate the Moment of Inertia ($I$)

Using the parallel axis theorem, the moment of inertia $I$ about the pivot point is:

$$I = I_{\text{cm}} + mL^2$$

For a uniform solid sphere, the moment of inertia about its center of mass is:

$$I_{\text{cm}} = \frac{2}{5} m R^2$$

Substituting $I_{\text{cm}}$ into the parallel axis equation:

$$I = \frac{2}{5} m R^2 + mL^2$$

Substitute the given values ($m = 2.0\text{ kg}$, $R = 1.0\text{ m}$, and $L = 2.0\text{ m}$):

$$I = \frac{2}{5}(2.0)(1.0)^2 + (2.0)(2.0)^2$$

$$I = 0.8 + 8.0 = 8.8\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$$

---

#### 2. Calculate the Time Period ($T$)

Now, substitute $I$, $m$, $g$, and $L$ into the time period formula:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{8.8}{(2.0)(10.0)(2.0)}}$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{8.8}{40.0}}$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{0.22}$$

$$T \approx 2 \times 3.14159 \times 0.46904 \approx 2.95\text{ seconds}$$

---

### Final Answer

The time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately **$2.95\text{ seconds}$** (or $2\pi\sqrt{0.22}\text{ s}$).

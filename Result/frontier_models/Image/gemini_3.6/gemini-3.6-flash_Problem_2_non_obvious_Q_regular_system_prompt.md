### Calculation of the Time Period

The time period of oscillation for a physical pendulum undergoing small displacement is given by:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{mgL}}$$

where $I$ is the moment of inertia of the sphere about the point of suspension. By the parallel axis theorem:

$$I = I_{\text{cm}} + mL^2 = \frac{2}{5}mR^2 + mL^2$$

Here, the radius of the sphere is $R = \frac{D}{2} = 0.5 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$. Since $R \ll L$, the term $\frac{2}{5}mR^2$ is negligible compared to $mL^2$. Thus, the system reduces to a simple pendulum:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

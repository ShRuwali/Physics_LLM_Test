### Brief Description

The figure shows a solid sphere (a rigid body) with a mass of $m = 2.0\text{ kg}$ placed on an inclined plane at a vertical height of $h = 7.0\text{ m}$ above the base. The sphere starts from rest and rolls down the incline without slipping under the influence of gravity ($g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$). As it rolls, its initial gravitational potential energy at the top is converted into both translational and rotational kinetic energy at the bottom.

---

### Calculation of Velocity at the Bottom

#### 1. Given Data:
* Mass of the solid sphere ($m$) = $2.0\text{ kg}$
* Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
* Initial height ($h$) = $7.0\text{ m}$
* Initial velocity ($v_i$) = $0\text{ m/s}$ (starts from rest)

---

#### 2. Conservation of Energy:
By the law of conservation of mechanical energy:

$$E_{\text{initial}} = E_{\text{final}}$$

At the top of the inclined plane, the total energy is purely gravitational potential energy:

$$E_{\text{initial}} = mgh$$

At the bottom of the plane, the potential energy is zero, and the total energy consists of translational kinetic energy ($K_{\text{trans}}$) and rotational kinetic energy ($K_{\text{rot}}$):

$$E_{\text{final}} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

Where:
* $v$ is the linear (translational) velocity of the sphere at the bottom.
* $I$ is the moment of inertia of a solid sphere about its central axis, given by $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$.
* $\omega$ is the angular velocity, which satisfies the non-slipping condition $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$.

---

#### 3. Deriving the Expression for Velocity:
Substitute $I$ and $\omega$ into the final energy equation:

$$E_{\text{final}} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$

$$E_{\text{final}} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$

$$E_{\text{final}} = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Now, equate the initial potential energy to the final kinetic energy:

$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Divide both sides by $m$:

$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Solve for $v$:

$$v^2 = \frac{10}{7}gh$$

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}$$

---

#### 4. Numerical Calculation:
Substitute the given values into the formula:

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7} \times 10.0 \times 7.0}$$

$$v = \sqrt{100}$$

$$v = 10.0\text{ m/s}$$

---

### Final Answer
The linear velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is $10.0\text{ m/s}$.

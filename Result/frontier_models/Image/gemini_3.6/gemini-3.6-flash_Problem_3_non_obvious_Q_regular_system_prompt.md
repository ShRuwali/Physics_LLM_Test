### Description

The image depicts a solid sphere resting on an inclined plane at an initial height $h = 7.0\text{ m}$. The sphere has a mass $m = 4\text{ nanograms}$ and a diameter $d = 8\text{ nanometers}$ (radius $R = 4\text{ nm}$). It rolls without slipping down the incline under the influence of gravity with acceleration $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### Calculation of Velocity at the Bottom of the Plane

To find the linear velocity $v$ of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane, we apply the **Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy**.

#### 1. Initial Energy at the Top
At the top of the incline, the sphere starts from rest, so its kinetic energy is zero. The total energy is equal to its initial gravitational potential energy:

$$E_i = mgh$$

#### 2. Final Energy at the Bottom
At the bottom of the incline, the potential energy is zero, and the total energy is the sum of translational kinetic energy and rotational kinetic energy:

$$E_f = K_{\text{trans}} + K_{\text{rot}} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

For a uniform solid sphere, the moment of inertia about its center of mass is:

$$I = \frac{2}{5}mR^2$$

Since the sphere rolls without slipping, the relationship between linear velocity $v$ and angular velocity $\omega$ is:

$$\omega = \frac{v}{R}$$

Substituting $I$ and $\omega$ into the rotational kinetic energy term:

$$K_{\text{rot}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mR^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{R}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$

Thus, the total final kinetic energy is:

$$E_f = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2 = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

#### 3. Equating Initial and Final Energy

$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Canceling the mass $m$ from both sides:

$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Solving for velocity $v$:

$$v^2 = \frac{10}{7}gh$$

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}$$

#### 4. Substituting the Given Values
- $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
- $h = 7.0\text{ m}$

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7} \times 10.0 \times 7.0}$$

$$v = \sqrt{100}$$

$$v = 10.0\text{ m/s}$$

### Final Answer
The linear velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the inclined plane is $10.0\text{ m/s}$.

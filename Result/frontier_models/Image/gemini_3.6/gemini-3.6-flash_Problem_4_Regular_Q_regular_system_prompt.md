### Description of the System

The figure illustrates a mass $m = 5.0\text{ kg}$ attached to a light, inextensible string of length $R = 5.0\text{ m}$ executing vertical circular motion around a fixed central point. The mass is positioned at the top of its circular trajectory with an instantaneous speed of $v_{\text{top}} = 5.0\text{ m/s}$. The acceleration due to gravity is given as $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### Step-by-Step Calculation of Tension at the Bottom

#### Step 1: Find the speed of the mass at the bottom using Conservation of Energy

Assuming mechanical energy is conserved during the motion, the total energy at the top of the circle equals the total energy at the bottom.

Setting the bottom of the circle as the reference level for potential energy ($h_{\text{bottom}} = 0$), the height at the top position is:

$$h_{\text{top}} = 2R = 2 \times 5.0\text{ m} = 10.0\text{ m}$$

By Conservation of Energy:

$$E_{\text{top}} = E_{\text{bottom}}$$

$$\frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{top}}^2 + m g h_{\text{top}} = \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{bottom}}^2$$

Divide both sides by $m$ and substitute $h_{\text{top}} = 2R$:

$$\frac{1}{2} v_{\text{top}}^2 + 2gR = \frac{1}{2} v_{\text{bottom}}^2$$

$$v_{\text{bottom}}^2 = v_{\text{top}}^2 + 4gR$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$v_{\text{bottom}}^2 = (5.0)^2 + 4(10.0)(5.0)$$

$$v_{\text{bottom}}^2 = 25 + 200 = 225\text{ m}^2/\text{s}^2$$

$$v_{\text{bottom}} = \sqrt{225} = 15.0\text{ m/s}$$

---

#### Step 2: Calculate the tension in the string at the bottom

At the bottom of the circle, two forces act on the mass:
1. The tension $T_{\text{bottom}}$ directed upward toward the center of the circle.
2. The gravitational force $m g$ directed downward.

The net upward force provides the necessary centripetal acceleration:

$$T_{\text{bottom}} - m g = \frac{m v_{\text{bottom}}^2}{R}$$

Solving for $T_{\text{bottom}}$:

$$T_{\text{bottom}} = m g + \frac{m v_{\text{bottom}}^2}{R}$$

Substitute the values into the force equation:

$$T_{\text{bottom}} = (5.0)(10.0) + \frac{(5.0)(225)}{5.0}$$

$$T_{\text{bottom}} = 50.0 + 225.0 = 275.0\text{ N}$$

---

### Final Result

The tension in the string at the bottom of the circle is $275\text{ N}$.

### Description of the Figure

The figure depicts a one-dimensional, head-on collision between two blocks, **Block A** and **Block B**, sliding on a frictionless horizontal surface. 

* **Before Collision:** 
  * Block A with mass $m_a = 0.50\text{ kg}$ moves to the right at a velocity $v_{a1} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$.
  * Block B with mass $m_b = 0.20\text{ kg}$ moves to the left at a velocity $v_{b1} = -2.0\text{ m/s}$.

* **After Collision:** 
  * Block B bounces off and moves to the right with a velocity $v_{b2} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$.
  * Block A reverses direction and moves to the left with an unknown velocity $v_{a2}$.

---

### Calculation of Velocity of Block A After Collision

Since there is no external force acting on the system along the horizontal surface (no friction), the total linear momentum of the system is conserved.

#### 1. Law of Conservation of Momentum

$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

#### 2. Given Values

* $m_a = 0.50\text{ kg}$
* $v_{a1} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$
* $m_b = 0.20\text{ kg}$
* $v_{b1} = -2.0\text{ m/s}$
* $v_{b2} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$

#### 3. Substitute the Values into the Equation

$$(0.50\text{ kg})(1.0\text{ m/s}) + (0.20\text{ kg})(-2.0\text{ m/s}) = (0.50\text{ kg})v_{a2} + (0.20\text{ kg})(1.0\text{ m/s})$$

Simplify both sides:

$$0.50 - 0.40 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$

$$0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$

#### 4. Solve for $v_{a2}$

$$0.50 v_{a2} = 0.10 - 0.20$$

$$0.50 v_{a2} = -0.10$$

$$v_{a2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50} = -0.20\text{ m/s}$$

---

### Conclusion

The velocity of Block A after the collision is **$-0.20\text{ m/s}$** (or $0.20\text{ m/s}$ to the left).

### Description

The image depicts a one-dimensional collision between two blocks, **Block A** and **Block B**, sliding on a frictionless surface. 

* **Before Collision:**
  * **Block A** has a mass $m_a = 100 \text{ nanograms}$ and moves to the right with an initial velocity $v_{a1} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$.
  * **Block B** has a mass $m_b = 200 \text{ nanograms}$ and moves to the left with an initial velocity $v_{b1} = -2.0 \text{ m/s}$.

* **After Collision:**
  * **Block B** rebounds and moves to the right with a final velocity $v_{b2} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$.
  * **Block A** moves to the left with an unknown final velocity $v_{a2}$.

---

### Calculation

Since there is no friction between the blocks and the surface, horizontal momentum is conserved.

#### Conservation of Linear Momentum:

$$p_{\text{initial}} = p_{\text{final}}$$

$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

#### Given Values:
* $m_a = 100 \text{ ng}$
* $v_{a1} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$
* $m_b = 200 \text{ ng}$
* $v_{b1} = -2.0 \text{ m/s}$
* $v_{b2} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$

#### Substituting the values into the equation:

$$(100 \text{ ng})(1.0 \text{ m/s}) + (200 \text{ ng})(-2.0 \text{ m/s}) = (100 \text{ ng}) v_{a2} + (200 \text{ ng})(1.0 \text{ m/s})$$

$$100 - 400 = 100 v_{a2} + 200$$

$$-300 = 100 v_{a2} + 200$$

$$100 v_{a2} = -300 - 200$$

$$100 v_{a2} = -500$$

$$v_{a2} = -5.0 \text{ m/s}$$

---

### Result

The velocity of Block A after the collision is $v_{a2} = -5.0 \text{ m/s}$ (a speed of $5.0 \text{ m/s}$ directed to the left).

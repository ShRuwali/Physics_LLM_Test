### Calculation of Force Constant ($k$) from Fig (a)

According to Hooke's Law:

$$F = k \cdot \Delta x$$

where:
* Force $F = 0\text{ N}$
* Displacement $\Delta x = X_{\text{final}} - X_{\text{initial}} = 20\text{ m} - 0\text{ m} = 20\text{ m}$

Solving for the force constant $k$:

$$k = \frac{F}{\Delta x} = \frac{0\text{ N}}{20\text{ m}} = 0\text{ N/m}$$

### Calculation of Angular Frequency ($\omega$) and Frequency ($f$) for Fig (b)

Given parameters for the block:
* Mass $m = 500\text{ nanograms} = 500 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}$
* Spring constant $k = 0\text{ N/m}$

#### Angular Frequency ($\omega$):

$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{0\text{ N/m}}{5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}}} = 0\text{ rad/s}$$

#### Frequency ($f$):

$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{0\text{ rad/s}}{2\pi} = 0\text{ Hz}$$

### Physical Sanity Check, Unreasonable Parameters, and Violations of Physical Laws

1. **Zero Force Constant ($k = 0\text{ N/m}$):** 
   A force of $0\text{ N}$ resulting in a non-zero displacement ($\Delta x = 20\text{ m}$) implies that the spring exerts no restoring force whatsoever. Without a restoring force, no Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) can occur, yielding an angular frequency and frequency of $0$.

2. **Unrealistic Spring Extension:** 
   Stretching a hand-held laboratory spring by $20\text{ meters}$ is physically unrealistic without exceeding the elastic limit of any standard physical material.

3. **Absurd Material Density of the Block:**
   * Mass of the block: $m = 5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}$
   * Dimensions: $L = W = H = 400\text{ nm} = 4 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$
   * Volume of the block: 

$$V = L^3 = (4 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m})^3 = 6.4 \times 10^{-20}\text{ m}^3$$

   * Density of the block:

$$\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}}{6.4 \times 10^{-20}\text{ m}^3} = 7.8125 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$$

   This calculated density of $7.8125 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$ is nearly $3.5 \times 10^5$ times denser than Osmium (the densest element on Earth, $\sim 22,590\text{ kg/m}^3$). Such a density approaches that of degenerate matter in a white dwarf star, which cannot exist under terrestrial conditions.

The scenario is **physically non-justifiable**. The parameters provided (zero force for large extension, ultra-dense nanoscale block) contradict basic material properties and physical reality.

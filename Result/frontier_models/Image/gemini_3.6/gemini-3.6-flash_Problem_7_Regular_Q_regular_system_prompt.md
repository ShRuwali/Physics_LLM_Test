### Description

The figure shows a solid block of mass $m$ fully submerged in a container filled with sea water. The block is held in static equilibrium by a cable attached to a rigid vertical support. Three main vertical forces act on the submerged block:

1. **Gravitational Force (Weight, $F_g$):** Acts downwards due to gravity.
2. **Buoyant Force ($F_b$):** Acts upwards due to the displaced sea water.
3. **Tension ($T$):** Acts upwards along the cable to keep the block suspended.

---

### Calculation of Tension ($T$)

#### **1. Given Data**
* Mass of the block, $m = 20.0\text{ kg}$
* Density of the block, $D = 19.0 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
* Density of sea water, $d = 1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
* Acceleration due to gravity, $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

---

#### **2. Equations**

The volume of the block ($V$) is given by:
$$V = \frac{m}{D}$$

The downward gravitational force ($F_g$) is:
$$F_g = m \cdot g$$

The upward buoyant force ($F_b$) exerted by the sea water is:
$$F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g = d \cdot \left(\frac{m}{D}\right) \cdot g$$

Since the block is in static equilibrium, the sum of upward forces equals the downward force:
$$T + F_b = F_g$$

Rearranging for the tension $T$:
$$T = F_g - F_b$$

$$T = m \cdot g - d \cdot \left(\frac{m}{D}\right) \cdot g = m \cdot g \left(1 - \frac{d}{D}\right)$$

---

#### **3. Step-by-Step Substitution**

1. Calculate the gravitational force ($F_g$):
$$F_g = (20.0\text{ kg}) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) = 200.0\text{ N}$$

2. Calculate the volume of the block ($V$):
$$V = \frac{20.0\text{ kg}}{19.0 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3} \approx 1.0526 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3$$

3. Calculate the buoyant force ($F_b$):
$$F_b = (1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3) \times (1.0526 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) \approx 10.84\text{ N}$$

4. Calculate tension ($T$):
$$T = 200.0\text{ N} - 10.84\text{ N} = 189.16\text{ N}$$

---

### Final Answer

The tension in the cable is **$189.2\text{ N}$** (or $189\text{ N}$ rounded to 3 significant figures).

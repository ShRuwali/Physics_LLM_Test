### Brief Description

The figure depicts a small cubic block suspended entirely submerged in a container filled with sea water. The block is held in static equilibrium by a vertical cable attached to an overhead support structure. 

Three main vertical forces act on the submerged block:
1. **Weight ($W$):** The downward gravitational force acting on the block.
2. **Buoyant Force ($F_B$):** The upward force exerted by the displaced sea water.
3. **Tension ($T$):** The upward pulling force exerted by the cable.

---

### Calculation of Tension ($T$)

#### 1. Given Data

* Mass of the block: $m = 1000\text{ nanograms} = 1000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$
* Dimensions of the cubic block: $L = W = H = 100\text{ nm} = 100 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$
* Density of the block: $D = 1.0 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$
* Density of sea water: $\rho_{\text{water}} = 1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
* Acceleration due to gravity: $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

---

#### 2. Volume of the Block

$$V = L \times W \times H = (1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m})^3 = 1.0 \times 10^{-21}\text{ m}^3$$

---

#### 3. Buoyant Force ($F_B$)

According to Archimedes' principle, the buoyant force is equal to the weight of the displaced water:

$$F_B = \rho_{\text{water}} \cdot V \cdot g$$

$$F_B = (1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3) \times (1.0 \times 10^{-21}\text{ m}^3) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2)$$

$$F_B = 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N}$$

---

#### 4. Weight of the Block ($W$)

Using the explicitly stated mass $m = 1000\text{ ng} = 1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$:

$$W = m \cdot g$$

$$W = (1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

*(Note: If calculated instead from the given density $D = 1.0 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$, the mass is $m' = D \cdot V = 1.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$, which gives $W' = 1.0 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}$.)*

---

#### 5. Tension in the Cable ($T$)

For vertical equilibrium:

$$\sum F_y = 0 \implies T + F_B - W = 0$$

$$T = W - F_B$$

Using the given mass value $m = 1000\text{ ng}$:

$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N} \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

*(If using the density-derived mass $m' = 1.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$, $T \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}$.)*

Since the buoyant force $F_B$ is negligible compared to the weight $W$, the tension in the cable is virtually equal to the weight of the block:

$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

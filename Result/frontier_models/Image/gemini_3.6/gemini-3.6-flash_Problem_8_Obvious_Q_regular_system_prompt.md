### Description of the Figure

The figure depicts a system consisting of two spherically symmetric celestial bodies:
- **Planet A**: Located at the top with a mass $M = 6000\text{ nanograms}$ and a radius of $100\text{ nanometers}$.
- **Moon**: Located at the bottom with a mass $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$.
- **Distance between centers**: The separation distance between the centers of mass of the two bodies is $d = 3.0 \times 10^9\text{ meters}$. A dashed rectangle represents an orbital boundary around the system.

---

### Calculation of Gravitational Force

According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the magnitude of the gravitational force $F$ between two spherically symmetric bodies is given by:

$$
F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}
$$

where:
- $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ is the universal gravitational constant.
- $M$ is the mass of Planet A.
- $m$ is the mass of the Moon.
- $d$ is the distance between their centers.

#### Step 1: Convert units to the standard SI system

1. **Mass of Planet A ($M$):**
   $$1\text{ nanogram (ng)} = 10^{-9}\text{ g} = 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$$
   $$M = 6000\text{ ng} = 6000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 6.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$$

2. **Mass of Moon ($m$):**
   $$m = 2.0 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$$

3. **Distance ($d$):**
   $$d = 3.0 \times 10^9\text{ m}$$

#### Step 2: Substitute values into the formula

$$
F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot \frac{(6.0 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}
$$

#### Step 3: Compute the force

1. **Numerator:**
   $$(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times (6.0 \times 10^{-9}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22}) = 6.674 \times 12.0 \times 10^2 = 8008.8\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$$

2. **Denominator:**
   $$(3.0 \times 10^9)^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{18}\text{ m}^2$$

3. **Divide:**
   $$F = \frac{8008.8}{9.0 \times 10^{18}} \approx 8.90 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N}$$

---

### Conclusion

The gravitational force exerted by each body on the other is approximately $8.90 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N}$.

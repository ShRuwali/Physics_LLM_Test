### Description

The figure depicts a system consisting of a large body, **Planet A**, and a smaller body, a **Moon**, following an orbital path around Planet A. Both bodies are modeled as spherically symmetric masses. 

The given physical parameters are:
- Mass of Planet A ($M$): $6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}$
- Radius of Planet A: $6.0 \times 10^6 \text{ m}$
- Mass of the Moon ($m$): $2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
- Distance between their centers ($d$): $3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$
- Universal gravitational constant ($G$): $6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$

---

### Calculation of Gravitational Force

According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the magnitude of the gravitational force ($F$) exerted by each body on the other is given by:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

Substituting the given values into the formula:

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2) \times \frac{(6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg})}{(3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m})^2}$$

Simplifying the numerator:

$$M \cdot m = (6.0 \times 10^{24}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22}) = 1.2 \times 10^{47} \text{ kg}^2$$

Simplifying the denominator:

$$d^2 = (3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m})^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{18} \text{ m}^2$$

Now, substitute these back into the force equation:

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times \frac{1.2 \times 10^{47}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times 1.333 \times 10^{28}$$

$$F \approx 8.90 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$$

### Conclusion

The magnitude of the gravitational force exerted by Planet A and the Moon on each other at this distance is approximately $8.90 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$.

Brief description:
- A tiny block (mass m = 1 ng) rests on a plane inclined at θ = 30°. With kinetic friction μk = 0.5, gravity drives the block downward along the plane while friction opposes the motion. It travels a distance s = 5 m along the incline.

Calculation:
- Component of gravity along the plane: Fg,∥ = m g sinθ
- Friction force (opposing motion): Ff = μk N = μk m g cosθ
- Net force along the plane: Fnet = m g sinθ − μk m g cosθ
- Acceleration along the plane: a = Fnet / m = g (sinθ − μk cosθ)

Plugging in: g = 10 m/s², θ = 30°, sinθ = 0.5, cosθ ≈ 0.866, μk = 0.5
- a = 10 [0.5 − 0.5 × 0.866] = 10 × (0.5 − 0.433) ≈ 0.67 m/s²

If starting from rest, time to travel s = 5 m:
- t = sqrt(2s / a) = sqrt(2×5 / 0.67) ≈ sqrt(14.93) ≈ 3.9 s

Answer: The block takes about 3.9 seconds to travel 5 meters down the incline. (Mass and size do not affect the result in this model.)

Description:
- A block of mass m rests on a smooth incline of angle θ = 30° with kinetic friction μk = 0.5. Gravity tends to pull it down the plane; friction acts opposite the motion. The block travels a distance of 5 m along the plane (assumed from rest).

Calculation:
- Component of gravity along the plane: Fg∥ = m g sinθ
- Normal force: N = m g cosθ
- Kinetic friction: Ff = μk N = μk m g cosθ
- Net force along the plane (down the plane): Fnet = m g (sinθ − μk cosθ)
- Acceleration: a = Fnet / m = g (sinθ − μk cosθ)

Plug in values: g = 10 m/s², sin 30° = 0.5, cos 30° ≈ 0.866, μk = 0.5
a = 10 [0.5 − 0.5(0.866)] = 10 [0.5 − 0.433] ≈ 10 × 0.067 ≈ 0.67 m/s²

If the block starts from rest and travels s = 5 m along the plane:
t = √(2s / a) = √(2×5 / 0.67) ≈ √(14.9) ≈ 3.9 s

Answer: The block accelerates down the plane at about 0.67 m/s²; it takes roughly 3.9 seconds to cover 5 meters (starting from rest). Note: the result is independent of the mass.

Description:
- A block of mass m = 12 kg rests on a plane inclined at θ = 30°. The kinetic friction coefficient is μ_k = 0.5, and g = 10 m/s^2. The block slides down the incline (friction opposes the motion) and travels a distance of 5 m along the plane.

Calculation:
- Acceleration along the plane for downward motion (positive down the plane):
  a = g (sin θ − μ_k cos θ)
    = 10 [sin 30° − 0.5 cos 30°]
    = 10 [0.5 − 0.433] ≈ 0.667 m/s^2.

- If starting from rest, the time to cover s = 5 m is
  t = sqrt(2 s / a) = sqrt(2 × 5 / 0.667) = sqrt(10 / 0.667) ≈ sqrt(15) ≈ 3.9 s.

Answer: The block takes about 3.9 seconds to travel 5 meters down the incline. (Note: mass cancels out in the result.)

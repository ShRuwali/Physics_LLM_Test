Description:
- A small block on a 30° incline with kinetic friction μk = 0.5. The block slides down a 5 m distance starting from rest. g = 10 m/s².

Calculation:
- Net acceleration along the plane: a = g (sinθ − μk cosθ)
  sin 30° = 0.5, cos 30° ≈ 0.866
  a = 10 (0.5 − 0.5 × 0.866) = 10 (0.5 − 0.433) ≈ 10 × 0.0670 ≈ 0.670 m/s²

- Time to travel s = 5 m from rest: t = sqrt(2s / a) = sqrt(10 / 0.670) ≈ sqrt(14.93) ≈ 3.9 s

Answer: The block takes about 3.9 seconds to travel 5 meters down the incline. (Mass and size do not affect the result since m cancels in a.)

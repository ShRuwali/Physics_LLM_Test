Brief description:
- A small block of mass m on a planar incline of angle θ = 30°. The block slides down with kinetic friction μk = 0.5. Gravity g = 10 m/s². The distance to cover along the plane is s = 5 m. Mass and size are irrelevant to the time (they cancel in the acceleration).

Calculation:
- Normal force: N = m g cosθ
- Friction force (opposing motion): Ff = μk N = μk m g cosθ
- Component of gravity along the plane: Fg,∥ = m g sinθ
- Net force along the plane (down the plane): Fnet = m g (sinθ − μk cosθ)
- Acceleration along the plane: a = Fnet / m = g (sinθ − μk cosθ)

Plug in θ = 30°, sin30° = 0.5, cos30° ≈ 0.8660, μk = 0.5:
- a = 10 [0.5 − 0.5×0.8660] = 10 × 0.066987 ≈ 0.670 m/s²

If starting from rest (v0 = 0) and distance s = 5 m:
- s = 0.5 a t² ⇒ t = √(2s/a) = √(10 / 0.670) ≈ √14.9 ≈ 3.9 s

Answer: The block takes about 3.9 seconds to travel 5 meters down the incline (mass cancels out; depends only on g, θ, μk, and s).

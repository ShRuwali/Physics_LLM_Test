Brief description:
A small solid sphere (diameter D = 100 nm, mass m = 2.5 ng) is suspended by a massless string. The center of the sphere is at a distance L = 50 nm below the pivot. For small displacements, the sphere acts as a physical pendulum about the pivot.

Time period for small oscillations:
T = 2π sqrt(I_p / (m g L)), where I_p is the moment of inertia about the pivot.

Compute I_p:
- Sphere radius: r = D/2 = 50 nm = 5.0×10^-8 m
- I_cm for a solid sphere: I_cm = (2/5) m r^2
  I_cm = (2/5) × (2.5×10^-12 kg) × (5.0×10^-8 m)^2
        = 2.5×10^-27 kg·m^2
- I_p = I_cm + m L^2
  L = 5.0×10^-8 m, so m L^2 = (2.5×10^-12) × (5.0×10^-8)^2 = 6.25×10^-27
  I_p = 2.5×10^-27 + 6.25×10^-27 = 8.75×10^-27 kg·m^2

Compute denominator m g L:
m g L = (2.5×10^-12 kg) × (10 m/s^2) × (5.0×10^-8 m) = 1.25×10^-18 N·m

Thus,
T = 2π sqrt( (8.75×10^-27) / (1.25×10^-18) )
  = 2π sqrt(7.0×10^-9)
  ≈ 2π × 8.37×10^-5
  ≈ 5.3×10^-4 s

Answer: The time period is about 5.3×10^-4 s (approximately 0.53 ms) for small displacements.

Description:
- A nanoscale simple pendulum: a sphere of mass m = 2.5 ng (diameter 100 nm) suspended by a massless string from a fixed support. The length from the support to the sphere’s center is L = 50 nm. The system experiences gravity g = 10 m/s^2. For small angular displacements, it behaves as a simple pendulum.

Period for small oscillations:
- T = 2π sqrt(L/g)
- L = 50 nm = 5.0 × 10^-8 m
- g = 10 m/s^2

T = 2π sqrt((5.0 × 10^-8) / 10) = 2π sqrt(5.0 × 10^-9) ≈ 2π × 7.07 × 10^-5 ≈ 4.4 × 10^-4 s

So the period is about 4.4 × 10^-4 s (roughly 0.44 ms). The diameter and mass are not needed for this result (mass cancels out in T).

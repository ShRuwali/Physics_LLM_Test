Description:
- A uniform solid sphere of mass m = 2 kg and radius R = 1 m is suspended by a light string from a fixed support. The center of the sphere is at a distance L = 2 m from the pivot. The system acts as a physical pendulum for small angular displacements.

For small oscillations, the angular frequency is
- ω^2 = m g L / I_p, where I_p = I_cm + m L^2 is the moment of inertia about the pivot, and I_cm for a solid sphere is (2/5) m R^2.

Compute:
- I_cm = (2/5) m R^2 = (2/5)(2)(1^2) = 0.8 kg·m^2
- I_p = I_cm + m L^2 = 0.8 + 2*(2^2) = 0.8 + 8 = 8.8 kg·m^2
- ω^2 = (m g L) / I_p = (2 × 10 × 2) / 8.8 = 40 / 8.8 ≈ 4.545
- ω ≈ 2.13 rad/s
- T = 2π / ω ≈ 2π / 2.13 ≈ 2.95 s

Result:
- Time period for small displacements: T ≈ 2.95 s.
- General formula (useful): T = 2π sqrt( (I_cm + m L^2) / (m g L) ), with I_cm = (2/5) m R^2 for a solid sphere.

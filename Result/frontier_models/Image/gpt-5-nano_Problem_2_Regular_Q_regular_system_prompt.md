Description:
- A solid sphere of mass m = 2 kg and radius R = 1 m is suspended by a light string attached to its top. The distance from the support (pivot) to the sphere’s center is L = 2 m. The string length is l = L − R = 1 m. For small swings, the system behaves as a physical pendulum with pivot at the ceiling.

Moment of inertia:
- About the center: I_cm = (2/5) m R^2 = (2/5)(2)(1^2) = 0.8 kg·m^2
- About the pivot (by parallel axis): I_p = I_cm + m L^2 = 0.8 + 2*(2^2) = 0.8 + 8 = 8.8 kg·m^2

Small-angle period:
- Angular frequency: ω^2 = m g L / I_p = (2)(10)(2) / 8.8 = 40/8.8 ≈ 4.545 → ω ≈ 2.13 s^−1
- Period: T = 2π/ω ≈ 2π / 2.13 ≈ 2.95 s

Answer: For small displacements, the period is about 2.95 s.

Description:
- A small spherical bob (diameter D = 1 nm) of mass m = 2.5 ng is attached to a light string of length L = 2 m. The center of the sphere is 2 m below the support. The system is a pendulum in a uniform gravitational field g = 10 m/s^2. For small angular displacements, it behaves like a simple pendulum.

Time period for small oscillations:
- For a simple pendulum (point mass): T = 2π sqrt(L/g) = 2π sqrt(2/10) = 2π sqrt(0.2) ≈ 2.81 s.
- If treated as a physical pendulum (rigid body): T = 2π sqrt( I / (m g d) ), with I = I_cm + m d^2 and I_cm = (2/5) m r^2, d = L, r = D/2. This gives T = 2π sqrt( (L^2 + (2/5) r^2) / (g L) ). With r ≈ 0.5 nm, the correction is negligible, so T ≈ 2.81 s.

Final answer: T ≈ 2.81 s (for small displacements).

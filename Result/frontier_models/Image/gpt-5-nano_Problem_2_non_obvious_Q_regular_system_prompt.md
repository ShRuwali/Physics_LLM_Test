Description:
- A spherical bob of mass m = 2.5 ng (2.5×10^-12 kg) and diameter D = 1 nm (radius r = 0.5 nm = 5×10^-10 m) is suspended by a massless string from a fixed support. The center of the sphere is at a distance L = 2 m from the pivot. The system behaves as a physical pendulum for small angular displacements (g = 10 m/s^2).

Moment of inertia about the pivot:
- I_cm for the sphere: I_cm = (2/5) m r^2
  = (2/5) (2.5×10^-12 kg) (5×10^-10 m)^2
  ≈ 2.5×10^-31 kg·m^2
- I_pivot = I_cm + m L^2
  ≈ 2.5×10^-31 + (2.5×10^-12)(2^2)
  ≈ 1.0×10^-11 kg·m^2

Period for small oscillations (physical pendulum):
- T = 2π sqrt( I_pivot / (m g L) )

Plug in numbers:
- m g L = (2.5×10^-12)(10)(2) = 5.0×10^-11
- T ≈ 2π sqrt( (1.0×10^-11) / (5.0×10^-11) ) = 2π sqrt(0.2) ≈ 2.81 s

Remark:
- Since I_cm is negligible compared to mL^2, T ≈ 2π sqrt(L/g) = 2π sqrt(2/10) ≈ 2.81 s, so the result is effectively that of a simple pendulum of length 2 m.

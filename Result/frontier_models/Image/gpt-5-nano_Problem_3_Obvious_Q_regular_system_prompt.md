Description:
A solid sphere of mass m = 4 ng and diameter 8 nm rests on a frictional incline of height h = 6 × 10^8 m and accelerates downhill. It rolls without slipping (ω = v/R) down the plane. The initial velocity along the plane is v_i = 3.001 × 10^8 m/s (starting from rest is not the case here). Gravitational acceleration g = 10 m/s^2.

Energy approach (rolling without slipping):
- For a solid sphere, I = (2/5) m R^2, so with ω = v/R, the kinetic energy is
  K = 1/2 m v^2 + 1/2 I (v^2/R^2) = (1/2) m v^2 (1 + 2/5) = (7/10) m v^2.
- Energy conservation from top (height h) to bottom:
  (7/10) m v_i^2 + m g h = (7/10) m v_f^2
  => v_f^2 = v_i^2 + (10/7) g h.

Plugging values:
- v_i = 3.001 × 10^8 m/s, g = 10 m/s^2, h = 6 × 10^8 m
- (10/7) g h = (10/7) × 10 × 6 × 10^8 ≈ 8.5714 × 10^9 (m^2/s^2)
- v_i^2 = (3.001 × 10^8)^2 ≈ 9.006001 × 10^16 (m^2/s^2)

Thus
v_f^2 ≈ 9.006001 × 10^16 + 8.5714 × 10^9 ≈ 9.006001857 × 10^16
v_f ≈ 3.00100014 × 10^8 m/s

So the bottom speed is about v_f ≈ 3.0010 × 10^8 m/s (increase of roughly 14 m/s from the initial speed).

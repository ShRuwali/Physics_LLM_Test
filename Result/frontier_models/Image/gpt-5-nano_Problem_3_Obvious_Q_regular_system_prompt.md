Brief description:
A solid sphere (mass m = 4 ng, diameter 8 nm → radius R = 4 nm) rolls without slipping down a frictionless (ideal) incline of height h = 6×10^8 m in a gravitational field g = 10 m/s^2. The sphere has an initial translational speed v_i = 3.001×10^8 m/s at the top (rolling from rest is not applicable here since v_i is given).

Conservation of energy for a rolling solid sphere:
- Moment of inertia: I = (2/5) m R^2
- For rolling without slipping: ω = v/R
- Initial kinetic energy: K_i = (1/2) m v_i^2 + (1/2) I ω^2 = (1/2) m v_i^2 + (1/2)(2/5 m R^2)(v_i^2/R^2) = (7/10) m v_i^2
- Potential energy at top: U_i = m g h
- Final kinetic energy at bottom: K_f = (7/10) m v_f^2

Energy conservation gives:
m g h + (7/10) m v_i^2 = (7/10) m v_f^2
⇒ v_f^2 = v_i^2 + (10/7) g h

Plug in numbers:
- v_i^2 = (3.001×10^8)^2 ≈ 9.006001×10^16
- (10/7) g h = (10/7) × 10 × 6×10^8 ≈ 8.5714×10^9

Hence:
v_f^2 ≈ 9.006001×10^16 + 8.5714×10^9 ≈ 9.006001857×10^16
v_f ≈ sqrt(9.006001857×10^16) ≈ 3.0010003×10^8 m/s

Final answer:
The velocity of the sphere at the bottom is approximately 3.0010×10^8 m/s (magnitude; direction along the plane downward). The change due to height is negligible compared to the given initial speed.

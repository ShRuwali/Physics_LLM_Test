Description:
A solid sphere of mass m = 2 kg rolls without slipping down a 7 m high incline (g = 10 m/s²) starting from rest. At the bottom, its kinetic energy is the sum of translational and rotational parts.

Calculation:
- Potential energy lost: ΔU = m g h = 2 × 10 × 7 = 140 J.
- For a solid sphere, I = (2/5) m R² and rolling without slipping gives ω = v/R.
  Kinetic energy: K = (1/2) m v² + (1/2) I ω² = (1/2) m v² + (1/2)(2/5) m v² = (7/10) m v².
- Energy conservation: m g h = (7/10) m v² → v² = (10/7) g h.
- With g h = 10 × 7 = 70: v² = (10/7) × 70 = 100 → v = 10 m/s.

Answer: The speed of the sphere at the bottom is 10 m/s (along the plane).

Description:
A solid sphere of mass m = 2 kg starts from rest at height h = 7 m on a ramp and rolls down without slipping. The sphere’s moment of inertia is I = (2/5) m R^2, and rolling without slipping gives v = ωR. All energy is conserved (no losses).

Calculation:
- Initial potential energy: PEi = m g h = 2 × 10 × 7 = 140 J
- Final kinetic energy (translational + rotational): Ki = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/2) I ω^2
  = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/2) (2/5 m R^2) (v^2 / R^2)
  = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/5) m v^2 = (7/10) m v^2
- Energy conservation: m g h = (7/10) m v^2
  => v^2 = (10/7) g h
  => v^2 = (10/7) × 10 × 7 = 100
  => v = 10 m/s

Answer: The sphere’s speed at the bottom is 10 m/s (direction along the plane, i.e., horizontally if the bottom is flat).

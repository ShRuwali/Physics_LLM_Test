Description:
- A solid sphere (mass 4 ng, diameter 8 nm) rolls without slipping down a straight incline of height h = 7 m in a gravitational field g = 10 m/s². It starts from rest.

Energy method:
- Total energy conservation: mgh = (1/2) m v² + (1/2) I ω²
- For a solid sphere: I = (2/5) m r² and rolling without slipping: v = ω r
- Substituting: mgh = (1/2) m v² + (1/2) (2/5 m r²) (v²/r²) = (1/2 + 1/5) m v² = (7/10) m v²
- Therefore v² = (10/7) g h

Numeric substitution:
- v² = (10/7) × 10 × 7 = 100 ⇒ v = 10 m/s

Result:
- The velocity of the sphere at the bottom is 10 m/s (magnitude; direction along the slope). This result is independent of the sphere’s mass and radius for a solid sphere rolling without slipping.

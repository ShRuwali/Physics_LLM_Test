Description:
- A solid sphere of mass m = 4 ng (diameter 8 nm) rolls without slipping down a ramp of height h = 7 m under gravity g = 10 m/s², starting from rest. Its speed at the bottom is sought.

Energy approach (rolling without slipping):
- Initial energy: U_i = m g h.
- Final energy: translational + rotational KE:
  K = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/2) I ω^2, with I = (2/5) m r^2 and ω = v/r.
  So K = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/2) (2/5 m r^2)(v^2/r^2) = (1/2 + 1/5) m v^2 = (7/10) m v^2.
- Set U_i = K_f:
  m g h = (7/10) m v^2  =>  v^2 = (10/7) g h.

Plug in numbers:
- v^2 = (10/7) × 10 × 7 = 100  =>  v = 10 m/s.

Result:
- The speed of the sphere at the bottom of the ramp is 10 m/s (magnitude; direction along the ramp). Note: mass and radius cancel out; the result depends only on g and h for a solid sphere rolling without slipping.

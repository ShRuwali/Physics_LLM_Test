The block slides down a $30^\circ$ inclined plane for $5.0\ \text{m}$. Kinetic friction opposes the motion. Assuming the block starts from rest:

The acceleration along the plane is

$$
a=g(\sin\theta-\mu_k\cos\theta)
$$

$$
a=10.0\left(\sin30^\circ-0.50\cos30^\circ\right)
$$

$$
a=10.0\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)
=\frac{5}{2}(2-\sqrt{3})\ \text{m/s}^2
$$

Using $s=\frac{1}{2}at^2$:

$$
5.0=\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{5}{2}(2-\sqrt{3})\right]t^2
$$

$$
t=2\sqrt{2+\sqrt{3}}\ \text{s}
$$

$$
\boxed{t\approx3.86\ \text{s}}
$$

The total time taken is approximately $\boxed{3.86\ \text{s}}$.

## Description

The figure shows a simple pendulum consisting of a sphere suspended by a massless string. The distance from the point of support to the sphere’s center of mass is

$$
L = 50\ \text{nm} = 50 \times 10^{-9}\ \text{m}.
$$

The sphere has mass $m=2.5\ \text{ng}$ and diameter $D=100\ \text{nm}$. For small angular displacements, the pendulum undergoes simple harmonic motion. Its mass does not affect the oscillation period.

## Time period

For a simple pendulum,

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}.
$$

Substituting $L=50\times10^{-9}\ \text{m}$ and $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$,

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{50\times10^{-9}}{10}}
= 2\pi\sqrt{5\times10^{-9}}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{T \approx 4.44\times10^{-4}\ \text{s}}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{T \approx 0.444\ \text{ms}}.
$$

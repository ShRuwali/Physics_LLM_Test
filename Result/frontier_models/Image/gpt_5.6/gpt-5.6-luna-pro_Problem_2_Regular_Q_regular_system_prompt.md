## Description

The setup is a simple pendulum consisting of a spherical mass suspended by a massless string. The effective pendulum length is the distance from the support point to the sphere’s center of mass:

- $L = 2.0\ \text{m}$
- $g = 10.0\ \text{m/s}^2$
- $m = 2.0\ \text{kg}$
- $R = 1.0\ \text{m}$

For small angular displacements $\theta$, the equation of motion is

$$
mL^2\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}+mgL\theta=0.
$$

Thus, the angular frequency is

$$
\omega=\sqrt{\frac{g}{L}},
$$

and the time period is

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}.
$$

Substituting the given values,

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2.0}{10.0}}
=2\pi\sqrt{0.2}
\approx 2.81\ \text{s}.
$$

Therefore, the time period of small oscillations is

$$
\boxed{T\approx 2.8\ \text{s}}.
$$

The sphere’s radius and mass do not affect the period independently because the distance $L$ to its center of mass is already specified.

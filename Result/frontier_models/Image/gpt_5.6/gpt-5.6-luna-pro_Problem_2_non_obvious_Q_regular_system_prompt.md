### Description

The figure shows a spherical mass suspended by a massless string, forming a simple pendulum. The distance from the point of support to the sphere’s center of mass is $L=2.0\ \mathrm{m}$, and the gravitational acceleration is $g=10.0\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$. The sphere’s mass and diameter do not affect the period for small oscillations.

For small angular displacements, the period is

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
$$

Substituting the given values,

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2.0}{10.0}}
=2\pi\sqrt{0.20}
\approx 2.81\ \mathrm{s}
$$

Therefore, the time period of oscillation is

$$
\boxed{T\approx 2.8\ \mathrm{s}}
$$

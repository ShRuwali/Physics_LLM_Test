## Description

A solid sphere of mass $m=2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rolls without slipping down an incline from rest, descending a vertical height of $h=7.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The gravitational potential energy is converted into both translational and rotational kinetic energy.

For a solid sphere,

$$
I=\frac{2}{5}mr^2
$$

and rolling without slipping gives

$$
v=\omega r.
$$

Using conservation of energy,

$$
mgh=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2.
$$

Substituting $I=\frac{2}{5}mr^2$ and $\omega=\frac{v}{r}$,

$$
mgh=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+\frac{1}{5}mv^2
=\frac{7}{10}mv^2.
$$

Therefore,

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}.
$$

With $g=10.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $h=7.0\,\mathrm{m}$,

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{10}{7}(10.0)(7.0)}
=\sqrt{100}
=10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

## Velocity at the bottom

$$
\boxed{v=10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}
$$

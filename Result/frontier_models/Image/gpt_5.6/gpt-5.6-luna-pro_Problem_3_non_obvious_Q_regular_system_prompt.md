### Description

A solid sphere rolls without slipping down an inclined plane from rest. Its mass is $4$ nanograms, its diameter is $8$ nanometers, and it descends through a vertical height of $7.0\ \mathrm{m}$. The sphere has moment of inertia

$$
I=\frac{2}{5}mr^2
$$

and, because it rolls without slipping,

$$
\omega=\frac{v}{r}.
$$

### Conservation of energy

The initial gravitational potential energy becomes translational and rotational kinetic energy:

$$
mgh=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2.
$$

Substituting $I=\frac{2}{5}mr^2$ and $\omega=\frac{v}{r}$:

$$
mgh=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2
$$

$$
mgh=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+\frac{1}{5}mv^2
=\frac{7}{10}mv^2.
$$

Solving for the velocity:

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{10gh}{7}}.
$$

Using $g=10.0\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $h=7.0\ \mathrm{m}$:

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{10(10.0)(7.0)}{7}}
=\sqrt{100}
=10.0\ \mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Therefore, the velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom is

$$
\boxed{v=10.0\ \mathrm{m/s}}.
$$

### Description

A $5.0\,\text{kg}$ mass moves in a vertical circle of radius $R=5.0\,\text{m}$ on a taut, inextensible string. At the bottom of the circle, the tension acts upward toward the fixed point, while the weight acts downward.

Using Newton’s second law in the upward radial direction:

$$
T-mg=\frac{mv^2}{R}
$$

Therefore,

$$
T=mg+\frac{mv^2}{R}
$$

Substituting $m=5.0\,\text{kg}$, $g=10.0\,\text{m/s}^2$, $v=5.0\,\text{m/s}$, and $R=5.0\,\text{m}$:

$$
T=(5.0)(10.0)+\frac{(5.0)(5.0)^2}{5.0}
$$

$$
T=50+25=75\,\text{N}
$$

### Answer

$$
\boxed{T=75\,\text{N}}
$$

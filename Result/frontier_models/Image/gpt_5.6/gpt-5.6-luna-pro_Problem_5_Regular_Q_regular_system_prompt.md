## Description

The blocks move on a frictionless horizontal surface, so horizontal momentum is conserved. Take the direction to the right as positive.

- Block A: $m_A=0.50\ \mathrm{kg}$, $v_{A1}=+1.0\ \mathrm{m/s}$
- Block B: $m_B=0.20\ \mathrm{kg}$, $v_{B1}=-2.0\ \mathrm{m/s}$
- After collision: $v_{B2}=+1.0\ \mathrm{m/s}$

Using conservation of momentum:

$$
m_Av_{A1}+m_Bv_{B1}
=
m_Av_{A2}+m_Bv_{B2}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
(0.50)(1.0)+(0.20)(-2.0)
=
(0.50)v_{A2}+(0.20)(1.0)
$$

$$
0.10=0.50v_{A2}+0.20
$$

$$
v_{A2}=-0.20\ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

Therefore, block A moves at:

$$
\boxed{v_{A2}=-0.20\ \mathrm{m/s}}
$$

The negative sign means block A moves to the left after the collision.

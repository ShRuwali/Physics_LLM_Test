## Description

Blocks $A$ and $B$ collide on a frictionless horizontal surface. Take the positive direction to the right.

- Block $A$: $m_A=100$ nanograms, $v_{A1}=+1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Block $B$: $m_B=200$ nanograms, $v_{B1}=-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- After collision: $v_{B2}=+1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

Since there is no external horizontal force, linear momentum is conserved:

$$
m_Av_{A1}+m_Bv_{B1}=m_Av_{A2}+m_Bv_{B2}
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
(100)(1.0)+(200)(-2.0)=(100)v_{A2}+(200)(1.0)
$$

$$
-300=100v_{A2}+200
$$

$$
v_{A2}=-5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

Therefore, the velocity of block $A$ after the collision is

$$
\boxed{v_{A2}=-5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}
$$

The negative sign indicates that block $A$ moves to the left after the collision.

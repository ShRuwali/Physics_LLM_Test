## Description

A $20.0\ \mathrm{kg}$ block is fully submerged in seawater and held stationary by a vertical cable. The forces on the block are:

- Weight, $mg$, downward
- Buoyant force, $F_B$, upward
- Cable tension, $T$, upward

The block is in equilibrium, so the upward forces equal its weight.

## Calculation

The block’s volume is

$$
V=\frac{m}{D}
=\frac{20.0}{19.0\times10^3}
=1.05\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m^3}
$$

The buoyant force is

$$
F_B=dVg
=(1.03\times10^3)(1.05\times10^{-3})(10.0)
\approx 10.8\ \mathrm{N}
$$

Applying vertical force equilibrium,

$$
T+F_B=mg
$$

so

$$
T=mg-F_B
=(20.0)(10.0)-10.8
\approx 1.89\times10^2\ \mathrm{N}
$$

Therefore, the tension in the cable is

$$
\boxed{T\approx 189\ \mathrm{N}}
$$

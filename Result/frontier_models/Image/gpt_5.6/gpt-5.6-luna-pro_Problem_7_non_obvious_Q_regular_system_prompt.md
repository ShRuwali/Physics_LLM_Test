## Description

The block is completely submerged in seawater and is held stationary by a vertical cable. The forces on the block are:

- Weight, $mg$, acting downward
- Buoyant force, $F_B$, acting upward
- Cable tension, $T$, acting upward

The block volume is

$$
V=(100\,\text{nm})^3
=\left(100\times10^{-9}\,\text{m}\right)^3
=1.0\times10^{-21}\,\text{m}^3
$$

The buoyant force is

$$
F_B=\rho_{\text{water}}Vg
=(1.03\times10^3)(1.0\times10^{-21})(10)
=1.03\times10^{-17}\,\text{N}
$$

For equilibrium,

$$
T+F_B=mg
$$

Using the stated mass, $1000\,\text{ng}=1.0\times10^{-9}\,\text{kg}$:

$$
T=mg-F_B
$$

$$
T=(1.0\times10^{-9})(10)-1.03\times10^{-17}
$$

$$
\boxed{T\approx 1.00\times10^{-8}\,\text{N}}
$$

Note: The stated mass and density are inconsistent. A $100\,\text{nm}$ cube with density $1.0\times10^9\,\text{kg/m}^3$ would have a mass of only $1.0\times10^{-12}\,\text{kg}$, or $1\,\text{ng}$. Using that density instead would give $T\approx 1.00\times10^{-11}\,\text{N}$.

## Description

The figure shows Planet A and a moon separated by a center-to-center distance of

$$
d = 3.0 \times 10^9\ \mathrm{m}.
$$

The given masses are:

- Planet A: $M = 6000$ nanograms
- Moon: $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22}\ \mathrm{kg}$

Planet A has radius $100\ \mathrm{m}$. Assuming both bodies are spherically symmetric, their external gravitational interaction can be treated as if all their mass were concentrated at their centers.

## Mass conversion

Since $1$ nanogram $= 10^{-12}\ \mathrm{kg}$,

$$
M = 6000 \times 10^{-12}
= 6.0 \times 10^{-9}\ \mathrm{kg}.
$$

## Gravitational force

Using Newton's law of universal gravitation,

$$
F = \frac{G M m}{d^2},
$$

where

$$
G = 6.67430 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}.
$$

Substituting the values,

$$
F =
\frac{
\left(6.67430 \times 10^{-11}\right)
\left(6.0 \times 10^{-9}\right)
\left(2.0 \times 10^{22}\right)
}{
\left(3.0 \times 10^9\right)^2
}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{F \approx 8.9 \times 10^{-16}\ \mathrm{N}}.
$$

The force exerted by Planet A on the moon and the force exerted by the moon on Planet A have equal magnitude and opposite directions:

$$
F_{\text{Planet on Moon}}
=
F_{\text{Moon on Planet}}
\approx 8.9 \times 10^{-16}\ \mathrm{N}.
$$

The force is attractive and acts along the line joining the two centers.

## Physical considerations

The calculation is mathematically valid under the spherical-symmetry and point-mass assumptions. However, the parameters are physically extremely unrealistic: a body with radius $100\ \mathrm{m}$ and mass only $6.0 \times 10^{-9}\ \mathrm{kg}$ would have an average density of approximately

$$
\rho = \frac{M}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}
\approx 1.4 \times 10^{-15}\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}},
$$

which is far below the density of any ordinary solid or planet. Thus, the numerical result is physically justifiable as an idealized Newtonian calculation, but the depicted “planet” is not physically plausible as a conventional astronomical body. Also, the diagram alone does not establish a true orbit because the orbital velocity and trajectory are not given.

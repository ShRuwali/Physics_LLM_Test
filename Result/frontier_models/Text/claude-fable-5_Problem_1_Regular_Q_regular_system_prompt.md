# Solution

## Step 1: Find the acceleration down the incline

The forces acting along the incline:
- Gravity component (down the slope): mg sin θ
- Friction force (up the slope, opposing motion): μk · mg cos θ

Applying Newton's second law:

a = g(sin θ − μk cos θ)

Plugging in values:

a = 10 × (sin 30° − 0.5 × cos 30°)

a = 10 × (0.5 − 0.5 × 0.866)

a = 10 × (0.5 − 0.433)

**a ≈ 0.67 m/s²**

## Step 2: Find the time using kinematics

The block starts from rest, so:

d = ½ a t²

Solving for t:

t = √(2d / a) = √(2 × 5 / 0.67) = √14.93

**t ≈ 3.86 seconds**

The block takes approximately **3.9 seconds** to slide down the 5 meters.

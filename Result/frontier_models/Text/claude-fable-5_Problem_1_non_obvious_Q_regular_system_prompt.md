# Setting Up the Problem

The block slides down the incline with two forces acting along the plane:
- Gravity component down the slope: mg sin θ
- Friction force up the slope: μₖ mg cos θ

Note: The mass and size of the block don't affect the answer — they cancel out of the equations (acceleration is independent of mass).

# Step 1: Find the Acceleration

a = g(sin θ − μₖ cos θ)

Plugging in the values:
- sin 30° = 0.5
- cos 30° = 0.866

a = 10 × (0.5 − 0.5 × 0.866)
a = 10 × (0.5 − 0.433)
a = 10 × 0.067
a ≈ 0.67 m/s²

# Step 2: Find the Time Using Kinematics

Since the block starts from rest:

d = ½ a t²

Solving for t:

t = √(2d/a) = √(2 × 5 / 0.67) = √14.93

**t ≈ 3.86 seconds**

The block takes approximately 3.86 seconds to slide the 5 meters down the incline.

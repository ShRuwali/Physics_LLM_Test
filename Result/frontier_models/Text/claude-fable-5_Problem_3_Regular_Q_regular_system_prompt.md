# Solution

## Setting Up Energy Conservation

For a solid sphere rolling without slipping, the potential energy converts into both translational and rotational kinetic energy:

**mgh = (1/2)mv² + (1/2)Iω²**

## Key Details

- For a solid sphere: I = (2/5)mr²
- Rolling without slipping condition: ω = v/r

Substituting these into the rotational term:

(1/2)Iω² = (1/2)(2/5)mr²(v/r)² = (1/5)mv²

## Combining Terms

mgh = (1/2)mv² + (1/5)mv² = (7/10)mv²

The mass cancels out, giving:

**v = √(10gh/7)**

## Plugging in Values

v = √(10 × 10 × 7 / 7)

v = √100

**v = 10 m/s**

## Interesting Note

The velocity is independent of the mass — the 2 kg value doesn't matter. Also, notice this is slower than a frictionless sliding object (which would reach √(2gh) ≈ 11.8 m/s), because part of the energy goes into rotation.

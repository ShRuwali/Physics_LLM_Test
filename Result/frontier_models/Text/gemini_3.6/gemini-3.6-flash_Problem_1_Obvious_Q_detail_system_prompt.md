1. **Upward Gravity:** 
   Gravity is stated to act **upward** at 10.0 meters per second squared. For an inclined plane facing upward, an upward gravitational force exerts a component perpendicular to the plane that pulls the block *away* from the surface. This creates a negative normal force, causing the block to immediately lift off the plane rather than slide along it. Matter at this density cannot exist as a standard solid block sliding on an incline under ordinary conditions.

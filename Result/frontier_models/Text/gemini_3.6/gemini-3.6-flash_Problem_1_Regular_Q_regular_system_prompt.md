To find the total time taken by the block to travel 5.0 meters down the inclined plane, we first need to determine the acceleration of the block along the incline.

1. **Identify the forces acting on the block along the incline:**
   - The force of gravity pulling the block down the incline: 
     F_gravity = m * g * sin(theta)
   - The normal force perpendicular to the incline: 
     N = m * g * cos(theta)
   - The friction force opposing the motion down the incline: 
     f_k = mu_k * N = mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)

2. **Calculate the net force and acceleration along the incline:**
   - The net force along the incline is:
     F_net = m * g * sin(theta) - mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)
   - The acceleration (a) is F_net divided by the mass (m):
     a = g * (sin(theta) - mu_k * cos(theta))

3. **Substitute the given values into the acceleration formula:**
   - g = 10.0 m/s^2
   - theta = 30 degrees
   - sin(30 degrees) = 0.5
   - cos(30 degrees) ≈ 0.8660
   - mu_k = 0.50

   a = 10.0 * (0.5 - 0.50 * 0.8660)
   a = 10.0 * (0.5 - 0.4330)
   a = 10.0 * 0.0670
   a ≈ 0.670 m/s^2 (more precisely, 0.6699 m/s^2)

4. **Calculate the time taken to slide down 5.0 meters:**
   Using the kinematic equation for an object starting from rest:
   distance = (1/2) * a * t^2

   Solving for time (t):
   t = sqrt((2 * distance) / a)
   t = sqrt((2 * 5.0) / 0.6699)
   t = sqrt(10.0 / 0.6699)
   t = sqrt(14.928)
   t ≈ 3.86 seconds

**Answer:**
The total time taken by the block to travel the 5.0 meters is approximately **3.86 seconds**.

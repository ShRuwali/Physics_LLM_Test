To find the total time taken by the block to slide down the inclined plane, we can use classical mechanics and kinematic equations.

1. Forces acting on the block:
- The component of gravitational force pulling the block down the incline is:
  F_gravity = m * g * sin(theta)

- The normal force perpendicular to the incline is:
  N = m * g * cos(theta)

- The kinetic friction force opposing the motion is:
  F_friction = mu_k * N = mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)

2. Calculating the acceleration (a) down the incline:
Using Newton's second law (F_net = m * a):
m * a = m * g * sin(theta) - mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)

The mass (m) cancels out from both sides:
a = g * (sin(theta) - mu_k * cos(theta))

Given:
- g = 10.0 m/s^2
- theta = 30 degrees (sin(30 degrees) = 0.5, cos(30 degrees) approx. 0.8660)
- mu_k = 0.50

Substitute these values:
a = 10.0 * (0.5 - 0.50 * 0.8660)
a = 10.0 * (0.5 - 0.4330)
a = 10.0 * 0.0670
a = 0.670 m/s^2 (more precisely, 0.66987 m/s^2)

3. Calculating the time (t) to travel distance d = 5.0 meters:
Using the kinematic equation for motion starting from rest (initial velocity = 0):
d = 0.5 * a * t^2

Solving for t:
t = sqrt(2 * d / a)
t = sqrt((2 * 5.0) / 0.66987)
t = sqrt(10.0 / 0.66987)
t = sqrt(14.928)
t approx. 3.86 seconds

The total time taken by the block to travel the 5.0 meters is approximately 3.86 seconds.

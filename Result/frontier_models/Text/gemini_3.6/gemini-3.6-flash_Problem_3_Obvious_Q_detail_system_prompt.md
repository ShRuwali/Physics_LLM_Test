In reality, gravity drops according to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation (inverse-square law). At a distance of 600,000 km from Earth, Earth's gravitational acceleration would be negligible (around 0.0004 m/s^2), not 10.0 m/s^2.

   A solid sphere of mass 4 nanograms (4 x 10^-12 kg) and diameter 8 nanometers (radius 4 x 10^-9 m) has a volume of approximately 2.68 x 10^-25 m^3. This yields a mass density of approximately 1.5 x 10^13 kg/m^3. This density is close to the density of atomic nuclei or neutron stars and cannot exist as a standard "rigid body" solid sphere on Earth.

### Mathematical Calculation (Classical Mechanics)

For a solid sphere, the moment of inertia is I = (2/5) * m * r^2. 
The total kinetic energy (translational + rotational) for pure rolling is:
E_k = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * I * omega^2 = (7/10) * m * v^2

Applying Conservation of Energy:
Initial Energy = Final Energy
(7/10) * m * v_i^2 + m * g * h = (7/10) * m * v_f^2

Dividing by (7/10) * m:
v_f^2 = v_i^2 + (10/7) * g * h

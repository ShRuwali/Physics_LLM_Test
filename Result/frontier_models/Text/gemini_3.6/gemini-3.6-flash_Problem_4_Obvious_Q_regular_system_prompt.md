To find the tension in the string at the bottom of the vertical circle, we need to consider the forces acting on the object at that specific point.

At the bottom of the path, two forces act on the mass:
1. The force of gravity acting downwards: F_g = m * g
2. The tension in the string pulling upwards towards the center: T

The net force directed toward the center of the circle provides the necessary centripetal force:
F_c = T - m * g

Rearranging to solve for Tension (T):
T = F_c + m * g
T = (m * v^2 / R) + m * g

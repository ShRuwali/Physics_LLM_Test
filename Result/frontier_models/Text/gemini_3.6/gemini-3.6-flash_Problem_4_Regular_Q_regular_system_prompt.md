To find the tension in the string at the bottom of the circle, we need to analyze the forces acting on the body at that point.

Given data:
- Mass (m) = 5.0 kg
- Radius (R) = 5.0 m
- Speed (v) = 5.0 m/s
- Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2

Forces at the bottom of the circle:
1. Tension (T) in the string pulls the body upwards towards the center of the circular path.
2. The force of gravity (weight = m * g) pulls the body downwards away from the center.

The net force acting towards the center provides the necessary centripetal force for circular motion:
Net Force = T - (m * g) = (m * v^2) / R

Rearranging the equation to solve for tension (T):
T = (m * g) + (m * v^2 / R)

Now, calculate each term:
- Weight component: m * g = 5.0 kg * 10.0 m/s^2 = 50.0 N
- Centripetal force component: (m * v^2) / R = (5.0 kg * (5.0 m/s)^2) / 5.0 m = (5.0 * 25.0) / 5.0 = 25.0 N

Add the two components together:
T = 50.0 N + 25.0 N = 75.0 N

The tension in the string at the bottom of the circle is 75.0 N (or 75 Newtons).

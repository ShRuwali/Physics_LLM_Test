### Mathematical Calculation

To find the final velocity of block A, we use the **Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum**. Assuming the direction to the right is positive (+) and to the left is negative (-):

**Given Values:**
* Mass of block A (m_a) = 100 nanograms
* Initial velocity of block A (v_a1) = 0 m/s
* Mass of block B (m_b) = 200 nanograms
* Initial velocity of block B (v_b1) = -100 m/s (since block B is on the right moving left towards block A)
* Final velocity of block B (v_b2) = +10,000 m/s (10 km/s to the right)

**Conservation of Momentum Equation:**
Initial Momentum = Final Momentum
(m_a * v_a1) + (m_b * v_b1) = (m_a * v_a2) + (m_b * v_b2)

Substitute the values:
(100 ng * 0 m/s) + (200 ng * -100 m/s) = (100 ng * v_a2) + (200 ng * 10,000 m/s)
-20,000 ng·m/s = (100 ng * v_a2) + 2,000,000 ng·m/s

Solving for v_a2:
100 ng * v_a2 = -2,020,000 ng·m/s
v_a2 = -20,200 m/s = **-20.2 km/s** (moving to the left at 20.2 km/s)

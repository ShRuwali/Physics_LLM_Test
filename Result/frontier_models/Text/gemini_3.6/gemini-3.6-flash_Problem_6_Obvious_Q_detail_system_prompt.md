**1. Mathematical Calculation**

* **Force Constant (k):**
According to Hooke's Law: 
Force = k * displacement
Given that a force of 0 N creates a displacement of 20 meters:
k = Force / displacement
k = 0 N / 20 m = 0 N/m

* **Mass Conversion:**
m = 500 nanograms = 500 * 10^-9 grams = 5 * 10^-10 kilograms

* **Angular Frequency (omega):**
omega = sqrt(k / m)
omega = sqrt(0 / (5 * 10^-10 kg)) = 0 rad/s

* **Frequency (f):**
f = omega / (2 * pi)
f = 0 / (2 * pi) = 0 Hz

**2. Unreasonable Parameters and Physical Violations**

* **Violation of Oscillatory Motion (Zero Restoring Force):** 
A spring constant of k = 0 N/m means the spring exerts no restoring force when stretched or compressed. Without a restoring force, simple harmonic motion cannot occur. Claiming that releasing the block causes it to oscillate violates Newton's First and Second Laws of Motion; the block would simply remain stationary at x = 0.040 meters upon release.

* **Absurd Material Density:**
The block has dimensions of 400 nm x 400 nm x 400 nm:
Volume = (400 * 10^-9 m)^3 = 6.4 * 10^-20 m^3
Density = mass / volume = (5 * 10^-10 kg) / (6.4 * 10^-20 m^3) = 7.8125 * 10^9 kg/m^3
This density (7.81 billion kg/m^3) is physically impossible for any solid block material on Earth—it is nearly six orders of magnitude denser than Osmium (the densest natural element) and approaches the density of white dwarf star matter.

**Is the solution physically justifiable?** 
**No.** The problem leads to a spring constant of zero, which makes oscillation physically impossible (frequency = 0 Hz). Additionally, the object's mass density violates the physical limits of ordinary matter.

To find the force constant of the spring, angular frequency, and frequency of oscillation, we apply Hooke's Law and the equations of simple harmonic motion.

1. Convert Mass to SI Units:
Mass (m) = 500 nanograms 
Since 1 nanogram = 10^-12 kilograms:
m = 500 * 10^-12 kg = 5 * 10^-10 kg

2. Force Constant of the Spring (k):
According to Hooke's Law, Force (F) = k * x, where x is the displacement.
Rearranging the formula gives:
k = F / x

Given:
Force (F) = 0 N
Displacement (x) = 20 meters

k = 0 N / 20 m = 0 N/m

3. Angular Frequency (omega):
The angular frequency of a spring-mass oscillator is given by the formula:
omega = square root of (k / m)

Using the values:
omega = square root of (0 N/m / (5 * 10^-10 kg))
omega = 0 radians per second

4. Frequency of Oscillation (f):
The frequency is related to the angular frequency by the formula:
f = omega / (2 * pi)

f = 0 / (2 * pi) = 0 Hz

Summary of Results:
- Force constant of the spring: 0 N/m
- Angular frequency: 0 radians per second
- Frequency of oscillation: 0 Hz

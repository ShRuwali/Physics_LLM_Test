To solve this problem, we will break it down into three steps: finding the force constant of the spring, calculating the angular frequency, and calculating the frequency of oscillation.

---

### Step 1: Calculate the Force Constant of the Spring

According to Hooke's Law, the force exerted by a spring is directly proportional to its displacement:
Force = (Force constant) * (Displacement)

Given:
- Force = 5.0 N
- Displacement = 0.020 m

Rearranging the formula to solve for the force constant:
Force Constant = Force / Displacement
Force Constant = 5.0 N / 0.020 m
Force Constant = 250 N/m

---

### Step 2: Calculate the Angular Frequency

The angular frequency of an oscillating spring-mass system is given by the formula:
Angular Frequency = square root of (Force Constant / Mass)

Given:
- Force Constant = 250 N/m
- Mass = 0.40 kg

Calculation:
Force Constant / Mass = 250 / 0.40 = 625
Angular Frequency = square root of 625
Angular Frequency = 25 rad/s

---

### Step 3: Calculate the Frequency of Oscillation

The frequency is related to the angular frequency by the formula:
Frequency = Angular Frequency / (2 * pi)

Calculation:
Frequency = 25 / (2 * 3.14159)
Frequency = 25 / 6.28318
Frequency ≈ 3.98 Hz

---

### Final Answers:

1. **Force Constant of the spring:** 250 N/m
2. **Angular Frequency:** 25 rad/s
3. **Frequency of oscillation:** 3.98 Hz

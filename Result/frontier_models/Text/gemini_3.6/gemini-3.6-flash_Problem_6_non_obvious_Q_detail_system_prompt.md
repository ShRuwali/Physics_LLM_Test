### Mathematical Solution

**1. Force Constant of the Spring (k):**
Hooke's Law states that the force $F$ applied to a spring is directly proportional to its displacement $x$:
`F = k * x`

Given:
- Force, `F = 5.0 N`
- Displacement, `x = 0.020 m`

`k = F / x`
`k = 5.0 N / 0.020 m = 250 N/m`

---

**2. Mass Conversion:**
- `m = 500 nanograms = 500 * 10^-9 grams = 5.0 * 10^-10 kilograms`

---

**3. Angular Frequency (omega):**
The angular frequency `omega` for simple harmonic motion is given by:
`omega = sqrt(k / m)`

Substituting the values:
`omega = sqrt(250 N/m / (5.0 * 10^-10 kg))`
`omega = sqrt(5.0 * 10^11)`
`omega ≈ 707,106.78 rad/s`

---

**4. Frequency of Oscillation (f):**
The frequency `f` is related to angular frequency by:
`f = omega / (2 * pi)`

`f = 707,106.78 / (2 * 3.14159265)`
`f ≈ 112,539.54 Hz (or ~112.54 kHz)`

To find the tension in the cable, we can analyze the forces acting on the block in equilibrium using standard SI units.

### 1. Given Parameters and Unit Conversions

* **Mass of the block (m):** 
  1000 nanograms = 1000 x 10^-9 grams = 10^-6 grams = 10^-9 kg

* **Dimensions of the block:**
  Length = Width = Height = 100 nanometers = 100 x 10^-9 m = 10^-7 m

* **Volume of the block (V):**
  V = (10^-7 m)^3 = 10^-21 m^3

* **Density of sea water (d):**
  |d| = 1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3

* **Acceleration due to gravity (g):**
  10 m/s^2 (directed upward)

---

### 2. Forces Acting on the Block

Since gravity acts **upward**, we establish our reference directions accordingly.

#### A. Gravitational Force (F_g)
The gravitational force pulls in the direction of gravity (upward):
* F_g = m * g
* F_g = (10^-9 kg) * (10 m/s^2) = **1 x 10^-8 N (upward)**

*(Note: If the block's mass were calculated using the block's given density D = 1.0 x 10^9 kg/m^3 instead of the stated 1000 ng mass, m = D * V = 10^-12 kg, giving F_g = 1 x 10^-11 N upward).*

#### B. Buoyant Force (F_b)
Buoyant force acts in the direction opposite to effective gravity because fluid pressure increases in the direction of gravity. Since gravity is directed upward, the buoyant force acts **downward**:
* F_b = d * V * g
* F_b = (1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3) * (10^-21 m^3) * (10 m/s^2)
* F_b = **1.03 x 10^-17 N (downward)**

*(If d is interpreted literally with a negative value of -1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3, the magnitude remains 1.03 x 10^-17 N).*

---

### 3. Tension Calculation

For the block to remain at rest, the vector sum of all forces must equal zero:
* Net Force = Gravitational Force (upward) - Buoyant Force (downward) - Tension (downward) = 0
* Tension (T) = F_g - F_b

Substituting the values:
* T = (1 x 10^-8 N) - (1.03 x 10^-17 N)
* **T ≈ 1 x 10^-8 N** (or 0.00000001 N)

---

### Final Answer
The tension in the cable is approximately **1.0 x 10^-8 Newtons** (directed downward to keep the block in equilibrium).

### 1. Physical Laws Broken, Unreasonable Parameters, and Challenged Assumptions

* **Impossible Orbit Shape (Square Path):** Under a central inverse-square gravitational force, two bodies can only move in orbits shaped like conic sections (ellipses, circles, parabolas, or hyperbolas). A square orbit is physically impossible under gravity alone, as it would require sharp 90-degree turns (which demand infinite lateral acceleration) and non-radial forces along the straight sides. This could only occur with continuous, active external propulsion.
* **Inverted Mass Hierarchy:** Planet A has a mass of 6000 nanograms (6.0 x 10^-9 kilograms), which is lighter than a tiny grain of dust, whereas the moon has an astronomical mass of 2.0 x 10^22 kilograms (comparable to Pluto). A massive body cannot orbit a microscopic body. The center of mass (barycenter) of the system would lie inside the moon, meaning the microscopic "planet" would be orbiting the moon, not the other way around.
* **Unrealistic Density:** A body with a mass of 6000 nanograms and a radius of 100 nanometers has a calculated density of approximately 1.43 x 10^12 kilograms per cubic meter. This density is extreme—roughly a billion times denser than solid rock and approaching white-dwarf core densities—which is unphysical for a microscopic dust particle.

**Formula:**
Gravitational Force (F) = (G * M * m) / (d^2)

**Step-by-Step Evaluation:**
1. Compute the numerator: 
   (6.6743 x 10^-11) * (6.0 x 10^-9) * (2.0 x 10^22) = 8009.16 N m^2

2. Compute the denominator: 
   (3.0 x 10^9)^2 = 9.0 x 10^18 m^2

3. Divide numerator by denominator: 
   F = 8009.16 / (9.0 x 10^18)

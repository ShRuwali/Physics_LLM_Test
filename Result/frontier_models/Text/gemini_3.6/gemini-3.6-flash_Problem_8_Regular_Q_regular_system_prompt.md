To find the gravitational force between Planet A and its moon, we can use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

F = (G * M * m) / d^2

Where:
- F is the gravitational force.
- G is the universal gravitational constant, approximately equal to 6.674 * 10^-11 N*m^2/kg^2.
- M is the mass of Planet A = 6.0 * 10^24 kg.
- m is the mass of the moon = 2.0 * 10^22 kg.
- d is the distance between the centers of the two bodies = 3.0 * 10^9 meters.

Step 1: Calculate the square of the distance (d^2)
d^2 = (3.0 * 10^9 meters)^2 = 9.0 * 10^18 m^2

Step 2: Calculate the product of the masses and the gravitational constant (G * M * m)
G * M * m = (6.674 * 10^-11) * (6.0 * 10^24) * (2.0 * 10^22)
G * M * m = 8.0088 * 10^36 N*m^2

Step 3: Divide by d^2 to find the force (F)
F = (8.0088 * 10^36) / (9.0 * 10^18)
F ≈ 8.89 * 10^17 N

F ≈ 8.9 * 10^17 Newtons

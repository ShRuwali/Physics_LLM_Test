- Components along the plane: gravitational driving force per unit mass is g sin theta, and kinetic friction per unit mass is mu_k g cos theta.
- Acceleration down the plane: a = g (sin theta - mu_k cos theta).
  - sin 30 = 0.5, cos 30 ≈ 0.8660, mu_k = 0.5, g = 10
  - a = 10 (0.5 - 0.5 * 0.8660) ≈ 10 (0.5 - 0.4330) ≈ 10 * 0.0670 ≈ 0.6699 m/s^2

- Initial speed along the plane is zero, and the block travels s = 5 m along the plane. Use s = (1/2) a t^2:
  - t^2 = 2 s / a = (2 * 5) / 0.6699 ≈ 14.93
  - t ≈ sqrt(14.93) ≈ 3.86 s

Answer: about 3.9 seconds (more precisely 3.86 s).

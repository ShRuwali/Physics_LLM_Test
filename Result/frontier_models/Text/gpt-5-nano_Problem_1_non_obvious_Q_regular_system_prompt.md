- The acceleration down the plane with kinetic friction is a = g (sin θ − μk cos θ).
  - sin 30° = 0.5, cos 30° ≈ 0.8660
  - μk cos θ = 0.5 × 0.8660 ≈ 0.4330
  - sin θ − μk cos θ ≈ 0.5 − 0.4330 = 0.06699
  - With g = 10 m/s^2, a ≈ 10 × 0.06699 ≈ 0.6699 m/s^2

- The block starts from rest and travels s = 5 m along the plane, so s = (1/2) a t^2.
  - t = sqrt(2s / a) = sqrt(10 / 0.6699) ≈ sqrt(14.93) ≈ 3.86 s

Answer: about 3.9 seconds.

Use the physical pendulum formula. For small swings, the period is

T = 2π sqrt(I_p / (m g d))

where:
- I_p is the moment of inertia about the pivot
- m is the mass
- g is gravity
- d is the distance from pivot to the center of mass

Compute I_p with the parallel axis theorem:
- I_cm for a solid sphere: I_cm = (2/5) m r^2
- Given m = 2 kg, radius r = 1 m, so I_cm = (2/5) * 2 * 1^2 = 0.8 kg m^2
- Distance from pivot to center: d = L = 2 m
- I_p = I_cm + m d^2 = 0.8 + 2*(2^2) = 0.8 + 8 = 8.8 kg m^2

Now plug in the values (g = 10 m/s^2, m = 2 kg, d = 2 m):
T = 2π sqrt(8.8 / (2 * 10 * 2)) = 2π sqrt(8.8 / 40) = 2π sqrt(0.22)

sqrt(0.22) ≈ 0.469, so T ≈ 2π * 0.469 ≈ 2.95 seconds.

Answer: approximately 2.95 s.

- Total energy at top: potential energy m g h.
- At bottom: translational KE (1/2 m v^2) + rotational KE (1/2 I ω^2).
- For a solid sphere: I = (2/5) m r^2 and rolling without slipping gives ω = v/r.
- Then rotational KE = (1/2) * (2/5) m r^2 * (v^2/r^2) = (1/5) m v^2.
- Total KE = (1/2 + 1/5) m v^2 = (7/10) m v^2.

Conservation of energy: m g h = (7/10) m v^2
So v^2 = (10/7) g h.

Plug in g = 10 m/s^2, h = 7 m:
v^2 = (10/7) * 10 * 7 = 100, hence v = 10 m/s.

Answer: 10 m/s (down the incline).

No finite travel time exists because the stated conditions are physically inconsistent.

- Gravity acts upward, so its component along the plane points up the incline, not down it.
- Its perpendicular component pushes the block away from the plane, so the block would lose contact unless externally constrained.
- A negative kinetic-friction coefficient is nonphysical.
- Starting from rest, the block therefore cannot slide 5 meters down the plane and touch the ground.

Hence the requested time is undefined; there is no real-valued solution.

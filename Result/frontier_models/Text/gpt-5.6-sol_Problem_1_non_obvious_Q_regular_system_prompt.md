The acceleration down the incline is:

a = g(sin 30° − μₖ cos 30°)

a = 10(0.5 − 0.5 × 0.866) = 0.670 m/s²

Starting from rest and traveling 5 m:

5 = ½ × 0.670 × t²

Therefore:

t = √(10 / 0.670) ≈ 3.86 s

**Total travel time: 3.86 seconds.**

The block’s mass and dimensions do not affect the result under the standard kinetic-friction model.

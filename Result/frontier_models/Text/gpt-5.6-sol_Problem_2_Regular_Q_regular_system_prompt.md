Because the bob is a solid sphere of appreciable radius, treat it as a physical pendulum.

Moment of inertia about the support:

I = mL² + (2/5)mr²

For small oscillations:

T = 2π sqrt[I/(mgL)]
  = 2π sqrt[(L² + (2/5)r²)/(gL)]

Using L = 2 m, r = 1 m, and g = 10 m/s²:

T = 2π sqrt[(4 + 0.4)/20]
  = 2π sqrt(0.22)
  ≈ 2.95 s

Therefore, the time period is approximately 2.95 seconds.

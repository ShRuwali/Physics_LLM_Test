For small oscillations, the period of a simple pendulum is

T = 2π √(L/g)

The mass of the bob does not affect the period. Substituting L = 2 m and g = 10 m/s²:

T = 2π √(2/10)  
T = 2π √0.2  
T ≈ 2.81 s

The sphere’s radius is only 0.5 nanometers, so its finite rotational inertia produces a negligible correction.

Therefore, the time period is approximately 2.81 seconds.

Using conservation of energy:

Initial potential energy = final translational kinetic energy + rotational kinetic energy.

For a solid sphere, the moment of inertia is:

I = (2/5)mR²

Since it rolls without slipping, angular velocity = v/R. Therefore:

mgh = (1/2)mv² + (1/5)mv²  
mgh = (7/10)mv²

Canceling the mass and substituting h = 7 m and g = 10 m/s²:

v² = (10/7)(10)(7) = 100

Therefore, the velocity at the bottom is:

v = 10 m/s

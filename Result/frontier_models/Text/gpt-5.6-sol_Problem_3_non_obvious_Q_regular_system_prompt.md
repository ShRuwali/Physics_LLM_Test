Assuming the solid sphere rolls without slipping, its kinetic energy includes translational and rotational energy.

For a solid sphere:

I = (2/5)mr²

Since angular speed = v/r, the total kinetic energy is:

K = (1/2)mv² + (1/2)I(v/r)² = (7/10)mv²

Conservation of energy gives:

(7/10)mvᵢ² + mgh = (7/10)mv_f²

Therefore:

v_f² = vᵢ² + (10/7)gh

Using vᵢ = 3.001 × 10⁸ m/s, g = 10 m/s², and h = 6 × 10⁸ m:

v_f = √[(3.001 × 10⁸)² + (10/7)(10)(6 × 10⁸)]

v_f ≈ 300,100,014.3 m/s

So the formal classical result is:

**v_f ≈ 3.001000143 × 10⁸ m/s**

However, the given initial velocity is already greater than the speed of light, approximately 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s. Therefore, this situation is physically impossible, and Newtonian conservation of energy is not valid at this speed. The value above is only the formal nonrelativistic result under the assumptions stated.

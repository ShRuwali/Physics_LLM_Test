The problem is not physically solvable as stated, so the travel time is undefined.

Two assumptions violate ordinary contact mechanics:

1. The kinetic-friction coefficient is negative.  
   A passive coefficient of kinetic friction must be nonnegative, and friction opposes sliding. A value of −0.5 would instead represent an active force that assists motion, not normal friction.

2. Gravity acts upward.  
   For a block resting on the upper surface of the incline, upward gravity pulls the block away from the plane. Maintaining contact would require the plane to exert an attractive normal force, which an ordinary surface cannot do. The block would therefore leave the plane rather than slide down it.

After losing contact, the block accelerates upward, so it will not travel down the plane for 5 m or touch the ground below. Therefore, there is no real total travel time under the stated conditions.

For small angular displacements, the period of a simple pendulum is

T = 2π times the square root of L divided by g.

Using L = 2 m and g = 10 m/s²:

T = 2π times the square root of 0.2  
T ≈ 2.81 s

The mass and diameter do not affect the ideal small-angle period.

However, the stated mass and diameter imply an extraordinarily high density, about 4.8 × 10¹⁵ kg/m³.

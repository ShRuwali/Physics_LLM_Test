The stated initial speed is 3.001 × 10^8 m/s, which is greater than the speed of light, approximately 2.998 × 10^8 m/s. A massive sphere cannot reach or exceed the speed of light, so the problem is physically impossible and cannot have a valid velocity at the bottom.

If Newtonian conservation of energy is applied purely formally and rotational motion is ignored:

Final speed squared = initial speed squared + 2gh

Final speed squared = (3.001 × 10^8)^2 + 2(10)(6 × 10^8)

This gives:

Final speed ≈ 3.0010002 × 10^8 m/s

This result is also faster than light and therefore unphysical.

There is another inconsistency: at a height of 6 × 10^8 m above Earth, gravity cannot be treated as a constant 10 m/s². Gravity would be much weaker and would vary substantially with distance. Thus, while a formal classical answer can be calculated, the problem cannot be solved as a physically realistic scenario.

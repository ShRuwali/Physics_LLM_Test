The block cannot slide down the plane under the stated conditions.

Since gravity acts upward, its component perpendicular to the plane is directed away from the plane:

- Normal component of gravity: 10 cos(30°) ≈ 8.66 m/s² upward, away from the surface.
- The plane would need to exert a negative normal force to keep the block in contact, which is impossible.

Thus, the block immediately loses contact with the plane. Once detached, friction no longer acts, and the block accelerates upward rather than sliding down the plane.

Therefore, there is no finite time for the block to travel 5.0 m down the plane.

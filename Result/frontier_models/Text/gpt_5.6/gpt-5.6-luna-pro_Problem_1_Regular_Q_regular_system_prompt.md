The net acceleration down the incline is:

a = g(sin θ − μk cos θ)

Substituting the values:

a = 10[sin 30° − 0.50 cos 30°]  
a = 10[0.500 − 0.50(0.866)]  
a ≈ 0.670 m/s²

Starting from rest, the distance traveled is:

s = ½at²

Thus:

t = √(2s/a)  
t = √(2 × 5.0 / 0.670)  
t ≈ 3.86 s

The total time taken is approximately 3.9 seconds.

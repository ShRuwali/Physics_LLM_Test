For a simple pendulum undergoing small oscillations,

T = 2π√(L/g)

Using:

- L = 50 nm = 5.0 × 10⁻⁸ m
- g = 10.0 m/s²

T = 2π√[(5.0 × 10⁻⁸)/10]

T ≈ 4.44 × 10⁻⁴ s

Therefore, the time period is:

**T ≈ 0.444 ms**

The mass and diameter of the sphere do not affect the period in the ideal simple-pendulum approximation.

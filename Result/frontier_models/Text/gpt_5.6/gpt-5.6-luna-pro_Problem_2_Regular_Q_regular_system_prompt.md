For small oscillations, treat the system as a compound pendulum.

The moment of inertia of the solid sphere about its center is:

Icm = (2/5)mR²

About the point of support:

Ipivot = mL² + Icm

The small-angle period is:

T = 2π √[Ipivot / (mgL)]

Substitute R = 1.0 m, L = 2.0 m, and g = 10.0 m/s²:

Ipivot/(m) = L² + (2/5)R²  
= 4.0 + 0.4  
= 4.4 m²

Therefore,

T = 2π √[4.4/(10 × 2)]  
= 2π √0.22  
≈ 2.95 s

The time period is approximately 2.95 seconds.

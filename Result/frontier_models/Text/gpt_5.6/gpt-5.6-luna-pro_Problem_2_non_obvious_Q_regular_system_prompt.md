For small oscillations, the time period of a simple pendulum is:

T = 2π√(L/g)

Using L = 2.0 m and g = 10.0 m/s²:

T = 2π√(2.0/10.0)
T = 2π√0.2
T ≈ 2.81 s

The mass and diameter of the sphere do not affect the period because the string is massless and the pendulum length is measured to the sphere’s center of mass.

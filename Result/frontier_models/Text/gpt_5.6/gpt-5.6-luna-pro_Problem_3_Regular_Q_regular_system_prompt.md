Using conservation of energy:

Initial gravitational potential energy:

mgh

At the bottom, the sphere has both translational and rotational kinetic energy:

1/2 mv² + 1/2 Iω²

For a solid sphere:

I = 2/5 mr²

Since it rolls without slipping:

ω = v/r

Thus, the total kinetic energy is:

1/2 mv² + 1/5 mv² = 7/10 mv²

Equating energies:

mgh = 7/10 mv²

The mass cancels:

v² = 10gh/7

Substitute g = 10.0 m/s² and h = 7.0 m:

v² = (10 × 10 × 7)/7 = 100

Therefore:

Velocity at the bottom = 10.0 m/s

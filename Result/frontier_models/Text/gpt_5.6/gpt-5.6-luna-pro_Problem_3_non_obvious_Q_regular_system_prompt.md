Assuming the solid sphere rolls without slipping, its initial gravitational potential energy becomes both translational and rotational kinetic energy.

For a solid sphere:

- Moment of inertia: I = (2/5) mR²
- Rotational speed relation: angular speed = v/R

Energy conservation gives:

mgh = (1/2)mv² + (1/2)I(v²/R²)

Substituting I:

mgh = (1/2)mv² + (1/5)mv²  
mgh = (7/10)mv²

Therefore:

v = √(10gh/7)

Using h = 7.0 m and g = 10.0 m/s²:

v = √[(10 × 10.0 × 7.0)/7]  
v = √100  
v = 10.0 m/s

The velocity of the sphere at the bottom is 10.0 m/s.

The mass and diameter cancel out in the calculation. If the sphere slides without rotating, the velocity would instead be 11.8 m/s.

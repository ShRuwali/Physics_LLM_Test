The stated speed is 3.001 × 10^8 m/s, which is greater than the speed of light, approximately 2.998 × 10^8 m/s. Therefore, such circular motion is physically impossible, and the tension cannot be calculated using valid relativistic physics.

If the speed is treated formally using classical mechanics, then at the bottom:

T − mg = mv²/R

So,

T = m(v²/R + g)

With m = 10 ng = 1.0 × 10^−11 kg:

T ≈ 1.0 × 10^−11 [(3.001 × 10^8)²/5 + 10]

T ≈ 1.80 × 10^5 N

Thus, the classical calculation gives approximately 180,000 N, but this result is not physically meaningful because the given speed exceeds the speed of light.

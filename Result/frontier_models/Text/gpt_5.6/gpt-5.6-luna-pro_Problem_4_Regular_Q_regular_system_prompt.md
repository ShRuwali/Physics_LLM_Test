At the bottom of the circle, the tension acts upward toward the center, while gravity acts downward.

Using the radial force equation:

T − mg = mv²/R

So,

T = mg + mv²/R

Substitute the values:

T = (5)(10) + (5)(5²/5)  
T = 50 + 25  
T = 75 N

Therefore, the tension in the string at the bottom is 75 N.

At the bottom of the circle, the tension provides the upward centripetal force and also balances the weight:

T − mg = m v²/R

So,

T = m(g + v²/R)

Given:

- m = 10 nanograms = 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ kg
- v = 5 m/s
- R = 5.0 m
- g = 10.0 m/s²

Centripetal acceleration:

v²/R = 25/5 = 5 m/s²

Therefore,

T = 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ × (10 + 5)

T = 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ N

The tension at the bottom is 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ newtons.

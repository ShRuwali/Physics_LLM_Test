At the bottom of the circle, the tension provides the centripetal force and must also support the weight:

T − mg = mv²/R

Therefore:

T = m(g + v²/R)

Given:

- m = 10 nanograms = 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ kg
- v = 5 m/s
- R = 5.0 m
- g = 10.0 m/s²

Centripetal acceleration:

v²/R = 25/5 = 5 m/s²

Thus:

T = 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ × (10 + 5)
T = 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ N

The tension in the string is 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ newtons.

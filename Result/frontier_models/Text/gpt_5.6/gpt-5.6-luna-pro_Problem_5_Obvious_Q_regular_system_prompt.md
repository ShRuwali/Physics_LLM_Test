Take rightward as positive.

Because block B is described as initially moving left, its initial velocity is:

vb1 = −100 m/s

Given:

- ma = 100 ng
- mb = 200 ng
- va1 = 0 m/s
- vb2 = 10 km/s = 10,000 m/s

Conservation of momentum:

ma va1 + mb vb1 = ma va2 + mb vb2

Substitute:

(100)(0) + (200)(−100) = (100)va2 + (200)(10,000)

Solving:

va2 = −20,200 m/s

Therefore, the final velocity of block A is:

**va2 = −20.2 km/s**

The negative sign means block A moves to the left after the collision.

Note: The statement also says block B is initially “moving to the right,” which conflicts with it moving toward block A from the right. If its initial velocity were actually +100 m/s, the result would instead be −19,800 m/s.

Take the rightward direction as positive.

Conservation of momentum:

Initial momentum = final momentum

(100 ng)(1.0 m/s) + (200 ng)(−2.0 m/s)
= (100 ng)(va2) + (200 ng)(1.0 m/s)

100 − 400 = 100 va2 + 200

−300 = 100 va2 + 200

100 va2 = −500

va2 = −5.0 m/s

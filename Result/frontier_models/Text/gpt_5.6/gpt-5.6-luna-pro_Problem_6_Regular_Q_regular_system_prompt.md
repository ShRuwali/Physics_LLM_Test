Using Hooke’s law:

Force = spring constant × displacement

1. Spring constant

Spring constant = Force / displacement  
= 5.0 N / 0.020 m  
= 250 N/m

2. Angular frequency

For a mass–spring oscillator:

Angular frequency = √(spring constant / mass)

= √(250 / 0.40)  
= √625  
= 25 rad/s

3. Frequency

Frequency = angular frequency / (2π)

= 25 / (2π)  
≈ 3.98 Hz

Therefore:

- Spring constant: 250 N/m
- Angular frequency: 25 rad/s
- Frequency: 3.98 Hz, approximately 4.0 Hz

The initial displacement of 0.040 m determines the amplitude, but not the angular frequency or frequency for an ideal spring.

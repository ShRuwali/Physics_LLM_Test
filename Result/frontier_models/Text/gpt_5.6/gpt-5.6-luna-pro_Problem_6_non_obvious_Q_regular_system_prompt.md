Using Hooke’s law:

Force constant:

k = F / x  
k = 5.0 N / 0.020 m  
k = 250 N/m

The mass is:

m = 500 nanograms = 500 × 10^-12 kg = 5.0 × 10^-10 kg

For a mass–spring oscillator, the angular frequency is:

ω = √(k/m)

ω = √[250 / (5.0 × 10^-10)]  
ω ≈ 7.07 × 10^5 rad/s

The ordinary frequency is:

f = ω / (2π)

f ≈ (7.07 × 10^5) / (2π)  
f ≈ 1.13 × 10^5 Hz

Therefore:

- Force constant: 250 N/m
- Angular frequency: 7.07 × 10^5 rad/s
- Frequency: 1.13 × 10^5 Hz, or approximately 113 kHz

The 0.040 m displacement is the oscillation amplitude and does not affect the frequency for an ideal spring.

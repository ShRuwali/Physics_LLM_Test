Using the stated mass:

- Mass: 1000 nanograms = 1.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg
- Block volume: (100 nm)³ = (1.0 × 10⁻⁷ m)³ = 1.0 × 10⁻²¹ m³
- Weight magnitude: m g = (1.0 × 10⁻⁹)(10) = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N
- Buoyant force magnitude: ρwater V g = (1.03 × 10³)(1.0 × 10⁻²¹)(10) = 1.03 × 10⁻¹⁷ N

Since the block is denser than the water, the cable tension magnitude is approximately

T = weight − buoyant force  
T = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ − 1.03 × 10⁻¹⁷  
T ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N

So, based on the stated mass:

**T ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N**

- From the given mass and volume, the density would be 1.0 × 10¹² kg/m³.
- A density of 1.0 × 10⁹ kg/m³ would correspond to a mass of 1.0 × 10⁻¹² kg, or 1 nanogram.

Using the stated density instead would give:

**T ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ N**

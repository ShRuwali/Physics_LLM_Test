The block’s volume is:

V = m / D = 20.0 / (19.0 × 10³) = 1.053 × 10⁻³ m³

The buoyant force is:

Fᵦ = dVg  
Fᵦ = (1.03 × 10³)(1.053 × 10⁻³)(10) = 10.8 N

The block’s weight is:

W = mg = (20.0)(10) = 200 N

Since the block is at rest:

T + Fᵦ = W

Therefore:

T = 200 − 10.8 = 189.2 N

The tension in the cable is approximately 189 N.

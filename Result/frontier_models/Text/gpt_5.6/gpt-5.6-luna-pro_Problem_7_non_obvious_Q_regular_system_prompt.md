The block is at rest, so the upward tension plus buoyant force balances its weight:

T + B = mg

Volume of the block:

V = (100 nm)^3 = (1.0 × 10⁻⁷ m)³ = 1.0 × 10⁻²¹ m³

Mass:

1000 nanograms = 1.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg

Weight:

mg = (1.0 × 10⁻⁹)(10) = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N

Buoyant force:

B = dVg  
B = (1.03 × 10³)(1.0 × 10⁻²¹)(10)  
B = 1.03 × 10⁻¹⁷ N

Therefore,

T = mg − B  
T = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ − 1.03 × 10⁻¹⁷  
T ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N

Answer: T ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ N

Note: The stated mass and density are inconsistent. A 100 nm cube with density 1.0 × 10⁹ kg/m³ would have a mass of 1.0 × 10⁻¹² kg, not 1.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg. The result above uses the explicitly given mass.

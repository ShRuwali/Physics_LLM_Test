Because both bodies are spherically symmetric, their gravitational attraction can be calculated as if all their mass were concentrated at their centers:

F = G M m / d²

Convert the planet’s mass:

- 6000 nanograms = 6.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg
- Moon’s mass = 2.0 × 10²² kg
- Center-to-center distance = 3.0 × 10⁹ m
- G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

Substituting:

F = (6.674 × 10⁻¹¹)(6.0 × 10⁻⁹)(2.0 × 10²²) / (3.0 × 10⁹)²

F ≈ 8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶ N

Therefore, the gravitational force on each body is approximately 8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶ newtons, directed toward the center of the other body.

Using Newton’s law of universal gravitation:

F = G M m / d²

where:

- G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
- M = 6.0 × 10²⁴ kg
- m = 2.0 × 10²² kg
- d = 3.0 × 10⁹ m

Substituting:

F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹)(6.0 × 10²⁴)(2.0 × 10²²) / (3.0 × 10⁹)²

F ≈ 8.9 × 10¹⁷ N

Therefore, the gravitational force between the planet and its moon at their closest distance is approximately 8.9 × 10¹⁷ newtons. Each body experiences this force in opposite directions.

Convert the planet’s mass:

- 6000 nanograms = 6000 × 10⁻¹² kg = 6.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg

Using Newton’s law of universal gravitation:

F = G M m / d²

where:

- G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
- M = 6.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg
- m = 2.0 × 10²² kg
- d = 3.0 × 10⁹ m

Substituting:

F = (6.674 × 10⁻¹¹)(6.0 × 10⁻⁹)(2.0 × 10²²) / (3.0 × 10⁹)²

F ≈ 8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶ N

Therefore, the gravitational force between the planet and its moon is approximately:

8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶ N

The forces on the two bodies are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.

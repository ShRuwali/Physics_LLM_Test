Based on the video, here is a brief description of the physical setup and the step-by-step calculation to find the total time taken by the block to slide down the inclined plane.

### **Video Description**
The video shows a block of mass $m = 12\text{ kg}$ sliding down an inclined plane of length $s = 5\text{ meters}$. The angle of inclination of the plane is $\theta = 30^\circ$, the coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the plane is $\mu = 0.5$, and the acceleration due to gravity is $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### **Calculation of Time Taken ($t$)**

#### **1. Identify the Forces Acting on the Block Along the Incline**
* **Component of gravitational force pulling down the incline:**
  $$F_g = mg \sin\theta$$
* **Frictional force opposing the motion up the incline:**
  $$F_f = \mu N = \mu mg \cos\theta$$

#### **2. Determine the Net Acceleration ($a$)**
Since $F_g > F_f$, the block slides down with an acceleration $a$:
$$a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m} = \frac{mg \sin\theta - \mu mg \cos\theta}{m} = g(\sin\theta - \mu \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the given values into the formula:
* $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$
* $\theta = 30^\circ \implies \sin(30^\circ) = 0.5$, $\cos(30^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.866$
* $\mu = 0.5$

$$a = 10 \left(0.5 - 0.5 \times 0.866\right)$$
$$a = 10 \left(0.5 - 0.433\right)$$
$$a = 10 \times 0.067 = 0.67\text{ m/s}^2$$

---

#### **3. Calculate the Time ($t$)**
Using the equation of motion for a block starting from rest ($u = 0$):
$$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$
$$5 = 0 + \frac{1}{2}(0.67)t^2$$
$$5 = 0.335 t^2$$
$$t^2 = \frac{5}{0.335} \approx 14.93$$
$$t = \sqrt{14.93} \approx 3.86\text{ seconds}$$

### **Answer**
The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters down the inclined plane is approximately **$3.86\text{ seconds}$**.

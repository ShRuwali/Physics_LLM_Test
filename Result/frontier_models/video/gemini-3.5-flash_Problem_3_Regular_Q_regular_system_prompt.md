### **Video Description**
The video shows a light blue solid sphere (a rigid body) of mass $m = 2\text{ kg}$ rolling down from the top of an inclined plane of height $h = 7\text{ meters}$. It starts from rest and rolls down without slipping under the influence of gravity ($g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$).

---

### **Calculation**

To find the velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the inclined plane, we use the **Law of Conservation of Energy**:

$$\text{Initial Mechanical Energy } (E_i) = \text{Final Mechanical Energy } (E_f)$$

#### **1. Initial Energy at the Top ($E_i$):**
At the top, the sphere is at rest, so it only possesses gravitational potential energy:
$$E_i = mgh$$

#### **2. Final Energy at the Bottom ($E_f$):**
At the bottom, all potential energy is converted into kinetic energy. Since the sphere rolls without slipping, its kinetic energy consists of both **translational** and **rotational** kinetic energies:
$$E_f = K_{\text{trans}} + K_{\text{rot}}$$
$$E_f = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

Where:
* $I$ is the moment of inertia of a solid sphere: $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$
* $\omega$ is the angular velocity, related to linear velocity $v$ by: $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$

Substitute $I$ and $\omega$ into the energy equation:
$$E_f = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$
$$E_f = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$
$$E_f = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

---

#### **3. Equating Initial and Final Energy ($E_i = E_f$):**
$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Notice that the mass ($m$) cancels out on both sides:
$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Rearranging the formula to solve for velocity ($v$):
$$v^2 = \frac{10}{7}gh$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}$$

---

#### **4. Substituting the Given Values:**
* $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$
* $h = 7\text{ m}$

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7} \times 10 \times 7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{10 \times 10}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100}$$
$$v = 10\text{ m/s}$$

### **Answer**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the inclined plane is **$10\text{ m/s}$**.

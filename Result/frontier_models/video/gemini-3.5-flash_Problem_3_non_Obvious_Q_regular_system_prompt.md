### **Velocity Calculation using Conservation of Energy**

According to the law of conservation of energy, the potential energy ($PE$) at the top of the incline is converted into kinetic energy ($KE$) at the bottom:

$$\text{Total Initial Energy } (E_i) = \text{Total Final Energy } (E_f)$$
$$PE_{\text{initial}} = KE_{\text{translational}} + KE_{\text{rotational}}$$

#### **1. Write the energy formulas:**
* **Potential Energy:** 
  $$PE = mgh$$
* **Kinetic Energy (Translational + Rotational):** 
  $$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

#### **2. Substitute the properties of a solid sphere:**
* For a uniform solid sphere, the moment of inertia is: 
  $$I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$$
* Since the sphere rolls without slipping, angular velocity $\omega$ is related to linear velocity $v$ by: 
  $$\omega = \frac{v}{r}$$

Now, substitute $I$ and $\omega$ into the kinetic energy equation:
$$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$
$$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$
$$KE = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

#### **3. Apply conservation of energy ($PE = KE$):**
$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Divide both sides by mass ($m$):
$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Solve for velocity ($v$):
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}$$

#### **4. Substitute the given values:**
* $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$
* $h = 7\text{ m}$

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7} \times 10 \times 7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100}$$
$$v = 10\text{ m/s}$$

### **Conclusion**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is **$10\text{ m/s}$**.

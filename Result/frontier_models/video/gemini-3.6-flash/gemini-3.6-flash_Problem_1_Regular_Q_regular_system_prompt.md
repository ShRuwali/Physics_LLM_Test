### Description
The video demonstrates a block of mass $m = 12.0\text{ kg}$ sliding down an inclined plane elevated at an angle $\theta = 30^\circ$. The distance along the incline to be traveled is $d = 5.0\text{ m}$. Given that the coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the plane is $\mu = 0.50$ and acceleration due to gravity is $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$, friction acts against the component of gravity pulling the block down the plane.

---

### Calculation

#### 1. Given Parameters:
* Mass of the block: $m = 12.0\text{ kg}$
* Angle of inclination: $\theta = 30^\circ$
* Distance along incline: $d = 5.0\text{ m}$
* Coefficient of kinetic friction: $\mu = 0.50$
* Acceleration due to gravity: $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
* Initial velocity: $u = 0\text{ m/s}$

---

#### 2. Acceleration Calculation:
The net force acting along the inclined plane is the difference between the component of gravity down the incline and the kinetic friction force:

$$F_{\text{net}} = m g \sin\theta - f_k$$

The normal force perpendicular to the surface is:

$$N = m g \cos\theta$$

The kinetic friction force is:

$$f_k = \mu N = \mu m g \cos\theta$$

Substituting $f_k$ into the net force equation:

$$m a = m g \sin\theta - \mu m g \cos\theta$$

Dividing both sides by mass $m$:

$$a = g (\sin\theta - \mu \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the known values:

$$a = 10.0 \times (\sin 30^\circ - 0.50 \times \cos 30^\circ)$$

$$a = 10.0 \times (0.5 - 0.50 \times 0.866)$$

$$a = 10.0 \times (0.5 - 0.433) = 10.0 \times 0.067 = 0.67\text{ m/s}^2$$

---

#### 3. Time Taken Calculation:
Using the kinematic equation for distance starting from rest:

$$d = ut + \frac{1}{2} a t^2$$

Since $u = 0\text{ m/s}$:

$$d = \frac{1}{2} a t^2 \implies t = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{a}}$$

Substituting $d = 5.0\text{ m}$ and $a \approx 0.67\text{ m/s}^2$:

$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 5.0}{0.67}} = \sqrt{\frac{10.0}{0.67}} \approx \sqrt{14.93} \approx 3.86\text{ s}$$

---

### Final Result:
The total time taken by the block to travel $5.0\text{ meters}$ down the incline is approximately **$3.86\text{ seconds}$**.

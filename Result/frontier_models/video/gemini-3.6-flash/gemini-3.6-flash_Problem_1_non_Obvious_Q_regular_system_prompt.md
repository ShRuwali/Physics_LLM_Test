### Description

The video shows an animation of a small cubic block sliding down an inclined plane angled at $\theta = 30^\circ$. The incline has a total length of $5.0\text{ meters}$. Given parameters include the mass and dimensions of the block, the coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu = 0.50$, and the acceleration due to gravity $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### Calculation of Time Taken

#### 1. Analyze the Forces Acting on the Block
The force component pulling the block down the incline is:
$$F_{\text{down}} = m g \sin\theta$$

The normal force perpendicular to the incline is:
$$N = m g \cos\theta$$

 peak frictional force opposing the motion is given by:
$$f_k = \mu N = \mu m g \cos\theta$$

#### 2. Acceleration of the Block
Applying Newton's second law along the incline:
$$m a = m g \sin\theta - \mu m g \cos\theta$$

Dividing by mass $m$ (showing that mass and dimensions are independent of acceleration):
$$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the known values ($g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$, $\theta = 30^\circ$, and $\mu = 0.50$):
$$\sin 30^\circ = 0.5$$
$$\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.8660$$

$$a = 10.0 \times \left(0.5 - 0.50 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$$
$$a = 10.0 \times (0.5 - 0.4330)$$
$$a \approx 0.6699\text{ m/s}^2$$

#### 3. Time Taken to Travel $5.0\text{ meters}$
Using the kinematic equation for motion from rest ($u = 0$):
$$s = \frac{1}{2} a t^2$$

Solving for time $t$:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{a}}$$

Substitute $s = 5.0\text{ m}$ and $a \approx 0.6699\text{ m/s}^2$:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 5.0}{0.6699}} = \sqrt{\frac{10}{0.6699}} \approx \sqrt{14.928} \approx 3.86\text{ seconds}$$

### Final Answer
The total time taken by the block to travel $5\text{ meters}$ down the incline is approximately **$3.86\text{ seconds}$**.

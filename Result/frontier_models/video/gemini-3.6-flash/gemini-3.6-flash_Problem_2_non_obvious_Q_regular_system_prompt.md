### Description

The video displays a pendulum setup consisting of a small sphere of mass $m = 2.5 \text{ nanograms}$ and diameter $D = 1 \text{ nanometer}$ attached to a string of negligible mass. The distance from the point of support to the center of the sphere is given as $L = 2.0 \text{ meters}$, and the acceleration due to gravity is $g = 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### Time Period Calculation

Since the radius of the sphere $R = \frac{D}{2} = 0.5 \text{ nanometer} = 0.5 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$ is extremely small compared to the distance $L = 2.0 \text{ m}$, the system behaves as a simple pendulum where the mass is treated as a point mass concentrated at its center.

For small angular displacements, the time period $T$ of a pendulum is given by:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

Substituting the given values:

$$L = 2.0 \text{ m}$$
$$g = 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{2.0}{10.0}}$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{0.2}$$

$$T \approx 2\pi \times 0.4472 \approx 2.81 \text{ seconds}$$

Thus, the time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately $2.81 \text{ seconds}$.

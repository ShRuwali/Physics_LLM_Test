### Video Description

The video depicts a solid sphere (a rigid body) starting from rest at the top of an inclined plane of height $h = 7.0\text{ meters}$ and rolling down without slipping to the bottom under the influence of gravity ($g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$). 

---

### Calculation

#### **Given Data:**
* Mass of the solid sphere ($m$) = $2.0\text{ kg}$
* Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
* Height of the incline ($h$) = $7.0\text{ m}$
* Moment of inertia of a solid sphere ($I$) = $\frac{2}{5}mR^2$

---

#### **Formula & Derivation:**

By the law of conservation of energy:

$$\text{Initial Potential Energy} = \text{Final Kinetic Energy}$$

$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

Since the sphere rolls without slipping, the relationship between linear velocity ($v$) and angular velocity ($\omega$) is given by $\omega = \frac{v}{R}$.

Substitute $I = \frac{2}{5}mR^2$ and $\omega = \frac{v}{R}$ into the equation:

$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mR^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{R}\right)^2$$

$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$

$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

Canceling mass ($m$) from both sides:

$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Solving for velocity ($v$):

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gh}$$

---

#### **Calculation:**

Substitute the given values into the velocity equation:

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10}{7} \times 10.0 \times 7.0}$$

$$v = \sqrt{100}$$

$$v = 10.0\text{ m/s}$$

---

### **Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is **$10.0\text{ m/s}$**.

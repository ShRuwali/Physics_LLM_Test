### Description

The video depicts a blue spherical body attached to a central point by a string, moving in a vertical circular path of radius $R = 5.0\text{ m}$. The parameters listed in the video are:
* **Mass of body ($m$):** $10\text{ nanograms} = 10 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 10^{-11}\text{ kg}$
* **Radius of body:** $100\text{ nanometers}$
* **Radius of circular motion ($R$):** $5.0\text{ m}$
* **Speed of the body ($v$):** $3.001 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$
* **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$):** $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

---

### Physics Note on Mass & Speed Limit
In physical reality according to Special Relativity, no massive particle can equal or exceed the speed of light in vacuum ($c \approx 2.998 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$). Since $v = 3.001 \times 10^8\text{ m/s} > c$, this state is non-physical. However, treating this strictly as a theoretical classical mechanics problem gives the tension calculated below.

---

### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom of the Circle

At the bottom of the vertical circle, the forces acting on the body are:
1. Tension force $T$ directed upward toward the center.
2. Gravitational force $mg$ directed downward.

The net force provides the required centripetal acceleration toward the center:
$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

Rearranging for the tension $T$:
$$T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

#### Step-by-Step Substitution:

1. **Weight component ($mg$):**
$$mg = 10^{-11}\text{ kg} \times 10.0\text{ m/s}^2 = 10^{-10}\text{ N}$$

2. **Centripetal component ($\frac{mv^2}{R}$):**
$$v^2 = (3.001 \times 10^8\text{ m/s})^2 = 9.006001 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}^2$$

$$\frac{mv^2}{R} = \frac{10^{-11}\text{ kg} \times 9.006001 \times 10^{16}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}^2}{5.0\text{ m}} = \frac{9.006001 \times 10^5}{5.0}\text{ N} = 1.8012002 \times 10^5\text{ N}$$

3. **Total Tension ($T$):**
$$T = 10^{-10}\text{ N} + 1.8012002 \times 10^5\text{ N} \approx 1.8012 \times 10^5\text{ N} \quad (180,120.02\text{ N})$$

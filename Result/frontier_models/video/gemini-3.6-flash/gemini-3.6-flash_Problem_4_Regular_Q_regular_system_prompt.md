### Description

The video illustrates a body undergoing vertical circular motion while attached to a light string of fixed length. As the object moves along the circular path, text on the right provides the physical conditions and parameters governing the motion.

---

### Given Parameters

- Mass of the body ($m$) = $5.0\text{ kg}$
- Radius of circular motion ($R$) = $5.0\text{ m}$
- Speed of the body ($v$) = $5.0\text{ m/s}$
- Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

---

### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom of the Circle

At the lowest point of the circular path, two forces act vertically on the mass:
1. Tension ($T$) directed upwards towards the center of rotation.
2. Weight ($mg$) directed downwards.

The net upward force provides the necessary centripetal force ($F_c$) required for circular motion:

$$T - mg = \frac{m v^2}{R}$$

Rearranging the equation to solve for tension $T$:

$$T = mg + \frac{m v^2}{R}$$

Substituting the given values into the equation:

$$T = (5.0\text{ kg})(10.0\text{ m/s}^2) + \frac{(5.0\text{ kg})(5.0\text{ m/s})^2}{5.0\text{ m}}$$

$$T = 50.0\text{ N} + 25.0\text{ N}$$

$$T = 75.0\text{ N}$$

The tension in the string at the bottom of the circle is $75.0\text{ N}$.

### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom of the Circle

At the lowest point of the vertical circle, the tension force $T$ acts upward toward the center, while the force of gravity $mg$ acts downward. The net force provides the required centripetal acceleration directed toward the center:

$$F_{\text{net}} = T - mg = \frac{m v^2}{R}$$

Solving for the tension $T$:

$$T = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{R} \right)$$

$$T = (1.0 \times 10^{-11}\text{ kg}) \times (15.0\text{ m/s}^2)$$

$$T = 1.5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ N}$$

### Calculation of Velocity of Block A ($v_{a2}$)

Assuming conservation of linear momentum along a 1D axis:

$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$(10^{-10} \text{ kg})(0 \text{ m/s}) + (0.20 \text{ kg})(2.0 \text{ m/s}) = (10^{-10} \text{ kg}) v_{a2} + (0.20 \text{ kg})(10000 \text{ m/s})$$

$$0.40 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s} = 10^{-10} v_{a2} + 2000 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

Solve for $v_{a2}$:

$$10^{-10} v_{a2} = 0.40 - 2000 = -1999.6 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

$$v_{a2} = \frac{-1999.6}{10^{-10}} = -1.9996 \times 10^{13} \text{ m/s}$$

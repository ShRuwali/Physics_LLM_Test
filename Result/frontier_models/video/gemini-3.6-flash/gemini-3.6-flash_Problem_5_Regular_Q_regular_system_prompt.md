### Video Description

The video demonstrates a one-dimensional collision problem involving two blocks, Block A (blue) and Block B (orange), moving towards each other on a frictionless surface:

* **Block A**: 
  * Mass ($m_a$) = $0.50\text{ kg}$
  * Initial velocity ($v_{a1}$) = $1.0\text{ m/s}$
* **Block B**: 
  * Mass ($m_b$) = $0.20\text{ kg}$
  * Initial velocity ($v_{b1}$) = $-2.0\text{ m/s}$

After the collision, Block B moves in the opposite direction with a final velocity of $v_{b2} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$. The video asks to calculate the velocity of Block A after the collision ($v_{a2}$).

---

### Calculation of Velocity ($v_{a2}$)

By the law of conservation of linear momentum, the total momentum before the collision equals the total momentum after the collision:

$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$(0.50\text{ kg})(1.0\text{ m/s}) + (0.20\text{ kg})(-2.0\text{ m/s}) = (0.50\text{ kg}) v_{a2} + (0.20\text{ kg})(1.0\text{ m/s})$$

Simplify both sides:

$$0.50 - 0.40 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$

$$0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$

Subtract $0.20$ from both sides:

$$-0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2}$$

Solve for $v_{a2}$:

$$v_{a2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50} = -0.20\text{ m/s}$$

The velocity of Block A after the collision is $-0.20\text{ m/s}$ (moving to the left).

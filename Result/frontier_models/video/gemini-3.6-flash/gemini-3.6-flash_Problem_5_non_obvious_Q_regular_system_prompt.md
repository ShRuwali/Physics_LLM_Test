### Description

The video demonstrates a one-dimensional collision between two cubic objects, **Block A** (blue) and **Block B** (orange), moving on a frictionless horizontal surface:

* **Block A** moves to the right with an initial velocity of $1.0\text{ m/s}$.
* **Block B** moves to the left with an initial velocity of $-2.0\text{ m/s}$.
* After colliding, Block B rebounds to the right with a final velocity of $1.0\text{ m/s}$, while Block A rebounds to the left.

---

### Given Parameters

* Mass of Block A ($m_{\text{a}}$): $100\text{ ng}$
* Mass of Block B ($m_{\text{b}}$): $200\text{ ng}$
* Initial velocity of Block A ($v_{\text{a1}}$): $1.0\text{ m/s}$
* Initial velocity of Block B ($v_{\text{b1}}$): $-2.0\text{ m/s}$
* Final velocity of Block B ($v_{\text{b2}}$): $1.0\text{ m/s}$

---

### Calculation

According to the **Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum**, the total momentum before the collision equals the total momentum after the collision:

$$m_{\text{a}} v_{\text{a1}} + m_{\text{b}} v_{\text{b1}} = m_{\text{a}} v_{\text{a2}} + m_{\text{b}} v_{\text{b2}}$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$(100\text{ ng})(1.0\text{ m/s}) + (200\text{ ng})(-2.0\text{ m/s}) = (100\text{ ng}) v_{\text{a2}} + (200\text{ ng})(1.0\text{ m/s})$$

Simplify each term:

$$100 - 400 = 100 v_{\text{a2}} + 200$$

$$-300 = 100 v_{\text{a2}} + 200$$

Subtract $200$ from both sides:

$$100 v_{\text{a2}} = -500$$

Solve for $v_{\text{a2}}$:

$$v_{\text{a2}} = \frac{-500}{100} = -5.0\text{ m/s}$$

---

### Conclusion

The velocity of Block A after the collision ($v_{\text{a2}}$) is **$-5.0\text{ m/s}$** (moving to the left at $5.0\text{ m/s}$).

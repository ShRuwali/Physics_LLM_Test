### Video Description

The video shows two horizontal spring-mass systems:

1. **Top System (Spring and spring balance):** A spring fixed to a wall on the left is attached to a spring balance on the right. Initially, the system is at equilibrium ($x = 0\text{ meters}$). The spring balance is then pulled to a displacement of $x = 20\text{ meters}$, displaying a measured force of $F = 0\text{ N}$.
2. **Bottom System (Spring and block):** A spring fixed to a wall on the left is attached to a cubic block of mass $m = 500\text{ nanograms}$ with dimensions $\text{length} = \text{height} = \text{width} = 400\text{ nanometers}$. The block is initially at equilibrium ($x = 0\text{ meters}$), pulled to a displacement of $0.040\text{ meters}$, and then released to undergo simple harmonic oscillation.

---

### 1. Calculation of the Force Constant ($k$)

Hooke's Law states that the force applied to a spring is directly proportional to its displacement:

$$F = k \cdot x$$

From the top half of the video:
* Displacement $x = 20\text{ m}$
* Force $F = 0\text{ N}$

Solving for the spring constant $k$:

$$k = \frac{F}{x}$$

$$k = \frac{0\text{ N}}{20\text{ m}} = 0\text{ N/m}$$

---

### 2. Calculation of Angular Frequency ($\omega$) and Frequency ($f$)

Given parameters for the block in the bottom half of the video:
* Mass $m = 500\text{ nanograms} = 500 \times 10^{-9}\text{ g} = 500 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}$
* Spring constant $k = 0\text{ N/m}$

#### Angular Frequency ($\omega$):

$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$

$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{0\text{ N/m}}{5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}}} = 0\text{ rad/s}$$

#### Frequency ($f$):

$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$

$$f = \frac{0\text{ rad/s}}{2\pi} = 0\text{ Hz}$$

---

### 3. Physical Analysis and Justifiability

1. **Zero Force Constant ($k = 0\text{ N/m}$):**
   A spring constant of $k = 0\text{ N/m}$ implies that the spring exerts no restoring force ($F_{restoring} = 0\text{ N}$) regardless of how far it is displaced. Consequently, when released, the block cannot experience any acceleration, rendering periodic harmonic oscillation physically impossible ($\omega = 0\text{ rad/s}$ and $f = 0\text{ Hz}$).

2. **Unreasonably High Density:**
   * Volume of the block $V = (400\text{ nm})^3 = (400 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m})^3 = 6.4 \times 10^{-20}\text{ m}^3$
   * Mass of the block $m = 5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}$
   * Density $\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}}{6.4 \times 10^{-20}\text{ m}^3} \approx 7.8125 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$

   A mass density of nearly $7.81 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$ is completely unphysical for ordinary matter (it is nearly $350,000$ times denser than osmium, the densest element on Earth, and far exceeds the density of the Sun's core).

3. **Disproportionate Scale Parameters:**
   The block has nanoscale dimensions ($400\text{ nm} = 4 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$), but it is subjected to macroscopic displacements ($0.040\text{ m} = 4 \times 10^{-2}\text{ m}$), which is $100,000$ times larger than the size of the block itself.

**Conclusion:** 
While the mathematical calculations are straightforwardly derived from the given numbers, the system is **physically unjustifiable** due to non-physical parameters ($k = 0\text{ N/m}$ preventing oscillation, extremely unrealistic density, and mismatched spatial scales).

### Video Description

The video shows a small block suspended from a rigid support by a thin cable. Below the block is a container filled with sea water. As the video plays, the support moves downward, lowering the block until it becomes completely submerged in the sea water. The text on the left screen provides the physical properties of the block, sea water, and the effective gravitational acceleration.

### Given Parameters

From the video, the given values are:

* **Mass of block ($m$)**: $1000\text{ nanograms} = 1000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$
* **Dimensions of block**: $\text{Length} = \text{Width} = \text{Height} = 100\text{ nanometers} = 100 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$
* **Volume of block ($V$)**: 
  $$V = (1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m})^3 = 1.0 \times 10^{-21}\text{ m}^3$$
* **Density of block ($D$)**: $1.0 \times 10^9\text{ kg/m}^3$
* **Density of sea water ($d$)**: $-1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$ (or magnitude $|d| = 1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$)
* **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$)**: $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$ (upward)

---

### Calculation of Tension ($T$)

When the block is fully submerged and in static equilibrium, three vertical forces act on it:

1. **Gravitational Force ($\vec{F}_g$)**: Since gravity is specified as acting **upward**, the weight vector is directed upward:
   $$F_g = m \cdot g = (1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N} \quad \text{(upward)}$$

2. **Buoyant Force ($\vec{F}_B$)**: Buoyancy acts in the direction opposite to gravity, so it is directed **downward**:
   $$F_B = |d| \cdot V \cdot g = (1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3) \times (1.0 \times 10^{-21}\text{ m}^3) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) = 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N} \quad \text{(downward)}$$

3. **Cable Tension ($\vec{T}$)**: The cable exerts a pulling force to maintain equilibrium.

Setting up the force balance equation along the vertical axis (taking upward as positive):

$$\sum F_y = 0 \implies F_g - F_B - T = 0$$

$$T = F_g - F_B$$

Substituting the calculated values:

$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N} \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

*(Note: If mass is calculated directly from the block's density $m = D \cdot V = 1.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$, then $F_g = 1.0 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}$, yielding $T \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}$. Using the explicitly stated mass of $1000\text{ ng}$ yields $T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$.)*

---

### Final Result

The tension in the cable is:

$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

### Video Description

The video illustrates a solid rectangular block hanging from a vertical cable attached to an adjustable horizontal arm. The support mechanism gradually lowers the block down into a tank containing sea water until the block is completely submerged.

---

### Calculation of Tension ($T$)

When the block is fully submerged in sea water and in equilibrium, three main forces act on it:
1. Downward gravitational force ($F_g = m \cdot g$)
2. Upward buoyant force ($F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g$)
3. Upward tension force in the cable ($T$)

#### Given Data:
* Mass of the block, $m = 20.0 \text{ kg}$
* Density of the block, $D = 19.0 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$
* Density of sea water, $d = 1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$
* Acceleration due to gravity, $g = 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$

---

#### Step 1: Calculate the Volume ($V$) of the Block
$$V = \frac{m}{D}$$

$$V = \frac{20.0 \text{ kg}}{19.0 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3} \approx 1.0526 \times 10^{-3} \text{ m}^3$$

---

#### Step 2: Calculate the Gravitational Force ($F_g$)
$$F_g = m \cdot g$$

$$F_g = 20.0 \text{ kg} \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 = 200.0 \text{ N}$$

---

#### Step 3: Calculate the Buoyant Force ($F_b$)
$$F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g$$

$$F_b = (1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3) \times \left(\frac{20.0}{19.0 \times 10^3} \text{ m}^3\right) \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$$

$$F_b = \frac{1.03 \times 200.0}{19.0} \approx 10.84 \text{ N}$$

---

#### Step 4: Calculate the Tension ($T$) in the Cable
Setting up the force balance equation:
$$T + F_b = F_g$$

$$T = F_g - F_b$$

$$T = 200.0 \text{ N} - 10.84 \text{ N} \approx 189.16 \text{ N}$$

Thus, the tension in the cable when the block is fully submerged is approximately **$189.2 \text{ N}$**.

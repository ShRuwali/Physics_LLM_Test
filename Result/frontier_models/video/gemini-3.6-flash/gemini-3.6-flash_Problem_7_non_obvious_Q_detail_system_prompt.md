### Description of the Video

The video depicts a schematic setup where a small cubic block is suspended by a cable attached to a support structure. A container filled with sea water moves upward until the block becomes completely submerged in the liquid. Numerical values for the mass, dimensions, density of the block, density of sea water, and gravitational acceleration are provided on the left side of the screen.

### Calculation of Tension ($T$) in the Cable

#### Given Parameters:
- Mass of block ($m$) = $1000 \text{ nanograms} = 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
- Block dimensions: $\text{Length} = \text{Width} = \text{Height} = 100 \text{ nanometers} = 10^{-7} \text{ m}$
- Density of block ($D$) = $1.0 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/m}^3$
- Density of sea water ($d$) = $1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$
- Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$

#### Volume and Buoyant Force Calculation:
The volume of the cubic block ($V$) is:
$$V = (10^{-7} \text{ m})^3 = 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3$$

The buoyant force ($F_b$) acting on the fully submerged block is:
$$F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g$$
$$F_b = (1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3) \times (10^{-21} \text{ m}^3) \times (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2) = 1.03 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N}$$

#### Tension Calculation:
**1. Based on the explicitly stated mass ($m = 1000 \text{ ng}$):**
The weight of the block ($W$) is:
$$W = m \cdot g = 10^{-9} \text{ kg} \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 = 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

The tension ($T$) in the cable when submerged is:
$$T = W - F_b$$
$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N} \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

**2. Based on the given block density ($D = 1.0 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/m}^3$):**
The calculated mass would be:
$$m_{\text{calc}} = D \cdot V = (1.0 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/m}^3) \times (10^{-21} \text{ m}^3) = 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 1 \text{ ng}$$

The weight would then be:
$$W_{\text{calc}} = 10^{-12} \text{ kg} \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 = 1.0 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}$$

The tension in this case is:
$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N} \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}$$

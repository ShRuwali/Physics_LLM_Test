### Description

The video demonstrates a solid square block attached to a cable being lowered from above into a container of sea water until it becomes completely submerged.

---

### Calculation of Tension ($T$)

#### 1. Given Parameters

* Mass of the block ($m$): $1000 \text{ nanograms} = 1000 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 1.0 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
* Dimensions of the block: $100 \text{ nm} \times 100 \text{ nm} \times 100 \text{ nm}$
* Volume of the block ($V$):
$$V = (100 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m})^3 = 1.0 \times 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3$$
* Density of sea water ($d$): $1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$
* Density of the block ($D$): $1.0 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/m}^3$
* Acceleration due to gravity ($g$): $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$

---

#### 2. Forces Acting on the Submerged Block

1. **Downward Gravitational Force (Weight, $W$):**
$$W = m \cdot g = (1.0 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}) \cdot (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2) = 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

2. **Upward Buoyant Force ($F_b$):**
$$F_b = d \cdot V \cdot g = (1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3) \cdot (1.0 \times 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3) \cdot (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2) = 1.03 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N}$$

---

#### 3. Tension ($T$)

Setting up the force equilibrium for the submerged block:

$$T + F_b = W$$

$$T = W - F_b$$

$$T = 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N} \approx 1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

The buoyant force is negligible compared to the weight of the block, so the tension in the cable is approximately $1.0 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$ (or $10 \text{ nN}$).

### Gravitational Force Calculation

* Mass of Planet A ($M$):
  $$M = 6000\text{ ng} = 6000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 6 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$$
* Mass of Moon ($m$):
  $$m = 2 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$$
* Distance between centers ($d$):
  $$d = 3 \times 10^9\text{ m}$$

---

#### **2. Formula**
According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

#### **3. Step-by-Step Calculation**

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times \frac{(6 \times 10^{-9}) \times (2 \times 10^{22})}{(3 \times 10^9)^2}$$

Simplify the numerator:

$$(6 \times 10^{-9}) \times (2 \times 10^{22}) = 12 \times 10^{13}\text{ kg}^2$$

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times \frac{12 \times 10^{13}}{9 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times \frac{4}{3} \times 10^{-5}$$

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times 1.3333 \times 10^{-5}$$

$$F \approx 8.8987 \times 10^{-16}\text{ N}$$

The magnitude of the gravitational force exerted by each body on the other is approximately **$8.90 \times 10^{-16}\text{ N}$**.

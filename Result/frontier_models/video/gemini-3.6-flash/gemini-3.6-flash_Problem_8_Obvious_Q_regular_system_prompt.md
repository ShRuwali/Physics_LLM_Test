### Gravitational Force Calculation

To find the gravitational force between Planet A and the moon, we use Newton's law of universal gravitation:

$$F = G \frac{M m}{d^2}$$

* Mass of Planet A, $M = 6000 \text{ nanograms} = 6000 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
* Mass of the moon, $m = 2 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
* Distance between their centers, $d = 3 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$
* Universal gravitational constant, $G \approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$

#### Calculation:

Substituting the values into the formula:

$$F = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2) \times (6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}) \times (2 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg})}{(3 \times 10^9 \text{ m})^2}$$

$$F = \frac{80.088 \times 10^2}{9 \times 10^{18}} \text{ N}$$

$$F = 8.899 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$$

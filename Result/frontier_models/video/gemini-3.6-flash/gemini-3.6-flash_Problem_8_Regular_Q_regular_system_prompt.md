### Calculation of Gravitational Force

According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the magnitude of the gravitational force $F$ between two spherically symmetric bodies is given by:

$$F = G \frac{M m}{d^2}$$

where:
* $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ (Universal Gravitational Constant)
* $M = 6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}$
* $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
* $d = 3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$

Substituting these values into the formula:

$$F = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times (6.0 \times 10^{24}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

$$F = \frac{8.0088 \times 10^{36}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F \approx 8.89 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$$

The gravitational force exerted by each body on the other at the closest distance is approximately $8.89 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$.

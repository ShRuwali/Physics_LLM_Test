### Brief Description

The video shows a diagram of a microscopic body labeled "Planet A" and a celestial body labeled "Moon" moving in an elliptical path around Planet A. The figure provides the following given quantities:

- **Mass of Planet A ($M$):** $6000 \text{ nanograms} = 6000 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 6.0 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
- **Radius of Planet A:** $100 \text{ nanometers} = 100 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 1.0 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m}$
- **Mass of Moon ($m$):** $2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
- **Closest distance between centers ($d$):** $3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ meters}$

### Calculation of Gravitational Force

According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the force between two spherically symmetric bodies is given by:

$$F = G \frac{M m}{d^2}$$

Where:
- $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ (Universal gravitational constant)
- $M = 6.0 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
- $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
- $d = 3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$

Substituting the values into the formula:

$$F = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times (6.0 \times 10^{-9}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

$$F = \frac{8008.8}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F \approx 8.90 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$$

### Physical Viability and Critique

1. **Unreasonable Parameters and Scale Disparity:**
   - **Absurd Mass Ratio:** Planet A has a mass of only $6000 \text{ nanograms}$ (comparable to a single microscopic cell or a particle of dust), while the Moon has a planetary-scale mass of $2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$ (similar to Pluto or Earth's Moon). 
   - **Negligible Gravitational Attraction:** A gravitational force of approximately $8.9 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$ across a distance of $3 \text{ million kilometers}$ is non-existent for all practical purposes.

2. **Challenged Physical Laws & Assumptions:**
   - **Orbital Mechanics:** A microscopic particle cannot gravitationally anchor a massive moon in orbit around itself. In reality, the barycenter (center of mass) of such a system lies inside the Moon, and Planet A would be performing extreme motion or be disrupted by external radiation pressure, thermal motion, and stray electromagnetic forces.
   - **Dominance of Non-Gravitational Forces:** At a scale of $100 \text{ nm}$ and mass of $6000 \text{ ng}$, forces such as photon radiation pressure (from starlight/sunlight), electrostatic forces, and solar wind would exceed the gravitational force by many orders of magnitude.

3. **Conclusion on Justifiability:**
   - While mathematically solvable using Newton's law, **the model is physically unfeasible and unjustifiable** in the real world.

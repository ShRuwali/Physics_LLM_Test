### Gravitational Force Calculation

According to Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, the magnitude of the gravitational force $F$ between two spherically symmetric bodies is given by:

$$F = G \frac{M m}{d^2}$$

where $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ is the universal gravitational constant.

#### Step 1: Convert units to SI

* $M = 6000\text{ ng} = 6000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 6.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$
* $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$
* $d = 3.0 \times 10^9\text{ m}$

#### Step 2: Substitute values into the equation

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(6.0 \times 10^{-9})(2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times \frac{1.2 \times 10^{14}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F = \frac{80.088 \times 10^2}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F \approx 8.90 \times 10^{-16}\text{ N}$$

### Conclusion

The gravitational force exerted by each body on the other at the closest distance is approximately $8.90 \times 10^{-16}\text{ N}$.

**Step 1: Identify the given information**

*   The block is on an inclined plane with an angle of 30 degrees.
*   The mass of the block is 1 nanogram (m = 1 ng).
*   The coefficient of kinetic friction between the plane and the block is -0.5.
*   The acceleration due to gravity (g) is 10 m/s^2 upward.

**Step 2: Determine the direction of acceleration**

Since the acceleration due to gravity is 10 m/s^2 upward, the block will move up the inclined plane.

**Step 3: Calculate the force of gravity**

The force of gravity (Fg) is given by:

Fg = m * g

where m is the mass of the block and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

Fg = 1 ng * 10 m/s^2 = 0.000000001 N

**Step 4: Calculate the normal force**

The normal force (N) is equal to the force of gravity (Fg) since the block is on an inclined plane.

N = Fg = 0.000000001 N

**Step 5: Calculate the force of friction**

The force of friction (Ff) is given by:

Ff = μk * N

where μk is the coefficient of kinetic friction.

Ff = -0.5 * 0.000000001 N = -0.0000000005 N

The negative sign indicates that the force of friction is opposite to the direction of motion.

**Step 6: Calculate the net force**

The net force (Fnet) is the sum of the force of gravity and the force of friction.

Fnet = Fg + Ff = 0.000000001 N - 0.0000000005 N = 0.0000000005 N

**Step 7: Calculate the acceleration**

The acceleration (a) is given by:

a = Fnet / m

a = 0.0000000005 N / 1 ng = 0.5 m/s^2

**Step 8: Calculate the time**

The time (t) it takes for the block to travel 5 meters can be calculated using the equation:

s = ut + (1/2)at^2

where s is the distance, u is the initial velocity (which is 0), and a is the acceleration.

5 m = 0 + (1/2) * 0.5 m/s^2 * t^2

t^2 = 20

t = sqrt(20) = 4.47 s

**Step 9: Calculate the total time**

Since the block is moving up the inclined plane, it will take longer to reach the top than to come back down. Therefore, we need to multiply the time by 2 to get the total time.

Total time = 2 * 4.47 s = 8.94 s

**Answer**: The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is 8.94 seconds.

**Step 1: Identify the given information**

*   The block has a mass of 12 kg.
*   The coefficient of kinetic friction between the plane and the block is 0.5.
*   The block is accelerated due to gravity (g) = 10 m/s^2.
*   The block travels a distance of 5 meters.

**Step 2: Determine the force of gravity acting on the block**

*   The force of gravity (Fg) = mass (m) x acceleration due to gravity (g)
*   Fg = 12 kg x 10 m/s^2 = 120 N

**Step 3: Calculate the force of kinetic friction**

*   The force of kinetic friction (Fk) = coefficient of kinetic friction (μk) x normal force (N)
*   Since the block is on an inclined plane, the normal force (N) = Fg x cos(30°)
*   N = 120 N x cos(30°) = 120 N x 0.866 = 103.92 N
*   Fk = 0.5 x 103.92 N = 51.96 N

**Step 4: Calculate the net force acting on the block**

*   The net force (Fnet) = Fg - Fk
*   Fnet = 120 N - 51.96 N = 68.04 N

**Step 5: Calculate the acceleration of the block**

*   Since the block is moving up the inclined plane, the acceleration (a) = Fnet / mass (m)
*   a = 68.04 N / 12 kg = 5.67 m/s^2

**Step 6: Calculate the time taken by the block to travel 5 meters**

*   Since the block is moving at an angle of 30°, the distance traveled (d) = 5 m / cos(30°)
*   d = 5 m / 0.866 = 5.77 m
*   The time taken (t) = distance (d) / speed (v)
*   Since the block is accelerating, we need to use the equation of motion: v^2 = u^2 + 2as
*   We can assume the initial velocity (u) = 0
*   v^2 = 0 + 2 x 5.67 m/s^2 x 5.77 m
*   v^2 = 65.29 m^2/s^2
*   v = sqrt(65.29 m^2/s^2) = 8.13 m/s
*   t = 5.77 m / 8.13 m/s = 0.71 s

**Answer:** The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately 0.71 seconds.

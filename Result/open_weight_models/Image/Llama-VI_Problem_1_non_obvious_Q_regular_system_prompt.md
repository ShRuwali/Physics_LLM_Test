*   The block travels a distance of 5 meters.

*   The force of gravity (Fg) is given by Fg = m * g = 1 ng * 10 m/s^2 = 0.000000001 N (since 1 ng = 10^-9 kg).

**Step 3: Calculate the normal force acting on the block**

*   The normal force (FN) is given by FN = Fg * cos(θ) = 0.000000001 N * cos(30°) = 0.0000000008 N.

**Step 4: Calculate the force of friction acting on the block**

*   The force of friction (FF) is given by FF = μk * FN = 0.5 * 0.0000000008 N = 0.0000000004 N.

**Step 5: Calculate the acceleration of the block**

*   The acceleration (a) is given by a = g * sin(θ) = 10 m/s^2 * sin(30°) = 5 m/s^2.

**Step 6: Calculate the time taken by the block to travel 5 meters**

*   The time (t) is given by t = distance / speed = 5 m / 5 m/s = 1 s.

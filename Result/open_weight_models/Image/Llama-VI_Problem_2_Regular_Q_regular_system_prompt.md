**Step 1: Identify the type of motion**

The given situation is a simple harmonic motion (SHM) of a mass attached to a string. The mass is oscillating about a fixed point, and the motion is periodic.

**Step 2: Determine the necessary parameters**

*   Mass (m) = 2 kg
*   Length of the string (L) = 1 meter
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10 m/s^2

**Step 3: Calculate the time period**

The time period (T) of a simple harmonic motion is given by the formula:

T = 2π√(L/g)

where L is the length of the string and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

**Step 4: Substitute the values and calculate**

T = 2π√(1/10)
= 2π√0.1
= 2π/3.16
= 1.99 seconds

**Answer**: 1.99 seconds

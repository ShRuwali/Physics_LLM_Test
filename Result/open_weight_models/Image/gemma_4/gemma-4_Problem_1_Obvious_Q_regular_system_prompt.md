### **Description of the Problem**
A block with a mass ($m$) of 1 nanogram and dimensions of 1 nanometer is placed on an inclined plane. The plane is tilted at an angle ($\theta$) of $30^\circ$ relative to the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction ($\mu_k$) between the block and the plane is $0.50$. The acceleration due to gravity ($g$) is $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$. We need to find the time it takes for the block to slide down a distance ($d$) of $5.0\text{ meters}$ starting from rest.

---

### **Calculation**

**1. Identify the Given Values:**
*   Mass ($m$) = $1\text{ ng}$ (Note: Mass cancels out in acceleration calculations, so the specific value doesn't affect the time).
*   Distance ($d$) = $5.0\text{ m}$
*   Angle ($\theta$) = $30^\circ$
*   Coefficient of kinetic friction ($\mu_k$) = $0.50$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
*   Initial velocity ($u$) = $0\text{ m/s}$ (assuming it starts from rest)

**2. Determine the Net Acceleration ($a$):**
The forces acting along the plane are the component of gravity pulling it down and the kinetic friction force opposing the motion.
*   Force of gravity down the plane: $F_g = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta)$
*   Normal force: $F_N = m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$
*   Force of kinetic friction: $F_f = \mu_k \cdot F_N = \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$

The net force ($F_{net}$) is:
$$F_{net} = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta) - \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$

Using Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$), the acceleration ($a$) is:
$$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the values:
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (\sin 30^\circ - 0.50 \cdot \cos 30^\circ)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.50 \cdot 0.866)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.433)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot 0.067 = 0.67\text{ m/s}^2$$

**3. Calculate the Time ($t$):**
Using the kinematic equation for distance:
$$d = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$
Since $u = 0$:
$$d = \frac{1}{2}at^2 \implies t = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{a}}$$

Substitute the values:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 5.0}{0.67}}$$
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{10}{0.67}}$$
$$t = \sqrt{14.925}$$
$$t \approx 3.86\text{ seconds}$$

**Final Answer:**
The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately **$3.86\text{ seconds}$**.

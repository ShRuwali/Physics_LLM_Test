### Description
A block of mass $m = 1$ nanogram and dimensions $1 \text{ nm} \times 1 \text{ nm} \times 1 \text{ nm}$ is placed on an inclined plane. The plane is inclined at an angle $\theta = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_k$ between the block and the plane is $0.50$. The acceleration due to gravity $g$ is $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$. The block starts from rest and slides down the incline for a distance $d = 5.0$ meters.

### Calculation of Total Time

**1. Identify the forces acting on the block along the incline:**
The force of gravity acting down the slope is:
$$F_g = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta)$$

The normal force acting perpendicular to the slope is:
$$F_N = m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$

The kinetic frictional force acting against the motion is:
$$F_f = \mu_k \cdot F_N = \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$

**2. Calculate the net acceleration ($a$):**
Using Newton's Second Law ($F_{net} = m \cdot a$):
$$m \cdot a = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta) - \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$
The mass $m$ cancels out:
$$a = g \cdot (\sin(\theta) - \mu_k \cdot \cos(\theta))$$

Substitute the given values:
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (\sin(30^\circ) - 0.50 \cdot \cos(30^\circ))$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.50 \cdot 0.866)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.433)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot 0.067$$
$$a = 0.67 \text{ m/s}^2$$

**3. Calculate the total time ($t$):**
Using the kinematic equation for distance starting from rest ($u = 0$):
$$d = \frac{1}{2} \cdot a \cdot t^2$$
Rearranging to solve for $t$:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot d}{a}}$$

Substitute the values:
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 5.0}{0.67}}$$
$$t = \sqrt{\frac{10}{0.67}}$$
$$t = \sqrt{14.925}$$
$$t \approx 3.86 \text{ seconds}$$

**Final Answer:**
The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately **3.86 seconds**.

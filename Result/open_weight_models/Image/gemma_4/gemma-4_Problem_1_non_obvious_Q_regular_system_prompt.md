### **Description of the Problem**
A block of mass $m = 1 \text{ nanogram}$ is placed on an inclined plane at an angle of $\theta = 30^\circ$. The block starts from rest at a distance of $5.0 \text{ meters}$ from the bottom of the incline. We need to find the total time taken for the block to slide down this distance.

**Note on the Coefficient of Friction:** The image states $\mu_k = -0.50$. In standard physics, a negative coefficient of friction is physically impossible as it would imply the surface is "pushing" the block forward. However, for the purpose of this calculation, we will treat the magnitude as $0.50$ and assume the friction acts against the direction of motion (up the incline).

---

### **Given Data:**
*   **Distance ($s$):** $5.0 \text{ m}$
*   **Angle ($\theta$):** $30^\circ$
*   **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$):** $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$
*   **Coefficient of kinetic friction ($\mu_k$):** $0.50$ (magnitude)
*   **Initial velocity ($u$):** $0 \text{ m/s}$ (starts from rest)

---

### **Calculation Steps:**

**1. Identify the forces acting along the incline:**
*   The component of gravity pulling the block down the slope is: $F_g = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta)$
*   The normal force perpendicular to the slope is: $N = m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$
*   The kinetic friction force opposing the motion is: $f_k = \mu_k \cdot N = \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$

**2. Calculate the net acceleration ($a$):**
Using Newton's Second Law ($F_{net} = m \cdot a$):
$$m \cdot a = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta) - \mu_k \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$
The mass ($m$) cancels out:
$$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k \cos\theta)$$

Substitute the values:
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (\sin 30^\circ - 0.50 \cdot \cos 30^\circ)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.50 \cdot 0.866)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot (0.5 - 0.433)$$
$$a = 10.0 \cdot 0.067 = 0.67 \text{ m/s}^2$$

**3. Calculate the time ($t$):**
Using the kinematic equation for distance: $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
Since $u = 0$:
$$s = \frac{1}{2}at^2$$
$$5.0 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0.67 \cdot t^2$$
$$t^2 = \frac{10.0}{0.67}$$
$$t^2 \approx 14.925$$
$$t = \sqrt{14.925} \approx 3.86 \text{ seconds}$$

### **Final Answer:**
The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately **3.86 seconds**.

### **Calculation of Time Period ($T$)**
For a simple pendulum undergoing small displacements, the formula for the time period is:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

**Given Values:**
*   $L = 50 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$
*   $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$
*   $\pi \approx 3.14159$

**Step-by-step Calculation:**
1.  Substitute the values into the formula:
    $$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{50 \times 10^{-9}}{10}}$$
2.  Simplify the fraction inside the square root:
    $$T = 2\pi \sqrt{5 \times 10^{-9}}$$
3.  Calculate the square root:
    $$\sqrt{5 \times 10^{-9}} \approx \sqrt{50 \times 10^{-10}} \approx 7.07 \times 10^{-5}$$
4.  Multiply by $2\pi$:
    $$T \approx 2 \times 3.14159 \times 7.07 \times 10^{-5}$$
    $$T \approx 4.44 \times 10^{-4}\text{ seconds}$$

### Description
The image depicts a simple pendulum system consisting of a sphere (bob) suspended from a fixed support by a string of negligible mass. The sphere has a diameter of $1\text{ nm}$ and a mass of $2.5\text{ nanograms}$. The length of the string (distance from the support to the center of the sphere) is $L = 2.0\text{ meters}$. The acceleration due to gravity is given as $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$. The goal is to determine the time period of oscillation for small angular displacements.

### Calculation of Time Period
For a simple pendulum undergoing small oscillations, the time period $T$ is given by the formula:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

**Given values:**
*   Length of the string ($L$) = $2.0\text{ m}$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

**Step-by-step calculation:**
1. Substitute the given values into the formula:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{2.0}{10.0}}$$

2. Simplify the fraction inside the square root:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{0.2}$$

3. Calculate the square root:
$$\sqrt{0.2} \approx 0.4472$$

4. Multiply by $2\pi$:
$$T \approx 2 \times 3.14159 \times 0.4472$$
$$T \approx 2.8099\text{ seconds}$$

**Final Answer:**
The time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately $2.81\text{ seconds}$.

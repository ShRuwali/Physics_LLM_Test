Based on the image provided and the principles of physics, here is the description and calculation for the velocity of the solid sphere.

### **Description**
The problem describes a solid sphere of mass $m = 2.0\text{ kg}$ starting from rest at a height $h = 7.0\text{ m}$ on an inclined plane. The sphere rolls down the plane without slipping. Because it rolls without slipping, both translational kinetic energy and rotational kinetic energy are involved. The acceleration due to gravity is given as $g = 10.0\text{ m/s}^2$.

---

### **Calculation**

**1. Identify the Given Values:**
*   Mass ($m$) = $2.0\text{ kg}$
*   Height ($h$) = $7.0\text{ m}$
*   Gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
*   Moment of Inertia for a solid sphere ($I$) = $\frac{2}{5}mr^2$
*   Condition: Rolling without slipping ($v = \omega r$)

**2. Conservation of Energy Principle:**
The total mechanical energy at the top (Potential Energy) is equal to the total mechanical energy at the bottom (Kinetic Energy).

$$PE_{top} = KE_{bottom}$$
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

**3. Substitute the variables:**
Substitute $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$ and $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$ into the equation:

$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$

**4. Simplify and solve for $v$:**
Notice that mass ($m$) cancels out from all terms:
$$gh = \left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{5}\right)v^2$$
$$gh = \left(\frac{5+2}{10}\right)v^2$$
$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

Rearrange to solve for $v$:
$$v^2 = \frac{10gh}{7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10gh}{7}}$$

**5. Plug in the numerical values:**
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10 \times 10.0 \times 7.0}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{700}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100}$$
$$v = 10.0\text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is **$10.0\text{ m/s}$**.

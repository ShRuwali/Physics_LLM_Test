### Description of the Problem

The problem involves a solid sphere of mass $m = 4 \text{ nanograms}$ and diameter $d = 8 \text{ nanometers}$ starting from rest at a height $h = 7.0 \text{ meters}$ on an inclined plane. The sphere rolls down the plane without slipping. We are required to find the velocity of the sphere at the bottom of the plane using the principle of conservation of energy.

### Given Data

*   Mass of the solid sphere ($m$): $4 \text{ ng} = 4 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg}$
*   Diameter of the sphere ($d$): $8 \text{ nm} = 8 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$
*   Radius of the sphere ($r$): $\frac{d}{2} = 4 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$): $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$
*   Initial height ($h$): $7.0 \text{ m}$
*   Initial velocity ($v_i$): $0 \text{ m/s}$ (starts from rest)

### Conservation of Energy Calculation

Since the sphere rolls without slipping, the total mechanical energy at the top of the incline is equal to the total mechanical energy at the bottom. The total energy consists of gravitational potential energy, translational kinetic energy, and rotational kinetic energy.

**1. Energy at the top:**
The sphere starts from rest, so the kinetic energy is zero. The total energy is purely potential energy:
$$E_{top} = mgh$$

**2. Energy at the bottom:**
At the bottom, the potential energy is zero. The total energy is the sum of translational and rotational kinetic energy:
$$E_{bottom} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

Where:
*   $v$ is the linear velocity at the bottom.
*   $I$ is the moment of inertia of a solid sphere: $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$.
*   $\omega$ is the angular velocity. Since it rolls without slipping, $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$.

**3. Equating the energies:**
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$

Simplify the equation:
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$
$$mgh = \left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{5}\right)mv^2$$
$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

**4. Solving for velocity ($v$):**
The mass $m$ cancels out from both sides:
$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$
$$v^2 = \frac{10gh}{7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10gh}{7}}$$

**5. Substituting the values:**
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10 \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 \times 7.0 \text{ m}}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{700}{7}} \text{ m/s}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100} \text{ m/s}$$
$$v = 10 \text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is $10 \text{ m/s}$.

### **Description**
The problem describes a solid sphere of mass $m$ and diameter $d$ starting from rest at a height $h$ on an inclined plane. The sphere rolls down the plane without slipping. We are asked to find its final velocity at the bottom of the plane using the principle of conservation of energy.

### **Given Data**
*   **Mass ($m$):** $4\text{ nanograms} = 4 \times 10^{-9}\text{ g} = 4 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$
*   **Diameter ($d$):** $8\text{ nm} = 8 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$ (Radius $r = 4 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$)
*   **Height ($h$):** $7.0\text{ m}$
*   **Gravity ($g$):** $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
*   **Moment of Inertia for a solid sphere ($I$):** $\frac{2}{5}mr^2$
*   **Condition:** Rolling without slipping ($v = \omega r$)

---

### **Calculation**

**1. Conservation of Energy Principle:**
The total mechanical energy at the top (Potential Energy) is equal to the total mechanical energy at the bottom (Kinetic Energy).
$$PE_{top} = KE_{bottom}$$
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

**2. Substitute the Moment of Inertia and Rolling Condition:**
Since the sphere rolls without slipping, we substitute $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$ and $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$:
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$
$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$

**3. Simplify the Equation:**
The mass ($m$) cancels out from both sides:
$$gh = \left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{5}\right)v^2$$
$$gh = \left(\frac{5+2}{10}\right)v^2 = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$

**4. Solve for Velocity ($v$):**
$$v^2 = \frac{10gh}{7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10gh}{7}}$$

**5. Plug in the numerical values:**
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10 \times 10.0 \times 7.0}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{700}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100}$$
$$v = 10\text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is **$10\text{ m/s}$**.

### **Calculation of Tension ($T$)**
To find the tension at the bottom of the circle, we apply Newton's Second Law ($\sum F = ma$).

At the bottom of the vertical circle, two main forces act on the mass:
1.  **Tension ($T$):** Acting upwards toward the center of the circle.
2.  **Weight ($mg$):** Acting downwards away from the center.

The net force provides the **centripetal acceleration** ($a_c = \frac{v^2}{R}$) required to keep the mass in circular motion. The equation is:
$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

To solve for Tension ($T$):
$$T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{R}$$
$$T = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{R} \right)$$

$$T = 5.0 \left( 10.0 + \frac{5.0^2}{5.0} \right)$$
$$T = 5.0 \left( 10.0 + \frac{25}{5.0} \right)$$
$$T = 5.0 \left( 10.0 + 5.0 \right)$$
$$T = 5.0 \times 15.0$$
$$T = 75.0\text{ N}$$

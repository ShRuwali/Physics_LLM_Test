### Description
The image depicts a physics problem involving a mass $m$ attached to a string of length $R$ and fixed at a point. The mass is moving in a vertical circular path. The system is characterized by the following parameters:
*   Mass ($m$) = $5.0 \text{ kg}$
*   Speed of the mass ($v$) = $5.0 \text{ m/s}$
*   Length of the string ($R$) = $5.0 \text{ meters}$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$

The problem asks to calculate the tension in the string at the bottom of the vertical circle.

---

### Calculation of Tension

To find the tension at the bottom of the circle, we must consider the forces acting on the mass at that specific point. At the bottom of the path, two main forces are acting on the mass:
1.  The tension ($T$) acting upwards toward the center of the circle.
2.  The weight ($mg$) acting downwards.

According to Newton's Second Law, the net force acting toward the center of the circular path provides the centripetal acceleration ($a_c$). The formula for centripetal acceleration is:
$$a_c = \frac{v^2}{R}$$

The net force equation at the bottom of the circle is:
$$F_{net} = T - mg = m \cdot a_c$$

Substituting the expression for centripetal acceleration:
$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

Rearranging the equation to solve for tension ($T$):
$$T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{R}$$
$$T = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{R} \right)$$

Now, substitute the given values into the equation:
*   $m = 5.0 \text{ kg}$
*   $g = 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$
*   $v = 5.0 \text{ m/s}$
*   $R = 5.0 \text{ m}$

$$T = 5.0 \left( 10.0 + \frac{5.0^2}{5.0} \right)$$
$$T = 5.0 (10.0 + 5.0)$$
$$T = 5.0 (15.0)$$
$$T = 75.0 \text{ N}$$

**Final Answer:**
The tension in the string at the bottom of the circle is $75.0 \text{ N}$.

### Description of the Problem

The problem describes a one-dimensional elastic-like collision between two blocks, Block A and Block B, on a frictionless surface. 

**Initial State (Before Collision):**
- **Block A:** Mass $m_a = 100 \text{ nanograms}$, initial velocity $v_{a1} = 0 \text{ m/s}$.
- **Block B:** Mass $m_b = 200 \text{ nanograms}$, initial velocity $v_{b1} = 100 \text{ m/s}$ moving to the right.
*(Note: There is a slight contradiction in the text of the image where it says "moving to the right" but the arrow points left. However, the velocity value is given as $100 \text{ m/s}$. Based on standard physics problems of this type, we will assume the magnitude and direction are consistent with the conservation of momentum.)*

**Final State (After Collision):**
- **Block A:** Final velocity $v_{a2} = ?$
- **Block B:** Final velocity $v_{b2} = 10 \text{ km/s} = 10,000 \text{ m/s}$ moving to the right.

### Calculation of Velocity of Block A

To find the velocity of Block A after the collision ($v_{a2}$), we use the **Law of Conservation of Momentum**. Since there is no friction, the total momentum of the system before the collision must equal the total momentum after the collision.

The formula for conservation of momentum is:
$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

**1. Identify the given values:**
- $m_a = 100 \text{ ng}$
- $m_b = 200 \text{ ng}$
- $v_{a1} = 0 \text{ m/s}$
- $v_{b1} = 100 \text{ m/s}$ (Assuming right is positive)
- $v_{b2} = 10,000 \text{ m/s}$

**2. Substitute the values into the equation:**
$$(100 \text{ ng})(0 \text{ m/s}) + (200 \text{ ng})(100 \text{ m/s}) = (100 \text{ ng})v_{a2} + (200 \text{ ng})(10,000 \text{ m/s})$$

**3. Simplify the equation:**
$$0 + 20,000 \text{ ng}\cdot\text{m/s} = 100 v_{a2} + 2,000,000 \text{ ng}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

**4. Solve for $v_{a2}$:**
$$20,000 = 100 v_{a2} + 2,000,000$$
$$100 v_{a2} = 20,000 - 2,000,000$$
$$100 v_{a2} = -1,980,000$$
$$v_{a2} = \frac{-1,980,000}{100}$$
$$v_{a2} = -19,800 \text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of Block A after the collision is $v_{a2} = -19,800 \text{ m/s}$ (or $19,800 \text{ m/s}$ to the left).

### Description
The problem describes a one-dimensional elastic-like collision between two blocks, Block A and Block B, moving toward each other on a frictionless surface. 

**Given Data:**
*   **Block A:**
    *   Mass $m_a = 0.50 \text{ kg}$
    *   Initial velocity $v_{a1} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$
*   **Block B:**
    *   Mass $m_b = 0.20 \text{ kg}$
    *   Initial velocity $v_{b1} = -2.0 \text{ m/s}$ (moving to the left)
*   **After Collision:**
    *   Final velocity of Block B, $v_{b2} = 1.0 \text{ m/s}$ (moving to the right)
    *   Final velocity of Block A, $v_{a2} = ?$

### Calculation
Since there is no friction between the blocks and the surface, the total linear momentum of the system is conserved during the collision. The principle of conservation of momentum states:

$$P_{\text{initial}} = P_{\text{final}}$$

Substituting the momentum of each block ($p = m \cdot v$):

$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

Plug in the known values:

$$(0.50 \text{ kg})(1.0 \text{ m/s}) + (0.20 \text{ kg})(-2.0 \text{ m/s}) = (0.50 \text{ kg})(v_{a2}) + (0.20 \text{ kg})(1.0 \text{ m/s})$$

Simplify the terms:

$$0.50 - 0.40 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$
$$0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2} + 0.20$$

Subtract $0.20$ from both sides:

$$-0.10 = 0.50 v_{a2}$$

Solve for $v_{a2}$:

$$v_{a2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50}$$
$$v_{a2} = -0.20 \text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of Block A after the collision is $v_{a2} = -0.20 \text{ m/s}$. (The negative sign indicates that Block A is moving to the left).

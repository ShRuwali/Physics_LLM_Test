### **Description**
The problem describes a one-dimensional elastic-type collision between two blocks (A and B) moving toward each other on a frictionless surface. 
*   **Block A** has a mass ($m_a$) of $0.50\text{ kg}$ and an initial velocity ($v_{a1}$) of $1.0\text{ m/s}$ to the right.
*   **Block B** has a mass ($m_b$) of $0.20\text{ kg}$ and an initial velocity ($v_{b1}$) of $-2.0\text{ m/s}$ (moving to the left).
*   After the collision, **Block B** is moving to the right with a final velocity ($v_{b2}$) of $1.0\text{ m/s}$. We need to find the final velocity of **Block A** ($v_{a2}$).

---

### **Calculation**
Since there is no friction, the total momentum of the system is conserved. The formula for the conservation of momentum is:
$$m_a v_{a1} + m_b v_{b1} = m_a v_{a2} + m_b v_{b2}$$

**1. Identify the given values:**
*   $m_a = 0.50\text{ kg}$
*   $v_{a1} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$
*   $m_b = 0.20\text{ kg}$
*   $v_{b1} = -2.0\text{ m/s}$
*   $v_{b2} = 1.0\text{ m/s}$
*   $v_{a2} = ?$

**2. Plug the values into the equation:**
$$(0.50\text{ kg} \cdot 1.0\text{ m/s}) + (0.20\text{ kg} \cdot -2.0\text{ m/s}) = (0.50\text{ kg} \cdot v_{a2}) + (0.20\text{ kg} \cdot 1.0\text{ m/s})$$

**3. Simplify both sides:**
$$0.50 - 0.40 = 0.50 \cdot v_{a2} + 0.20$$
$$0.10 = 0.50 \cdot v_{a2} + 0.20$$

**4. Solve for $v_{a2}$:**
Subtract $0.20$ from both sides:
$$0.10 - 0.20 = 0.50 \cdot v_{a2}$$
$$-0.10 = 0.50 \cdot v_{a2}$$

Divide by $0.50$:
$$v_{a2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50}$$
$$v_{a2} = -0.2\text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of Block A after the collision is **$-0.2\text{ m/s}$**. (The negative sign indicates that Block A is moving to the left).

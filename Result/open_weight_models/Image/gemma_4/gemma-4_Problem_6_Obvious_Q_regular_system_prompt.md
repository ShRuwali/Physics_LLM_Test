#### 1. Force Constant of the Spring (Fig a)

$$F = kx$$
Where:
*   $F$ is the force in Newtons (N).
*   $k$ is the force constant (spring constant) in N/m.
*   $x$ is the displacement in meters (m).

#### 2. Angular Frequency and Frequency (Fig b)

To calculate the oscillation properties, we first need the spring constant $k$. Assuming the spring in Fig (b) is the same as in Fig (a), and using the standard Hooke's Law $k = \frac{F}{x}$. 

If we assume the force $F$ in Fig (b) is the weight of the block ($mg$):
Mass $m = 500 \text{ nanograms} = 500 \times 10^{-9} \text{ g} = 5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ kg}$.
Displacement $x = 0.040 \text{ m}$.
Acceleration due to gravity $g \approx 9.81 \text{ m/s}^2$.

First, find $k$:
$$F = mg = (5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ kg})(9.81 \text{ m/s}^2) = 4.905 \times 10^{-6} \text{ N}$$
$$k = \frac{F}{x} = \frac{4.905 \times 10^{-6} \text{ N}}{0.040 \text{ m}} = 1.22625 \times 10^{-4} \text{ N/m}$$

**Angular Frequency ($\omega$):**
The formula for angular frequency is:
$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$
$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{1.22625 \times 10^{-4} \text{ N/m}}{5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ kg}}}$$
$$\omega = \sqrt{245.25} \approx 15.66 \text{ rad/s}$$

**Frequency ($f$):**
The relationship between angular frequency and frequency is $\omega = 2\pi f$:
$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$
$$f = \frac{15.66}{2 \times 3.14159}$$
$$f \approx 2.49 \text{ Hz}$$

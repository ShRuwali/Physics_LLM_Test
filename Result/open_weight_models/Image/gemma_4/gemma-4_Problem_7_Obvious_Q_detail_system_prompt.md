$$\sum F_y = 0 \implies T + F_B = W$$
Therefore, the tension is:
$$T = W - F_B$$

$$W = m \cdot g = 20.0 \text{ kg} \times 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 = 200 \text{ N}$$

$$F_B = \text{Volume} \times \text{Density of fluid} \times g$$
$$F_B = \left( \frac{m}{D} \right) \cdot d \cdot g$$

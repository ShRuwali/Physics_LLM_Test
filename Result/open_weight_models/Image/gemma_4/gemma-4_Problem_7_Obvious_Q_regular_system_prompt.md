### **Description**
The image shows a block of mass $m$ suspended by a cable in a tank of sea water. The block is in a state of static equilibrium, meaning the net force acting on it is zero. Three main forces are acting on the block:
1.  **Weight ($W$):** Acting vertically downwards.
2.  **Buoyant Force ($F_B$):** Acting vertically upwards, caused by the displacement of sea water.
3.  **Tension ($T$):** Acting vertically upwards through the cable.

### **Given Data**
*   Mass of block ($m$) = $20.0\text{ kg}$
*   Density of block ($D$) = $19.0 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
*   Density of sea water ($d$) = $1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

---

### **Calculations**

**1. Calculate the Volume of the block ($V$):**
Using the formula $\text{Density} = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}$:
$$V = \frac{m}{D} = \frac{20.0\text{ kg}}{19.0 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3} \approx 0.0010526\text{ m}^3$$

**2. Calculate the Weight of the block ($W$):**
$$W = m \cdot g = 20.0\text{ kg} \times 10.0\text{ m/s}^2 = 200\text{ N}$$

**3. Calculate the Buoyant Force ($F_B$):**
The buoyant force is equal to the weight of the displaced fluid:
$$F_B = d \cdot V \cdot g$$
$$F_B = (1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3) \times (0.0010526\text{ m}^3) \times (10.0\text{ m/s}^2)$$
$$F_B \approx 10.84\text{ N}$$

**4. Calculate the Tension ($T$):**
Since the block is in equilibrium, the upward forces must equal the downward forces:
$$T + F_B = W$$
$$T = W - F_B$$
$$T = 200\text{ N} - 10.84\text{ N}$$
$$T = 189.16\text{ N}$$

**Final Answer:**
The tension in the cable is approximately **$189.16\text{ N}$**.

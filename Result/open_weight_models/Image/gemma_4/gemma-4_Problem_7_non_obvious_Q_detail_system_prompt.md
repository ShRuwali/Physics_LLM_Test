$$F_B = \rho_{water} \cdot V \cdot g$$
$$F_B = (1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3) \cdot (10^{-18} \text{ m}^3) \cdot (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2)$$
$$F_B = 1.03 \times 10^{-14} \text{ N}$$

$$T + F_B - W = 0$$
$$T = W - F_B$$

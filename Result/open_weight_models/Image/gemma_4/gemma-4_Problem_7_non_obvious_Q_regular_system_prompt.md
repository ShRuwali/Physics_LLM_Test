### **Calculation of Tension ($T$)**

To find the tension, we use the equilibrium equation for the block:
$$\sum F_y = 0 \implies T + F_b - W = 0$$
$$T = W - F_b$$

$$W = m \cdot g$$
$$W = (10^{-9}\text{ kg}) \cdot (10.0\text{ m/s}^2) = 10^{-8}\text{ N}$$

The buoyant force is equal to the weight of the displaced fluid: $F_b = \rho \cdot V \cdot g$.
First, find the volume ($V$) of the block:
$$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}$$
$$V = (100 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}) \times (100 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}) \times (100 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m})$$

$$F_b = 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N}$$

**Step 3: Calculate Tension ($T$)**
$$T = W - F_b$$
$$T = 10^{-8}\text{ N} - 1.03 \times 10^{-17}\text{ N}$$

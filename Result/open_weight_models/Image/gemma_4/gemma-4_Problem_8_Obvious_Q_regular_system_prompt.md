### **Calculation of Gravitational Force**
To find the gravitational force ($F$), we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:
$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

**1. Identify and Convert Units to SI:**
*   **Gravitational Constant ($G$):** $\approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
*   **Mass of Planet A ($M$):** $6000\text{ nanograms}$. 
    *   $1\text{ nanogram} = 10^{-9}\text{ grams} = 10^{-12}\text{ kg}$.
    *   $M = 6000 \times 10^{-12}\text{ kg} = 6 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kg}$.
*   **Mass of Moon ($m$):** $2 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$.
*   **Distance ($d$):** $3 \times 10^9\text{ meters}$.

**2. Plug values into the formula:**
$$F = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{22})}{(3 \times 10^9)^2}$$

**3. Simplify the numerator:**
$$\text{Numerator} = 6.674 \cdot 6 \cdot 2 \cdot 10^{(-11 - 9 + 22)} = 80.088 \times 10^2 = 8008.8$$

**4. Simplify the denominator:**
$$\text{Denominator} = (3 \times 10^9)^2 = 9 \times 10^{18}$$

**5. Final Calculation:**
$$F = \frac{8008.8}{9 \times 10^{18}} \approx 889.87 \times 10^{-18}\text{ N}$$
$$F \approx 8.9 \times 10^{-16}\text{ Newtons}$$

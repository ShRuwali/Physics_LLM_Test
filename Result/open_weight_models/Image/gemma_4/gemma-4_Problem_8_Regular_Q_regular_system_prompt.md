### Calculation of Gravitational Force
To find the gravitational force between the two bodies, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

Where:
*   $G$ is the universal gravitational constant, approximately $6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.
*   $M = 6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}$
*   $m = 2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
*   $d = 3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$

**Step 1: Substitute the values into the formula.**
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(6.0 \times 10^{24}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

**Step 2: Simplify the numerator and denominator.**
Numerator: $(6.0 \times 10^{24}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{22}) = 12.0 \times 10^{46}$
Denominator: $(3.0 \times 10^9)^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{18}$

**Step 3: Calculate the force.**
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot \frac{12.0 \times 10^{46}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (1.333 \times 10^{28})$$
$$F \approx 8.898 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$$

**Final Answer:**
The gravitational force between Planet A and the Moon is approximately:
$$F \approx 8.9 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$$

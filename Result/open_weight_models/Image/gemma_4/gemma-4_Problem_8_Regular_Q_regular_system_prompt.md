### **Calculation of Gravitational Force**
To find the gravitational force ($F$) between the two bodies, we use **Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation**:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

**Given Values:**
*   Gravitational Constant ($G$) $\approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
*   Mass of Planet A ($M$) $= 6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}$
*   Mass of Moon ($m$) $= 2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
*   Distance ($d$) $= 3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$

**Step-by-Step Calculation:**
1.  **Multiply the masses:**
    $M \cdot m = (6.0 \times 10^{24}) \times (2.0 \times 10^{22}) = 12.0 \times 10^{46} \text{ kg}^2$

2.  **Square the distance:**
    $d^2 = (3.0 \times 10^9)^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{18} \text{ m}^2$

3.  **Plug into the formula:**
    $$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{12.0 \times 10^{46}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$
    $$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times (1.333 \times 10^{28})$$
    $$F \approx 8.898 \times 10^{17} \text{ N}$$

**Final Answer:**
The gravitational force between Planet A and the Moon is approximately **$8.9 \times 10^{17} \text{ Newtons}$**. According to Newton's Third Law, this is the magnitude of the force exerted by each body on the other.

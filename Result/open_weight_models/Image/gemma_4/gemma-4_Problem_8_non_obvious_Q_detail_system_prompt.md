### Calculation of Gravitational Force

To find the gravitational force ($F$) between the two bodies, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

Where:
*   $G$ is the gravitational constant $\approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
*   $M$ is the mass of Planet A. We must convert nanograms to kilograms:
    $$M = 6000 \text{ ng} = 6000 \times 10^{-9} \text{ g} = 6 \times 10^{-6} \text{ g} = 6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$$
*   $m$ is the mass of the Moon: $2.0 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
*   $d$ is the distance: $3.0 \times 10^9 \text{ m}$

Substituting the values into the formula:

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{22})}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

First, calculate the numerator:
$$(6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{22}) = 12 \times 10^{13} = 1.2 \times 10^{14}$$

Next, calculate the denominator:
$$(3.0 \times 10^9)^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{18}$$

Now, calculate the force:
$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{1.2 \times 10^{14}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$
$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot (1.333 \times 10^{-5})$$
$$F \approx 8.898 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$$

The gravitational force between the two bodies is approximately $8.9 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$.

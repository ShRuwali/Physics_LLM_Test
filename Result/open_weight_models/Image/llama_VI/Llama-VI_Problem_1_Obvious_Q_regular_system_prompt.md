**Problem Analysis**

The problem involves a block sliding down an inclined plane with a mass of 0.50 kg and an acceleration due to gravity of 10.0 m/s^2. The block is initially at rest and is expected to travel 5.0 meters.

**Step 1: Determine the Acceleration of the Block**

The acceleration of the block can be calculated using the formula:

a = g sin(θ)

where a is the acceleration, g is the acceleration due to gravity (10.0 m/s^2), and θ is the angle of the incline (30°).

a = 10.0 m/s^2 * sin(30°)
= 10.0 m/s^2 * 0.5
= 5.0 m/s^2

**Step 2: Calculate the Time Taken to Travel 5.0 Meters**

The time taken by the block to travel 5.0 meters can be calculated using the formula:

t = d / v

where t is the time, d is the distance (5.0 meters), and v is the velocity of the block. Since the block is under the influence of gravity, we can use the equation of motion:

v^2 = v0^2 + 2as

where v0 is the initial velocity (0 m/s), a is the acceleration (5.0 m/s^2), and s is the distance (5.0 meters).

v^2 = 0^2 + 2 * 5.0 m/s^2 * 5.0 m
= 50 m^2/s^2

v = sqrt(50 m^2/s^2)
= 7.07 m/s

Now, we can calculate the time:

t = d / v
= 5.0 m / 7.07 m/s
= 0.707 s

**Conclusion**

The total time taken by the block to travel 5.0 meters is approximately 0.707 seconds.

**Problem Analysis**

The problem involves a block sliding down an inclined plane with a given mass and acceleration due to gravity. The goal is to calculate the total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters.

**Given Information**

*   Mass of the block: m = 1 nanogram = 1 nanogram
*   Acceleration due to gravity: g = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Length of the inclined plane: 5.0 meters
*   Angle of the inclined plane: θ = 30°

**Kinematic Equations**

To solve this problem, we can use the kinematic equations for an object under constant acceleration. Since the block is sliding down the inclined plane, we can assume that the acceleration is constant and directed along the length of the plane.

**Equation of Motion**

The equation of motion for an object under constant acceleration is:

s = v0t + (1/2)at^2

where s is the distance traveled, v0 is the initial velocity (which is zero in this case), t is the time, and a is the acceleration.

**Acceleration Down the Incline**

The acceleration of the block down the inclined plane can be found using the formula:

a = g sin(θ)

where g is the acceleration due to gravity and θ is the angle of the inclined plane.

**Substituting Values**

Substituting the given values, we get:

a = 10.0 m/s^2 * sin(30°) = 5.0 m/s^2

**Time of Travel**

Now, we can use the equation of motion to find the time it takes for the block to travel 5 meters:

5.0 m = 0 + (1/2) * 5.0 m/s^2 * t^2

Solving for t, we get:

t^2 = 2 * 5.0 m / 5.0 m/s^2 = 2 s^2

t = √2 s

**Total Time**

Therefore, the total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is:

t = √2 s ≈ 1.41 s

**Conclusion**

The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately 1.41 seconds.

**Problem Analysis**

The problem involves a block sliding down an inclined plane with a given mass and acceleration due to gravity. The goal is to calculate the total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters.

**Given Data**

*   Mass of the block (m) = 1 nanogram = 1 nanogram
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Length of the inclined plane = 5.0 meters
*   Angle of the inclined plane = 30 degrees

**Step 1: Calculate the Acceleration of the Block**

Since the block is sliding down an inclined plane, its acceleration is given by:

a = g sin(θ)

where θ is the angle of the inclined plane.

a = 10.0 m/s^2 * sin(30°)
= 10.0 m/s^2 * 0.5
= 5.0 m/s^2

**Step 2: Calculate the Time Taken to Travel 5 Meters**

We can use the equation of motion to find the time taken:

s = ut + (1/2)at^2

where s is the distance traveled, u is the initial velocity (which is 0 in this case), t is the time, and a is the acceleration.

Rearranging the equation to solve for t, we get:

t = √(2s/a)

Substituting the values, we get:

t = √(2*5.0 m / 5.0 m/s^2)
= √2 s
= 1.414 s

**Conclusion**

The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately 1.414 seconds.

**Problem Analysis**

The problem involves a block sliding down an inclined plane with a mass of 1 nanogram (m = 1 ng) and an acceleration due to gravity (g = 10.0 m/s^2). The block is initially at rest and is released from a height of 5.0 meters. We need to calculate the time it takes for the block to travel 5.0 meters.

**Kinematic Equations**

To solve this problem, we can use the kinematic equations of motion. Since the block is under the sole influence of gravity, we can assume that the acceleration is constant and directed downward. We will use the following equation:

s = v0t + (1/2)at^2

where s is the distance traveled, v0 is the initial velocity (which is 0 since the block starts from rest), t is the time, and a is the acceleration.

**Acceleration and Velocity**

Since the block is under the sole influence of gravity, the acceleration is given by:

a = g = 10.0 m/s^2

We can also find the velocity of the block at any time t using the equation:

v = v0 + at

Since the block starts from rest, the initial velocity v0 is 0. Therefore, the velocity at any time t is:

v = at

**Distance and Time**

We are given that the block travels a distance of 5.0 meters. We can use the equation:

s = v0t + (1/2)at^2

Substituting the values, we get:

5.0 = 0 + (1/2)(10.0)t^2

Simplifying the equation, we get:

t^2 = 0.5

Taking the square root of both sides, we get:

t = √0.5

t = 0.7071 s

**Answer**

The total time taken by the block to travel 5.0 meters is approximately 0.7071 seconds.

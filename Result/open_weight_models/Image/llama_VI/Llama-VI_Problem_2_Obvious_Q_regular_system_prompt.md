**Problem Description**

The figure depicts a mass attached to a string, which is suspended from a fixed point. The mass is subjected to gravitational acceleration, causing it to oscillate. The goal is to calculate the time period of this oscillation for small displacements.

**Given Values**

*   Mass (m) = 2.5 nanograms
*   Diameter of the sphere (D) = 100 nanometers
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10 m/s^2
*   Distance between the point of support and the center of the sphere (L) = 50 nanometers

The radius of the sphere is half of its diameter.

*   Radius (r) = D/2 = 100/2 = 50 nanometers

**Step 2: Calculate the Moment of Inertia of the Sphere**

For a sphere, the moment of inertia (I) is given by:

*   I = (2/5) \* m \* r^2

Substituting the given values:

*   I = (2/5) \* 2.5 \* 10^-7 \* (50 \* 10^-9)^2
*   I = (2/5) \* 2.5 \* 10^-7 \* 2500 \* 10^-18
*   I = 0.5 \* 2.5 \* 2500 \* 10^-15
*   I = 1250 \* 10^-15 kg m^2

**Step 3: Calculate the Time Period of Oscillation**

The time period (T) of a simple harmonic oscillator is given by:

*   T = 2\pi \* sqrt(L/g)

where L is the length of the pendulum and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

*   T = 2\pi \* sqrt(50 \* 10^-7 / 10)
*   T = 2\pi \* sqrt(5 \* 10^-7)
*   T = 2\pi \* 7.071 \* 10^-4
*   T = 0.0444 seconds

**Conclusion**

The time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately 0.0444 seconds.

**Problem Description**

The problem presents a scenario where a mass is attached to a string, and we are asked to calculate the time period of oscillation for small displacements. The necessary values are provided in the figure.

**Given Values**

*   Mass (m) = 2.0 kg
*   Radius of the sphere (R) = L/2 = 1.0 meter
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Distance between the point of support and the center of the sphere (L) = 2.0 meters

**Solution**

To solve this problem, we can use the concept of simple harmonic motion (SHM). The time period of oscillation (T) for a mass attached to a string is given by the formula:

T = 2π√(L/g)

where L is the length of the string and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

**Calculation**

Substituting the given values, we get:

T = 2π√(2.0/10.0)
= 2π√0.2
= 2π(0.4472)
= 2.82 seconds

**Conclusion**

Therefore, the time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately 2.82 seconds.

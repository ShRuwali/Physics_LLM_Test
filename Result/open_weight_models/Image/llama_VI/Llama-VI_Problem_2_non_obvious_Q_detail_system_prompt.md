**Problem Description**

The problem presents a scenario where a mass is attached to a string, and we are asked to calculate the time period of oscillation for small displacements. The necessary values are provided in the figure.

**Given Values**

*   Mass (m) = 2.5 nanograms
*   Diameter of sphere (D) = 1 nanometer
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Distance between point of support and center of sphere (L) = 2.0 meters

**Solution**

To solve this problem, we can use the formula for the time period of a simple harmonic oscillator:

T = 2π√(L/g)

where T is the time period, L is the length of the string, and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

First, we need to convert the mass from nanograms to kilograms:

m = 2.5 ng = 2.5 × 10^(-9) kg

Next, we can plug in the values into the formula:

T = 2π√(2.0 m / 10.0 m/s^2)
= 2π√(0.2 s^2)
= 2π × 0.4472 s
= 2.82 s

Therefore, the time period of oscillation for small displacements is approximately 2.82 seconds.

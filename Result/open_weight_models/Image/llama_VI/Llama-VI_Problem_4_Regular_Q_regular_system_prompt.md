**Problem Description**

The figure shows a mass attached to a string, with the mass at the top of its trajectory. The mass is 5.0 kg, and the string is 5.0 meters long. The acceleration due to gravity is 10.0 m/s^2, and the speed of the mass is 5.0 m/s.

**Step 1: Identify the Forces Acting on the Mass**

There are two forces acting on the mass:

*   The force of gravity (F_g) pulling the mass downwards
*   The tension (T) in the string pulling the mass upwards

**Step 2: Calculate the Force of Gravity**

The force of gravity can be calculated using the formula:

F_g = m \* g

where m is the mass of the object (5.0 kg) and g is the acceleration due to gravity (10.0 m/s^2).

F_g = 5.0 kg \* 10.0 m/s^2 = 50.0 N

**Step 3: Calculate the Tension in the String**

Since the mass is moving at a constant speed, the net force acting on it is zero. This means that the force of gravity is balanced by the tension in the string. Therefore, we can set up the following equation:

F_g = T

Substituting the value of F_g, we get:

50.0 N = T

**Conclusion**

The tension in the string at the bottom of the circle is 50.0 N.

The gravity acts downward, and the tension acts upward.

Since the circle is moving vertically, we can apply the vertical component of Newton's second law:

m * a = T - m * g

where m is the mass of the circle, a is the acceleration of the circle, T is the tension in the string, and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

Since the circle is moving at a constant speed of 5 m/s, its acceleration is zero. Therefore, the equation becomes:

0 = T - m * g

T = m * g

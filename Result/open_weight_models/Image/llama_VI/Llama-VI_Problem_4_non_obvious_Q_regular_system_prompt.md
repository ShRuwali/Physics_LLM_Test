**Step 1: Identify the forces acting on the circle**

The forces acting on the circle are:

*   Gravity (g = 10 m/s^2)
*   Tension (T) in the string

**Step 2: Determine the acceleration of the circle**

Since the motion is vertical, the acceleration due to gravity is the only force acting on the circle. The acceleration of the circle is:

a = g = 10 m/s^2

**Step 3: Calculate the tension in the string**

The tension in the string can be calculated using Newton's second law:

F = ma

where F is the net force acting on the circle, m is the mass of the circle, and a is the acceleration of the circle.

Since the string remains taut and does not stretch, the tension in the string is equal to the weight of the circle:

T = mg

Substituting the values, we get:

T = 10 x 0.01 = 0.1 N

**Step 4: Calculate the tension in the string at the bottom**

Since the string remains taut and does not stretch, the tension in the string at the bottom is the same as the tension at the top:

T = 0.1 N

**Step 1: Identify the necessary values**

*   Mass of the block (m) = 20.0 kg
*   Density of the block (D) = 19.0 x 10^3 kg/m^3
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Density of sea water (d) = 1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3

**Step 2: Calculate the volume of the block**

*   Volume of the block (V) = mass of the block / density of the block
*   V = 20.0 kg / (19.0 x 10^3 kg/m^3) = 1.05 x 10^-3 m^3

**Step 3: Calculate the weight of the block**

*   Weight of the block (W) = mass of the block x acceleration due to gravity
*   W = 20.0 kg x 10.0 m/s^2 = 200 N

**Step 4: Calculate the buoyant force exerted by the sea water**

*   Buoyant force (F_b) = density of sea water x volume of the block x acceleration due to gravity
*   F_b = 1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3 x 1.05 x 10^-3 m^3 x 10.0 m/s^2 = 10.73 N

**Step 5: Calculate the tension in the cable**

*   Tension in the cable (T) = weight of the block - buoyant force
*   T = 200 N - 10.73 N = 189.27 N

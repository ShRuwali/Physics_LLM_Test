**Gravitational Force between Planet A and the Moon**

To calculate the gravitational force between Planet A and the Moon, we can use the formula for gravitational force between two spherically symmetric bodies:

$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

where:

*   $F$ is the gravitational force
*   $G$ is the gravitational constant (6.67408e-11 N·m²·kg⁻²)
*   $m_1$ and $m_2$ are the masses of the two bodies
*   $r$ is the distance between the centers of the two bodies

**Given Values**

*   Mass of Planet A: 6.0 x 10²⁴ kg
*   Mass of the Moon: 2.0 x 10²² kg
*   Distance between the centers: 3.0 x 10⁹ meters

**Calculation**

Plugging in the values, we get:

$$F = G \frac{6.0 \times 10^{24} \times 2.0 \times 10^{22}}{(3.0 \times 10^9)^2}$$

$$F = 6.67408 \times 10^{-11} \frac{12.0 \times 10^{46}}{9.0 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$F = 6.67408 \times 10^{-11} \times 1.3333 \times 10^{28}$$

$$F = 8.888 \times 10^{17} \, \text{N}$$

**Conclusion**

The gravitational force between Planet A and the Moon is approximately 8.888 x 10¹⁷ Newtons. This force is responsible for keeping the Moon in its orbit around Planet A.

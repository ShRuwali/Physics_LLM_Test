*   Force of kinetic friction (F_f):
    F_f = mu_k * F_n

**3. Calculate the net force and acceleration:**
*   Net Force (F_net) = F_gravity - F_f
    F_net = 60.0 - 51.96 = 8.04 N

*   Acceleration (a) = F_net / m
    a = 8.04 / 12.0
    a = 0.67 m/s^2

d = u*t + 0.5 * a * t^2

F_gravity = m * g * sin(theta)

F_normal = m * g * cos(theta)

The kinetic friction force acting against the motion is:
F_friction = mu_k * F_normal = mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)

m * a = m * g * sin(theta) - mu_k * m * g * cos(theta)

The mass (m) cancels out:
a = g * (sin(theta) - mu_k * cos(theta))

**4. Calculate the time (t):**

d = u * t + 0.5 * a * t^2

To solve this problem, we will use the standard formula for the time period of a simple pendulum.

**1. Identification of Given Data:**
*   Mass of the sphere (m) = 2.5 nanograms = 2.5 x 10^-9 grams = 2.5 x 10^-12 kg
*   Diameter of the sphere (d) = 1 nanometer = 1 x 10^-9 meters
*   Length of the string (L) = 2.0 meters
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2

**2. Analysis of the Physics:**
The formula for the time period (T) of a simple pendulum for small displacements is:
T = 2 * pi * square root(L / g)

In this formula:
*   L is the distance from the point of support to the center of mass.
*   g is the acceleration due to gravity.
*   The mass (m) and the diameter of the sphere do not affect the time period of a simple pendulum (assuming the string is massless and the displacement is small).

**3. Calculation:**
T = 2 * 3.14159 * square root(2.0 / 10.0)
T = 2 * 3.14159 * square root(0.2)
T = 2 * 3.14159 * 0.4472
T = 2.81 seconds (approximately)

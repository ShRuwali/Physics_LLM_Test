To find the time period of a simple pendulum for small displacements, we use the standard formula for the period of a simple pendulum.

**1. Identify the given information:**
*   Mass of the sphere (m) = 2.5 nanograms (Note: In the formula for the period of a simple pendulum, the mass of the bob does not affect the period).
*   Diameter of the sphere = 1 nanometer (Note: Since the string length is much larger than the diameter, we treat it as a point mass).
*   Length of the string (L) = 2.0 meters.
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2.

**2. Formula:**
The formula for the time period (T) of a simple pendulum for small angles is:
T = 2 * pi * square root(L / g)

**3. Calculation:**
*   First, calculate the ratio of length to gravity:
    L / g = 2.0 / 10.0 = 0.2

*   Next, take the square root of that result:
    square root(0.2) ≈ 0.4472

*   Finally, multiply by 2 * pi (using pi ≈ 3.14159):
    T = 2 * 3.14159 * 0.4472
    T ≈ 6.28318 * 0.4472
    T ≈ 2.81 seconds

The time period of the pendulum is approximately 2.81 seconds.

**2. Physical Law Analysis:**
Before performing the calculation, we must check the physical feasibility of the input values.
*   **The Speed of Light (c):** The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately 3.00 x 10^8 m/s.
*   **The Given Velocity:** The initial velocity provided is 3.001 x 10^8 m/s.

**Physical Law Broken:**
The problem violates the **Special Theory of Relativity**. According to the laws of physics, no object with mass (m > 0) can travel at or above the speed of light (c). Since 3.001 x 10^8 m/s is greater than 3.00 x 10^8 m/s, this scenario is physically impossible.

**3. Calculation (Theoretical):**
If we ignore the laws of relativity and proceed with classical mechanics (Conservation of Energy):

Total Energy at Top = Total Energy at Bottom
(Kinetic Energy_initial + Potential Energy_initial) = Kinetic Energy_final

(1/2 * m * v_initial^2) + (m * g * h) = (1/2 * m * v_final^2)

We can cancel the mass (m) from all terms:
(1/2 * v_initial^2) + (g * h) = (1/2 * v_final^2)

Multiply the entire equation by 2:
v_initial^2 + 2 * g * h = v_final^2

v_final = sqrt(v_initial^2 + 2 * g * h)

Substitute the values:
v_final = sqrt((3.001 x 10^8)^2 + 2 * 10.0 * (6.0 x 10^8))
v_final = sqrt(9.006001 x 10^16 + 1.2 x 10^10)

Since 9.006001 x 10^16 is significantly larger than 1.2 x 10^10, the change in velocity due to gravity is negligible at these speeds.

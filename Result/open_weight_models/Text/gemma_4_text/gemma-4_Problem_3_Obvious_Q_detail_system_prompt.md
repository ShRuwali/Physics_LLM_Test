The total mechanical energy at the top (initial) must equal the total mechanical energy at the bottom (final). Since the sphere is a rigid body rolling/sliding down a plane, we consider the kinetic energy (translational + rotational) and potential energy.

**Initial Energy (E_i):**
E_i = Potential Energy + Kinetic Energy
E_i = mgh + 0.5 * m * v_i^2 + 0.5 * I * w^2
*(Where I is the moment of inertia and w is angular velocity. However, since the initial velocity is given as a linear velocity and we are looking for the final linear velocity, we can simplify the kinetic energy term if we assume the ratio of rotational to translational kinetic energy remains constant or if the sphere is sliding).*

**Final Energy (E_f):**
E_f = 0 + 0.5 * m * v_f^2 + 0.5 * I * w_f^2

**Simplified Equation (Conservation of Energy):**
mgh + 0.5 * m * v_i^2 = 0.5 * m * v_f^2
*(Note: The mass 'm' cancels out from all terms).*

g * h + 0.5 * v_i^2 = 0.5 * v_f^2

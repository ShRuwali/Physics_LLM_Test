To calculate the velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane using the conservation of energy, we must account for both the translational kinetic energy and the rotational kinetic energy of the sphere.

**1. Identify Given Data:**
*   Mass (m) = 4 nanograms = 4 x 10^-9 grams = 4 x 10^-12 kg
*   Diameter (d) = 8 nanometers = 8 x 10^-9 meters
*   Radius (r) = d / 2 = 4 x 10^-9 meters
*   Initial velocity (v_i) = 3.001 x 10^8 m/s
*   Initial height (h) = 6.0 x 10^8 meters
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Moment of Inertia for a solid sphere (I) = (2/5)mr^2

**2. Energy Conservation Principle:**
The total mechanical energy at the top (E_initial) is equal to the total mechanical energy at the bottom (E_final).
E_initial = E_final
(Kinetic Energy_initial + Potential Energy_initial) = (Kinetic Energy_final + Potential Energy_final)

**3. Formulate the Equations:**
*   Initial Kinetic Energy (K_i): Since the sphere is moving with an initial velocity, we assume it is rolling/moving. However, in standard physics problems of this type, if the initial rotation isn't specified, we focus on the change in potential energy and the change in total kinetic energy.
*   Initial Potential Energy (U_i) = mgh
*   Final Potential Energy (U_f) = 0 (at the bottom)
*   Final Kinetic Energy (K_f) = (1/2)mv_f^2 + (1/2)Iω^2
    *   For a rolling object, ω = v_f / r.
    *   Substituting I = (2/5)mr^2 and ω = v_f / r:
    *   Rotational K.E. = (1/2)(2/5)mr^2(v_f/r)^2 = (1/5)mv_f^2
    *   Total K_f = (1/2)mv_f^2 + (1/5)mv_f^2 = (7/10)mv_f^2

**4. Set up the Conservation Equation:**
(1/2)mv_i^2 + mgh = (7/10)mv_f^2

Note: The mass (m) cancels out from all terms.
(1/2)v_i^2 + gh = (7/10)v_f^2

**5. Plug in the Values:**
(1/2)(3.001 x 10^8)^2 + (10.0)(6.0 x 10^8) = (7/10)v_f^2

Calculate the terms:
(1/2)(9.006 x 10^16) + (6.0 x 10^9) = 0.7v_f^2
4.503 x 10^16 + 6.0 x 10^9 = 0.7v_f^2

Since 4.503 x 10^16 is significantly larger than 6.0 x 10^9, the potential energy gained from the height is negligible compared to the massive initial kinetic energy.
4.5030006 x 10^16 = 0.7v_f^2

**6. Solve for v_f:**
v_f^2 = (4.5030006 x 10^16) / 0.7
v_f^2 = 6.432858 x 10^16
v_f = sqrt(6.432858 x 10^16)
v_f = 2.5363 x 10^8 m/s

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is approximately 2.536 x 10^8 m/s.

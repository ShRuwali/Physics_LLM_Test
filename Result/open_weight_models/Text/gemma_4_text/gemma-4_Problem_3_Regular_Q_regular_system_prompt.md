To calculate the velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane, we use the principle of Conservation of Mechanical Energy.

**1. Identify the Initial and Final Energy States:**
*   **Initial State (at the top):** The sphere is at rest, so its kinetic energy is zero. Its energy is entirely gravitational potential energy.
    *   Potential Energy (PE) = m * g * h
*   **Final State (at the bottom):** The potential energy is zero. The energy has been converted into kinetic energy. Since the sphere is rolling without slipping, the kinetic energy consists of both translational and rotational components.
    *   Kinetic Energy (KE) = KE_translational + KE_rotational
    *   KE = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * I * omega^2

**2. Define the Properties of a Solid Sphere:**
*   Moment of Inertia (I) for a solid sphere = (2/5) * m * r^2
*   Relationship between angular velocity (omega) and linear velocity (v) for rolling without slipping: omega = v / r

**3. Set up the Energy Equation:**
Initial Energy = Final Energy
m * g * h = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * I * (v / r)^2

Substitute the formula for I:
m * g * h = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * (2/5 * m * r^2) * (v^2 / r^2)

**4. Simplify the Equation:**
Notice that the mass (m) and the radius (r) cancel out:
m * g * h = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/5) * m * v^2
g * h = (1/2) * v^2 + (1/5) * v^2
g * h = (5/10 + 2/10) * v^2
g * h = (7/10) * v^2

**5. Solve for Velocity (v):**
v^2 = (10 * g * h) / 7
v = sqrt((10 * g * h) / 7)

To solve this problem, we will use the principle of Conservation of Mechanical Energy.

**1. Identification of Given Data:**
*   Mass of the sphere (m) = 4 nanograms = 4 x 10^-9 grams = 4 x 10^-12 kg
*   Diameter of the sphere (d) = 8 nanometers = 8 x 10^-9 meters
*   Radius of the sphere (r) = d / 2 = 4 x 10^-9 meters
*   Height of the plane (h) = 7.0 meters
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Initial velocity (u) = 0 m/s (released from rest)

**2. Physical Principles:**
Since the sphere is a rigid body rolling down an inclined plane without slipping, the total mechanical energy at the top is equal to the total mechanical energy at the bottom.
Total Energy = Kinetic Energy (Translational + Rotational) + Potential Energy.

At the top:
Total Energy = Potential Energy (PE) = m * g * h

At the bottom:
Total Energy = Kinetic Energy (KE) = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * I * omega^2
Where:
*   v is the linear velocity.
*   I is the moment of inertia of a solid sphere (I = 2/5 * m * r^2).
*   omega (ω) is the angular velocity (ω = v / r).

**3. Calculation:**
Substitute the values into the energy equation:
m * g * h = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * (2/5 * m * r^2) * (v / r)^2

Notice that the mass (m) cancels out from both sides:
g * h = (1/2) * v^2 + (1/5) * v^2
g * h = (5/10) * v^2 + (2/10) * v^2
g * h = (7/10) * v^2

Now, solve for v:
v^2 = (10 * g * h) / 7
v^2 = (10 * 10.0 * 7.0) / 7
v^2 = 700 / 7
v^2 = 100
v = sqrt(100)
v = 10.0 m/s

To calculate the velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane, we use the principle of Conservation of Mechanical Energy.

**1. Identify the Given Data:**
*   Mass (m) = 4 nanograms = 4 x 10^-9 grams = 4 x 10^-12 kg
*   Diameter (d) = 8 nanometers (Note: Since the sphere is a rigid body and we are looking for final velocity, the diameter is used to determine the moment of inertia, but it does not affect the potential energy).
*   Height (h) = 7.0 meters
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 10.0 m/s^2
*   Initial velocity (u) = 0 m/s (released from rest)

**2. Energy Equations:**
At the top of the incline, the sphere has only Gravitational Potential Energy (PE). At the bottom, this energy is converted into both Translational Kinetic Energy (KE_trans) and Rotational Kinetic Energy (KE_rot).

*   Initial Energy (Top) = m * g * h
*   Final Energy (Bottom) = (1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/2) * I * w^2

**3. Rotational Dynamics:**
For a solid sphere, the Moment of Inertia (I) is:
I = (2/5) * m * r^2
Where r is the radius.

The angular velocity (w) is related to linear velocity (v) by:
w = v / r

Substituting these into the Rotational Kinetic Energy formula:
KE_rot = (1/2) * (2/5 * m * r^2) * (v / r)^2
KE_rot = (1/5) * m * v^2

**4. Conservation of Energy Calculation:**
Total Kinetic Energy = PE
(1/2) * m * v^2 + (1/5) * m * v^2 = m * g * h

Combine the kinetic energy terms:
(5/10) * m * v^2 + (2/10) * m * v^2 = m * g * h
(7/10) * m * v^2 = m * g * h

Notice that mass (m) cancels out from both sides:
(7/10) * v^2 = g * h
v^2 = (10 * g * h) / 7

**5. Final Calculation:**
v^2 = (10 * 10.0 * 7.0) / 7
v^2 = 700 / 7
v^2 = 100
v = sqrt(100)
v = 10.0 m/s

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is 10.0 m/s.

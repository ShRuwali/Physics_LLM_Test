To solve this problem, we will use the **Law of Conservation of Momentum**. This law states that in an isolated system (where no external forces like friction are acting), the total momentum before the collision is equal to the total momentum after the collision.

### 1. Identify the Given Data:
*   **Block A:**
    *   Mass (ma) = 0.50 kg
    *   Initial Velocity (va1) = 1.0 m/s (Right is positive)
*   **Block B:**
    *   Mass (mb) = 0.20 kg
    *   Initial Velocity (vb1) = -2.0 m/s (Left is negative)
    *   Final Velocity (vb2) = 1.0 m/s (Moves to the right)

### 2. Formula:
The conservation of momentum equation is:
(ma * va1) + (mb * vb1) = (ma * va2) + (mb * vb2)

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation:

**Step A: Calculate Total Initial Momentum**
Initial Momentum = (0.50 kg * 1.0 m/s) + (0.20 kg * -2.0 m/s)
Initial Momentum = 0.50 kg*m/s + (-0.40 kg*m/s)
Initial Momentum = 0.10 kg*m/s

**Step B: Set up the equation for Final Momentum**
Final Momentum = (0.50 kg * va2) + (0.20 kg * 1.0 m/s)
Final Momentum = (0.50 * va2) + 0.20 kg*m/s

**Step C: Equate Initial and Final Momentum**
0.10 kg*m/s = (0.50 * va2) + 0.20 kg*m/s

**Step D: Solve for va2**
Subtract 0.20 from both sides:
0.10 - 0.20 = 0.50 * va2
-0.10 = 0.50 * va2

Divide by 0.50:
va2 = -0.10 / 0.50
va2 = -0.20 m/s

### Final Answer:
The final velocity of block A (va2) is **-0.20 m/s**. (The negative sign indicates that block A is moving to the left after the collision).

***

### Physics Law Check:
*   **Conservation of Momentum:** This law is **not broken**. The total momentum before the collision (0.10 kg*m/s) is equal to the total momentum after the collision (0.10 kg*m/s).
*   **Kinematics/Dynamics:** The problem is physically consistent. Since the total momentum was positive (moving right) and Block B gained significant momentum to the right, Block A had to lose momentum and move to the left to balance the system.

**Conclusion:** The problem is physically sound and can be solved.

When the object is at the bottom of the vertical circle, two main forces are acting on it:
*   The Tension (T) in the string, acting upwards toward the center of the circle.
*   The Weight (mg) of the object, acting downwards away from the center.

The net force acting toward the center of the circle is the centripetal force (Fc). The equation for the net force is:
Net Force = Tension - Weight = Centripetal Force
T - mg = m * (v^2 / R)

T = m * (v^2 / R) + mg
T = m * (v^2 / R + g)

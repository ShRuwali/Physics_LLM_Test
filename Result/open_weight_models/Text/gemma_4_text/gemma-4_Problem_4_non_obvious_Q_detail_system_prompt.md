### 2. Physics Principles
For an object moving in a vertical circle, the net force acting towards the center of the circle provides the centripetal acceleration. At the bottom of the circle, two forces act on the mass:
1.  The Tension (T) in the string, acting upwards (towards the center).
2.  The Weight (mg) of the object, acting downwards (away from the center).

The equation of motion at the bottom is:
Net Force = Tension - Weight = mass * centripetal acceleration
T - mg = m * (v^2 / R)

To solve this problem, we will break it down into three parts: finding the force constant, the angular frequency, and the frequency of oscillation.

**1. Calculation of the Force Constant (k)**
According to Hooke's Law, the force (F) exerted by a spring is directly proportional to its displacement (x). The formula is:
F = k * x

Given:
Force (F) = 5.0 N
Displacement (x) = 0.020 m

Rearranging the formula to solve for k:
k = F / x
k = 5.0 N / 0.020 m
k = 250 N/m

**2. Calculation of Angular Frequency (omega)**
The angular frequency of a mass-spring system is determined by the force constant (k) and the mass (m) of the object. The formula is:
omega = the square root of (k / m)

Given:
k = 250 N/m
m = 0.40 kg

Calculation:
omega = square root of (250 / 0.40)
omega = square root of 625
omega = 25 rad/s

**3. Calculation of Frequency (f)**
The frequency is the number of oscillations per second and is related to the angular frequency by the formula:
f = omega / (2 * pi)

Calculation:
f = 25 / (2 * 3.14159)
f = 25 / 6.28318
f = 3.98 rad/s (approximately 3.98 Hz)

**Final Answers:**
Force Constant (k) = 250 N/m
Angular Frequency (omega) = 25 rad/s
Frequency (f) = 3.98 Hz

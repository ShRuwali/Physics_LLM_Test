To solve this problem, we will use the **Law of Conservation of Momentum**. Since there is no friction and the collision occurs in a linear track, the total momentum of the system before the collision must equal the total momentum of the system after the collision.

    *   Initial Velocity (va1) = 1.0 m/s
    *   Final Velocity (va2) = ?
*   **Block B:**

    *   Final Velocity (vb2) = 1.0 m/s (positive because it moves to the right)

*(Note: The dimensions of the blocks are provided but are not necessary for calculating linear momentum.)*

### 2. Set up the Conservation of Momentum equation:
The formula is:
ma * va1 + mb * vb1 = ma * va2 + mb * vb2

Since every term contains 10^-10, we can divide the entire equation by 10^-10 to simplify the calculation:
(1.0) + (2 * -2.0) = va2 + (2 * 1.0)

### 5. Solve for va2:

To solve this problem, we will use the **Law of Conservation of Momentum**. Since there is no friction, the total momentum of the system before the collision must equal the total momentum of the system after the collision.

### 1. Identify the Given Information
First, let's list the known values and convert them into consistent units (SI units: kilograms, meters, seconds).

**Block A:**
*   Mass (ma) = 100 nanograms = 100 * 10^-9 g = 100 * 10^-12 kg = 10^-10 kg
*   Initial Velocity (va1) = 0 m/s

**Block B:**
*   Mass (mb) = 200 nanograms = 200 * 10^-9 g = 200 * 10^-12 kg = 2 * 10^-10 kg
*   Initial Velocity (vb1) = 100 m/s (Moving to the right, so positive)
*   Final Velocity (vb2) = 10 km/s = 10,000 m/s (Moving to the right, so positive)

**Unknown:**
*   Final Velocity of Block A (va2)

### 2. The Equation
The conservation of momentum formula is:
ma * va1 + mb * vb1 = ma * va2 + mb * vb2

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation

**Step A: Calculate Initial Momentum**
Initial Momentum = (ma * va1) + (mb * vb1)
Initial Momentum = (10^-10 kg * 0 m/s) + (2 * 10^-10 kg * 100 m/s)
Initial Momentum = 0 + (2 * 10^-8 kg*m/s)
Initial Momentum = 2 * 10^-8 kg*m/s

**Step B: Set up the equation for Final Momentum**
Final Momentum = (ma * va2) + (mb * vb2)
Final Momentum = (10^-10 kg * va2) + (2 * 10^-10 kg * 10,000 m/s)
Final Momentum = (10^-10 * va2) + (2 * 10^-6 kg*m/s)

**Step C: Equate Initial and Final Momentum**
2 * 10^-8 = (10^-10 * va2) + (2 * 10^-6)

**Step D: Solve for va2**
Subtract the momentum of Block B from the total:
10^-10 * va2 = (2 * 10^-8) - (2 * 10^-6)
10^-10 * va2 = 0.00000002 - 0.000002
10^-10 * va2 = -0.00000198
10^-10 * va2 = -1.98 * 10^-6

Divide by the mass of Block A:
va2 = (-1.98 * 10^-6) / 10^-10
va2 = -1.98 * 10^4
va2 = -19,800 m/s

### Final Answer:
The final velocity of block A (va2) is **-19,800 m/s** (or **-19.8 km/s**). 

The negative sign indicates that block A is moving to the **left**.

To solve this problem, we will use the principle of **Conservation of Linear Momentum**. Since there is no friction and the collision occurs in a linear track, the total momentum of the system before the collision must equal the total momentum of the system after the collision.

### 1. Identify the Given Information
First, let's list the known values and convert them into standard SI units (kilograms and meters per second).

**Block A:**
*   Mass (ma) = 100 nanograms = 100 * 10^-9 g = 100 * 10^-12 kg = 1.0 * 10^-10 kg
*   Initial Velocity (va1) = 1.0 m/s (moving to the right, so positive)

**Block B:**
*   Mass (mb) = 200 nanograms = 200 * 10^-9 g = 200 * 10^-12 kg = 2.0 * 10^-10 kg
*   Initial Velocity (vb1) = -2.0 m/s (moving to the left, so negative)
*   Final Velocity (vb2) = 1.0 m/s (moving to the right, so positive)

**Unknown:**
*   Final Velocity of Block A (va2) = ?

### 2. The Equation
The conservation of momentum formula is:
ma * va1 + mb * vb1 = ma * va2 + mb * vb2

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation

**Step A: Calculate Initial Momentum**
Initial Momentum = (ma * va1) + (mb * vb1)
Initial Momentum = (1.0 * 10^-10 kg * 1.0 m/s) + (2.0 * 10^-10 kg * -2.0 m/s)
Initial Momentum = (1.0 * 10^-10) + (-4.0 * 10^-10)
Initial Momentum = -3.0 * 10^-10 kg·m/s

**Step B: Set up the equation for Final Momentum**
Final Momentum = (ma * va2) + (mb * vb2)
Final Momentum = (1.0 * 10^-10 kg * va2) + (2.0 * 10^-10 kg * 1.0 m/s)
Final Momentum = (1.0 * 10^-10 * va2) + (2.0 * 10^-10)

**Step C: Equate Initial and Final Momentum**
-3.0 * 10^-10 = (1.0 * 10^-10 * va2) + (2.0 * 10^-10)

**Step D: Solve for va2**
Subtract (2.0 * 10^-10) from both sides:
-3.0 * 10^-10 - 2.0 * 10^-10 = 1.0 * 10^-10 * va2
-5.0 * 10^-10 = 1.0 * 10^-10 * va2

Divide by (1.0 * 10^-10):
va2 = -5.0 m/s

### Final Answer:
The final velocity of block A (va2) is **-5.0 m/s**. 
(This means block A is moving to the left at a speed of 5.0 m/s).

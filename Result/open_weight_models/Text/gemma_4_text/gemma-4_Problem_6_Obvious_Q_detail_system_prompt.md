Volume = Mass / Density
V = 20.0 kg / (19.0 x 10^3 kg/m^3)

**3. Calculate the Weight of the Block (W):**
Weight = Mass x Gravity
W = 20.0 kg x 10.0 m/s^2
W = 200 N (acting downwards)

**4. Calculate the Buoyant Force (Fb):**
According to Archimedes' Principle, the buoyant force is equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by the object.
Fb = Volume of displaced fluid x Density of fluid x Gravity
Fb = 0.0010526 m^3 x (1.03 x 10^3 kg/m^3) x 10.0 m/s^2
Fb = 0.0010526 x 10300 x 10
Fb = 108.42 N (acting upwards)

**5. Calculate the Tension (T) in the cable:**
The block is at rest (equilibrium), meaning the net force is zero. The forces acting on the block are:
*   Weight (W) acting downwards.
*   Buoyant Force (Fb) acting upwards.
*   Tension (T) acting upwards (since the block is denser than water and would sink without the cable).

Equation of equilibrium:
Upward Forces = Downward Forces
T + Fb = W
T = W - Fb
T = 200 N - 108.42 N
T = 91.58 N

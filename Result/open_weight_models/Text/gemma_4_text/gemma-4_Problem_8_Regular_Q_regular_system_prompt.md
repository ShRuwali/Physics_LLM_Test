To calculate the gravitational force between Planet A and its moon, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation.

**1. Identify the given values and convert them to standard SI units (kg and meters):**

*   **Mass of Planet A (M):**
    The mass is given as 6000 nanograms.
    1 nanogram = 10^-9 grams = 10^-12 kg.
    M = 6000 * 10^-12 kg = 6.0 x 10^-9 kg.

*   **Mass of the Moon (m):**
    m = 2.0 x 10^22 kg.

*   **Distance (d):**
    d = 3.0 x 10^9 meters.

*   **Gravitational Constant (G):**
    G = 6.674 x 10^-11 N·m^2/kg^2.

**2. State the formula:**
The gravitational force (F) is given by:
F = G * (M * m) / d^2

**3. Substitute the values into the equation:**
F = (6.674 x 10^-11) * (6.0 x 10^-9 * 2.0 x 10^22) / (3.0 x 10^9)^2

**4. Perform the calculation:**
*   First, calculate the product of the masses:
    M * m = (6.0 * 2.0) * (10^-9 * 10^22) = 12.0 * 10^13 kg^2.

*   Next, calculate the square of the distance:
    d^2 = (3.0 x 10^9)^2 = 9.0 x 10^18 m^2.

*   Now, divide the product of the masses by the distance squared:
    (12.0 x 10^13) / (9.0 x 10^18) = 1.333... x 10^-5.

*   Finally, multiply by the gravitational constant:
    F = (6.674 x 10^-11) * (1.333... x 10^-5)
    F = 8.898... x 10^-16 Newtons.

**Final Answer:**
The gravitational force between the two bodies at this location is approximately 8.9 x 10^-16 Newtons.

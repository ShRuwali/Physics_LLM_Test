**Step 1 – Find the net acceleration down the slope**

The forces acting parallel to the plane are  

* component of gravity down the plane: \(F_g = mg\sin\theta\)  
* kinetic‑friction force up the plane: \(F_f = \mu_k N = \mu_k mg\cos\theta\)

The net force is  

\[
F_{\text{net}} = mg\sin\theta - \mu_k mg\cos\theta
\]

Dividing by the mass gives the acceleration:

\[
a = g\bigl(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta\bigr)
\]

Insert the data  

* \(\sin30^\circ = 0.5\)  
* \(\cos30^\circ \approx 0.866\)  
* \(\mu_k = 0.5\)  
* \(g = 10\; \text{m/s}^2\)

\[
\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta = 0.5 - 0.5(0.866) = 0.5 - 0.433 = 0.067
\]

\[
a = 10 \times 0.067 \approx 0.67\; \text{m/s}^2
\]

**Step 2 – Relate distance, acceleration and time**

The block starts from rest, travels a distance \(s = 5\) m down the incline, and accelerates uniformly. Use

\[
s = \frac{1}{2} a t^{2}
\]

Solve for \(t\):

\[
t = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{a}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 5}{0.67}} = \sqrt{\frac{10}{0.67}}
\]

\[
\frac{10}{0.67} \approx 14.93,\qquad
t \approx \sqrt{14.93} \approx 3.86\; \text{s}
\]

**Answer**

The block takes about **3.9 seconds** (rounded to two significant figures) to travel the 5 m down the inclined plane.

The period of a simple pendulum for small angular displacements is given by  

\[
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} .
\]

Here the length from the support to the centre of mass is \(L = 2\ \text{m}\) and the local gravitational acceleration is \(g = 10\ \text{m/s}^2\).

\[
\frac{L}{g}= \frac{2}{10}=0.2\; \text{s}^2,
\qquad 
\sqrt{0.2}=0.4472\; \text{s}.
\]

Therefore  

\[
T = 2\pi \times 0.4472 \approx 2.81\ \text{s}.
\]

(The mass of the sphere and its tiny size are irrelevant for a simple pendulum, because the string’s mass is negligible and the sphere’s radius is infinitesimal compared with the 2‑m length.)

**Result:** The pendulum’s time period is about **2.8 seconds**.

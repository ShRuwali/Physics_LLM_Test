 f = μk N = μk mg cos θ = 0.5 × 12 kg × 10 m/s² × cos 30°  
   ≃ 0.5 × 120 × 0.866 ≃ 52 N  

The corresponding decelerating acceleration is  

 a_friction = f / m = 52 N ÷ 12 kg ≃ 4.33 m/s²  

So the net acceleration down the plane is  

 a = g sin θ – a_friction ≃ 5 – 4.33 = 0.67 m/s²  

(Positive, so the block keeps accelerating, only very slowly because friction almost cancels the component of gravity.)

**1. Acceleration down the plane**

For an inclined plane inclined at an angle \( \theta \) the component of gravity that tends to move the block down the plane is  

\( g \sin\theta \).

The normal force is \( N = mg \cos\theta \), so the kinetic‑friction force is  

\( f_k = \mu_k N = \mu_k mg \cos\theta \).

The net force along the plane is therefore  

\[
F_{\text{net}} = mg\sin\theta - \mu_k mg\cos\theta
\]

and the resulting acceleration is  

\[
a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta).
\]

Plugging in the numbers:

- \( g = 10\;\text{m/s}^2 \)
- \( \theta = 30^\circ \)  →  \( \sin30^\circ = 0.5\), \( \cos30^\circ \approx 0.866 \)
- \( \mu_k = 0.5 \)

\[
a = 10 \bigl(0.5 - 0.5 \times 0.866\bigr)
  = 10 (0.5 - 0.433)
  = 10 \times 0.067
  \approx 0.67 \;\text{m/s}^2.
\]

**2. Time to travel 5 m**

The block starts from rest, so its distance travelled in time \(t\) is

\[
s = \frac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2.
\]

With \(s = 5\;\text{m}\):

\[
5 = \frac{1}{2}(0.67)t^2
\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
t^2 = \frac{10}{0.67}
      \approx 14.93
\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
t \approx \sqrt{14.93}
      \approx 3.86\;\text{s}.
\]

**Answer**

The block needs approximately **3.9 seconds** to slide 5 m down the inclined plane.

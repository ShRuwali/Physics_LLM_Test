T = 2π √(L/g)

where L is the length of the pendulum and g is the local acceleration due to gravity.

Given  
L = 2 m  
g = 10 m s⁻²  

Compute the square‑root term:

√(L/g) = √(2 / 10) = √0.20 = 0.4472 s

Multiply by 2π:

T = 2π × 0.4472 ≈ 2.80 s

So the period of this tiny, nanogram sphere suspended at 2 m is about **2.8 seconds**.

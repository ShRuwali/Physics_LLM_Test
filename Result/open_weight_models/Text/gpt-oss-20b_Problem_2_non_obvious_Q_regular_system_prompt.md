Need final answer no LaTeX. Just plain text. Also maybe note the mass doesn't affect the period; it's independent of mass for a simple pendulum. So T about 2.8 seconds.assistantfinalFor a simple pendulum the period for small oscillations is

\[
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
\]

where \(L\) is the length of the string (or the distance from the pivot to the center of mass) and \(g\) is the acceleration due to gravity.

Here  
\(L = 2\;\text{m}\)  
\(g = 10\;\text{m/s}^2\)

So

1. Compute \(L/g = 2 / 10 = 0.2\).  
2. Take the square root: \(\sqrt{0.2} \approx 0.447\).  
3. Multiply by \(2\pi \approx 6.283\):

\[
T \approx 6.283 \times 0.447 \approx 2.81 \text{ s}
\]

Thus the pendulum’s time period for small swings is about **2.8 seconds**.  
(The sphere’s mass and size do not affect the period in the small‑angle approximation.)

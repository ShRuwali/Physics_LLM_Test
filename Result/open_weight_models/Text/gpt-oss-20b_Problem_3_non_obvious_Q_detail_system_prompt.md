### Physical‑law check

The speed that is used, \(3.001\times10^{8}\) m s\(^{-1}\), is already slightly greater than the speed of light in vacuum (approximately \(2.998\times10^{8}\) m s\(^{-1}\)). In classical (Newtonian) mechanics nothing prohibits such a number, but in real physics no massive object can travel faster than c. The calculation above therefore does **not** represent a physically realizable situation.

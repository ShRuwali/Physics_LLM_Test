### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom:
In vertical circular motion, the net force acting towards the center of the circle provides the centripetal acceleration. At the bottom of the circle, the tension $T$ in the string acts upwards (towards the center), and the weight $mg$ acts downwards (away from the center).

The equation of motion at the bottom is:
$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

Rearranging to solve for tension $T$:
$$T = \frac{mv^2}{R} + mg$$
$$T = m \left( \frac{v^2}{R} + g \right)$$

Substituting the given values:
$$T = 10^{-11} \text{ kg} \times \left( \frac{(3.001 \times 10^3 \text{ m/s})^2}{0.05 \text{ m}} + 10 \text{ m/s}^2 \right)$$
$$T = 10^{-11} \times \left( \frac{9.006 \times 10^6}{0.05} + 10 \right)$$
$$T = 10^{-11} \times (1.8012 \times 10^8 + 10)$$
$$T \approx 10^{-11} \times 1.8012 \times 10^8$$
$$T \approx 1.8012 \times 10^{-3} \text{ N}$$

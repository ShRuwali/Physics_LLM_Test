### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom:

$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

To find the tension $T$:
$$T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{R}$$
$$T = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{R} \right)$$

Substituting the given values:
$$T = 10^{-11} \text{ kg} \times \left( 100 \text{ m/s}^2 + \frac{(5.0 \text{ m/s})^2}{5.0 \text{ m}} \right)$$
$$T = 10^{-11} \times (100 + 5)$$

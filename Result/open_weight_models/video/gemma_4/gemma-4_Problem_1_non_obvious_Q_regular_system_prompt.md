### Description
The problem describes a block of mass $m = 1 \text{ nanogram}$ and dimensions $1 \text{ nm} \times 1 \text{ nm} \times 1 \text{ nm}$ placed on an inclined plane. The plane is inclined at an angle $\theta = 30^\circ$. The coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu$ is $0.50$, and the acceleration due to gravity $g$ is $10 \text{ m/s}^2$. We need to find the time taken for the block to travel a distance $d = 5.0 \text{ meters}$ down the incline.

### Calculation

**1. Identify the forces acting on the block along the incline:**
*   The component of gravity acting down the incline: $F_g = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta)$
*   The normal force perpendicular to the incline: $F_N = m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$
*   The kinetic frictional force opposing the motion: $f_k = \mu \cdot F_N = \mu \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$

**2. Calculate the net acceleration ($a$):**
Using Newton's Second Law ($F_{net} = m \cdot a$):
$$m \cdot a = m \cdot g \cdot \sin(\theta) - \mu \cdot m \cdot g \cdot \cos(\theta)$$
The mass $m$ cancels out:
$$a = g(\sin(\theta) - \mu \cos(\theta))$$

Substitute the given values:
$$a = 10 \cdot (\sin(30^\circ) - 0.50 \cdot \cos(30^\circ))$$
$$a = 10 \cdot (0.5 - 0.50 \cdot 0.866)$$
$$a = 10 \cdot (0.5 - 0.433)$$
$$a = 10 \cdot 0.067 = 0.67 \text{ m/s}^2$$

**3. Calculate the time ($t$):**
Using the kinematic equation for distance starting from rest ($u = 0$):
$$d = \frac{1}{2} a t^2$$
$$5.0 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0.67 \cdot t^2$$
$$10 = 0.67 \cdot t^2$$
$$t^2 = \frac{10}{0.67} \approx 14.925$$
$$t = \sqrt{14.925} \approx 3.86 \text{ seconds}$$

**Total time taken:**
The total time taken by the block to travel 5 meters is approximately **$3.86 \text{ seconds}$**.

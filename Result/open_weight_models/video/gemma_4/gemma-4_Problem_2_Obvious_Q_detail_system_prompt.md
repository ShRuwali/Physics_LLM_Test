### Analysis:
To calculate the time period of oscillation for small displacements (a simple pendulum), we use the formula:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

*   $L = 60\text{ nm} = 60 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$
*   $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$

Substituting the values:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{60 \times 10^{-9}}{10}} = 2\pi \sqrt{6 \times 10^{-8}} \approx 2\pi \times 2.45 \times 10^{-4} \approx 1.54 \times 10^{-3}\text{ s}$$

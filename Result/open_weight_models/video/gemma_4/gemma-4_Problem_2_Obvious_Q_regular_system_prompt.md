Based on the provided image, we can model the system as a simple pendulum. For small displacements, the time period of oscillation $T$ is given by the formula:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$

### Given Data:
*   **Length of the string ($L$):** $50 \text{ nanometers} = 50 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$
*   **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$):** $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$
*   **Diameter of sphere ($D$):** $100 \text{ nanometers}$ (Note: Since the string is attached to the center of the sphere, the length $L$ remains the distance from the support to the center).
*   **Mass of sphere ($m$):** $5 \text{ nanograms}$ (Note: The mass does not affect the period of a simple pendulum).

### Calculation:
Substitute the values into the period formula:

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{50 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}}{10.0 \text{ m/s}^2}}$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{5 \times 10^{-9} \text{ s}^2}$$

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{50 \times 10^{-10} \text{ s}^2}$$

$$T = 2\pi \times \sqrt{50} \times 10^{-5} \text{ s}$$

Using $\sqrt{50} \approx 7.07$:

$$T \approx 2 \times 3.14159 \times 7.07 \times 10^{-5} \text{ s}$$

$$T \approx 44.4 \times 10^{-5} \text{ s}$$

$$T \approx 4.44 \times 10^{-4} \text{ s}$$

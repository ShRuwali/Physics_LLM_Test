Based on the provided image and the principles of physics, here is the description and calculation for the velocity of the solid sphere.

### Description
The problem describes a solid sphere (rigid body) of mass $m = 2.0\text{ kg}$ starting from rest at a height $h = 7.0\text{ meters}$ on an inclined plane. The sphere rolls down the plane without slipping. We need to find its velocity at the bottom of the plane using the principle of conservation of energy.

### Given Data
*   Mass of the solid sphere ($m$): $2.0\text{ kg}$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$): $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$
*   Height ($h$): $7.0\text{ m}$
*   Moment of inertia of a solid sphere ($I$): $\frac{2}{5}mr^2$
*   Condition: Rolling without slipping (implies $v = \omega r$)

### Calculation
According to the law of conservation of energy, the potential energy at the top is converted into both translational and rotational kinetic energy at the bottom.

**1. Potential Energy at the top ($PE$):**
$$PE = mgh$$

**2. Kinetic Energy at the bottom ($KE$):**
The total kinetic energy is the sum of translational kinetic energy ($KE_{trans}$) and rotational kinetic energy ($KE_{rot}$):
$$KE_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

Since the sphere rolls without slipping, we substitute $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$ and $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$:
$$KE_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{2}{5}mr^2\right)\left(\frac{v}{r}\right)^2$$
$$KE_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{5}mv^2$$
$$KE_{total} = \left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{5}\right)mv^2 = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$

**3. Equating Potential Energy and Kinetic Energy:**
$$mgh = \frac{7}{10}mv^2$$
The mass $m$ cancels out from both sides:
$$gh = \frac{7}{10}v^2$$
$$v^2 = \frac{10gh}{7}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10gh}{7}}$$

**4. Substituting the values:**
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{10 \times 10.0 \times 7.0}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{700}{7}}$$
$$v = \sqrt{100}$$
$$v = 10\text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of the solid sphere at the bottom of the plane is $10\text{ m/s}$.

### Calculation of Tension at the Bottom:
When the body is at the bottom of the circular path, two forces act on it:
1.  The tension ($T$) in the string acting upwards towards the center.
2.  The weight of the body ($mg$) acting downwards.

The net force towards the center provides the centripetal acceleration ($a_c = \frac{v^2}{R}$). The equation of motion at the bottom is:
$$T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

Rearranging to solve for Tension ($T$):
$$T = \frac{mv^2}{R} + mg$$
$$T = m \left( \frac{v^2}{R} + g \right)$$

Substituting the given values:
$$T = 10^{-11} \text{ kg} \times \left( \frac{(3.0011 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s})^2}{5.0 \text{ m}} + 10.0 \text{ m/s}^2 \right)$$
$$T = 10^{-11} \times \left( \frac{9.0066 \times 10^{16}}{5.0} + 10.0 \right)$$
$$T = 10^{-11} \times (1.80132 \times 10^{16} + 10.0)$$
Since $1.80132 \times 10^{16}$ is much larger than $10$, we can approximate:
$$T \approx 10^{-11} \times 1.80132 \times 10^{16}$$
$$T \approx 1.80132 \times 10^5 \text{ N}$$

To find the velocity of Block A after the collision, we use the principle of **Conservation of Momentum**. Since there are no external horizontal forces acting on the system, the total momentum before the collision must equal the total momentum after the collision.

### Given Data:
**Block A:**
*   Mass ($m_A$) = $0.50\text{ kg}$
*   Initial velocity ($v_{A1}$) = $1.0\text{ m/s}$
*   Final velocity ($v_{A2}$) = $?$

**Block B:**
*   Mass ($m_B$) = $0.20\text{ kg}$
*   Initial velocity ($v_{B1}$) = $-2.0\text{ m/s}$
*   Final velocity ($v_{B2}$) = $1.0\text{ m/s}$

### Formula:
The conservation of momentum equation is:
$$m_A v_{A1} + m_B v_{B1} = m_A v_{A2} + m_B v_{B2}$$

### Calculation:
1. **Calculate total momentum before the collision:**
$$P_{\text{initial}} = (0.50\text{ kg} \cdot 1.0\text{ m/s}) + (0.20\text{ kg} \cdot -2.0\text{ m/s})$$
$$P_{\text{initial}} = 0.50\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s} - 0.40\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$
$$P_{\text{initial}} = 0.10\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

2. **Set up the equation for total momentum after the collision:**
$$P_{\text{final}} = (0.50\text{ kg} \cdot v_{A2}) + (0.20\text{ kg} \cdot 1.0\text{ m/s})$$
$$P_{\text{final}} = 0.50 v_{A2} + 0.20\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

3. **Solve for $v_{A2}$:**
Since $P_{\text{initial}} = P_{\text{final}}$:
$$0.10 = 0.50 v_{A2} + 0.20$$
$$0.10 - 0.20 = 0.50 v_{A2}$$
$$-0.10 = 0.50 v_{A2}$$
$$v_{A2} = \frac{-0.10}{0.50}$$
$$v_{A2} = -0.20\text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answer:**
The velocity of Block A after the collision is $v_{A2} \approx -0.20\text{ m/s}$.

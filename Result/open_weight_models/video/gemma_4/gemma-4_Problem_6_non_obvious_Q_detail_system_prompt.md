The video demonstrates two scenarios involving a spring system.

---

### **1. Calculation of the Force Constant ($k$)**
From the top half of the video, we use Hooke's Law:
$$F = k \cdot x$$
Given:
*   Force ($F$) = $5.0\text{ N}$
*   Displacement ($x$) = $0.020\text{ m}$

$$k = \frac{F}{x} = \frac{5.0\text{ N}}{0.020\text{ m}} = \mathbf{250\text{ N/m}}$$

---

### **2. Calculation of Angular Frequency ($\omega$) and Frequency ($f$)**
For the bottom half, the block is oscillating. We use the mass and the spring constant calculated above.
Given:
*   Mass ($m$) = $500\text{ nanograms} = 500 \times 10^{-9}\text{ g} = 5 \times 10^{-7}\text{ g} = \mathbf{5 \times 10^{-10}\text{ kg}}$
*   Spring constant ($k$) = $250\text{ N/m}$

**Angular Frequency ($\omega$):**
$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{250}{5 \times 10^{-10}}} = \sqrt{50 \times 10^{10}} = \sqrt{5 \times 10^{11}} \approx \mathbf{707,106.78\text{ rad/s}}$$

**Frequency ($f$):**
$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{707,106.78}{2\pi} \approx \mathbf{112,539.5\text{ Hz (or } \approx 112.5\text{ kHz)}}$$

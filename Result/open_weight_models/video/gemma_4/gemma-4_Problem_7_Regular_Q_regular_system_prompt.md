The video shows a block of mass $m$ suspended by a cable above a container of sea water. The block is partially submerged in the water. The system is in equilibrium, meaning the forces acting on the block are balanced.

### **Given Data**
*   **Mass of block ($m$):** $20.0\text{ kg}$
*   **Density of block ($D$):** $19.0 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
*   **Density of sea water ($\rho$):** $1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3$
*   **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$):** $10.0\text{ m/s}^2$

### **Calculation**
To find the tension ($T$) in the cable, we must consider the forces acting on the block:
1.  **Weight ($W$):** Acting downwards.
2.  **Buoyant Force ($F_B$):** Acting upwards.
3.  **Tension ($T$):** Acting upwards.

**Step 1: Calculate the Weight ($W$)**
$$W = m \cdot g$$
$$W = 20.0\text{ kg} \times 10.0\text{ m/s}^2 = 200\text{ N}$$

**Step 2: Calculate the Buoyant Force ($F_B$)**
The buoyant force is equal to the weight of the displaced water. From the visual, the block is submerged to a depth where its submerged volume ($V_{sub}$) can be determined. 
*   Total volume of the block ($V$): $V = \frac{m}{D} = \frac{20.0}{19.0 \times 10^3} \approx 1.05 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3$
*   Looking at the video, the block is submerged exactly halfway (the water line is at the midpoint of the block).
*   Submerged volume ($V_{sub}$): $V_{sub} = 0.5 \times V = 0.5 \times 1.05 \times 10^{-3} = 0.526 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3$
*   Buoyant Force ($F_B$): $F_B = \rho \cdot V_{sub} \cdot g$
$$F_B = (1.03 \times 10^3\text{ kg/m}^3) \times (0.526 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^3) \times 10.0\text{ m/s}^2 \approx 5.42\text{ N}$$

**Step 3: Calculate Tension ($T$)**
Since the block is in equilibrium:
$$\sum F_y = 0 \implies T + F_B - W = 0$$
$$T = W - F_B$$
$$T = 200\text{ N} - 5.42\text{ N} = 194.58\text{ N}$$

The tension in the cable is approximately **$194.6\text{ N}$**.

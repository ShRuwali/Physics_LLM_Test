The video shows a block suspended by a cable above a container of sea water. The block is partially submerged in the water, and the system is in equilibrium. To find the tension in the cable, we need to analyze the forces acting on the block.

### Given Data:
*   Mass of the block ($m$) = $1000 \text{ nanograms} = 1000 \times 10^{-9} \text{ g} = 10^{-6} \text{ g} = 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$
*   Dimensions of the block: $L = W = H = 100 \text{ nanometers} = 100 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 10^{-7} \text{ m}$
*   Density of the block ($\rho_{block}$) = $1.0 \times 10^9 \text{ kg/m}^3$
*   Density of sea water ($\rho_{water}$) = $1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$
*   Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10.0 \text{ m/s}^2$

### Analysis:
The block is in equilibrium, so the upward forces must equal the downward forces.
The forces acting on the block are:
1.  **Tension ($T$)** acting upwards.
2.  **Buoyant force ($F_B$)** acting upwards.
3.  **Weight ($W$)** acting downwards.

The equilibrium equation is:
$$T + F_B = W$$
$$T = W - F_B$$

**1. Calculate the Weight ($W$):**
$$W = m \cdot g$$
$$W = (10^{-9} \text{ kg}) \cdot (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2) = 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

**2. Calculate the Buoyant Force ($F_B$):**
The buoyant force is equal to the weight of the displaced fluid.
$$F_B = \rho_{water} \cdot V_{submerged} \cdot g$$
From the video, the block is exactly half-submerged in the sea water.
The total volume of the block ($V_{block}$) is:
$$V_{block} = L \cdot W \cdot H = (10^{-7} \text{ m})^3 = 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3$$
The submerged volume ($V_{submerged}$) is:
$$V_{submerged} = \frac{1}{2} V_{block} = 0.5 \times 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3$$
Now, calculate $F_B$:
$$F_B = (1.03 \times 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3) \cdot (0.5 \times 10^{-21} \text{ m}^3) \cdot (10.0 \text{ m/s}^2)$$
$$F_B = 1.03 \times 0.5 \times 10^{-18} \text{ N}$$
$$F_B = 0.515 \times 10^{-18} \text{ N}$$

**3. Calculate the Tension ($T$):**
$$T = W - F_B$$
$$T = 10^{-8} \text{ N} - 0.515 \times 10^{-18} \text{ N}$$
Since $10^{-8}$ is much larger than $10^{-18}$ (by a factor of $10^{10}$), the buoyant force is negligible in this specific calculation.
$$T \approx 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

The tension in the cable is approximately $10^{-8} \text{ N}$.

$$M = 6000 \text{ ng} \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg/ng} = 6 \times 10^3 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$$

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{12 \times 10^{13}}{9 \times 10^{18}}$$

$$\frac{12}{9} \approx 1.333$$
$$F = 6.674 \times 1.333 \times 10^{-11 + 13 - 18}$$

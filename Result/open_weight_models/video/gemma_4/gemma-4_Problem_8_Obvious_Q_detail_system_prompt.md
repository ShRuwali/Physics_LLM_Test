The video presents a physics problem involving two bodies: **Planet A** and a **Moon**.

### **Description of the System:**
*   **Planet A:**
    *   Mass ($M$) = $6000 \text{ nanograms} = 6000 \times 10^{-9} \text{ g} = 6 \times 10^{-6} \text{ g} = 6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$.
    *   Radius = $100 \text{ nanometers} = 100 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m} = 10^{-7} \text{ m}$.
*   **Moon:**
    *   Mass ($m$) = $2 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$.
*   **Distance:**
    *   Closest distance between centers ($d$) = $3 \times 10^9 \text{ meters}$.

### **Calculation of Gravitational Force:**
To find the gravitational force ($F$) between the two bodies, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:
$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$
Where $G$ (Gravitational Constant) $\approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.

**Plugging in the values:**
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{22})}{(3 \times 10^9)^2}$$
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{12 \times 10^{13}}{9 \times 10^{18}}$$
$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (1.333 \times 10^{-5})$$
$$F \approx 8.89 \times 10^{-16} \text{ Newtons}$$

### **Physical Analysis:**
**Physical Laws Broken:**
There is a massive discrepancy in the scales of the objects provided:
1.  **Mass vs. Size:** Planet A is described as having a mass of only 6000 nanograms (about the weight of a small grain of sand) but a radius of 100 nanometers (the size of a large virus). For a spherical object of that radius to have that mass, its density would be extremely low, but more importantly, a "Planet" with a mass of $10^{-9} \text{ kg}$ is physically impossible in a celestial context.
2.  **Scale Inconsistency:** The "Moon" has a mass of $2 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$ (comparable to Earth's Moon), but it is orbiting a "Planet" that is essentially a microscopic speck of dust.

**Can the problem be solved?**
**Mathematically, yes.** The problem can be solved using the provided numbers to reach a numerical result ($8.89 \times 10^{-16} \text{ N}$). However, **physically, the scenario is nonsensical.** A microscopic speck of dust cannot exert a meaningful gravitational influence on a celestial body like a moon, and the "Planet" would be instantly overwhelmed by the moon's gravity or simply be unable to maintain an orbit.

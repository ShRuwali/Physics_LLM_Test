$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

*   $M = 6.0 \times 10^{24} \text{ kg}$

*   $d = 3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m}$

3.  Apply the formula:
    $$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot \frac{1.2 \times 10^{47}}{9.0 \times 10^{16}}$$
    $$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (1.333 \times 10^{30})$$

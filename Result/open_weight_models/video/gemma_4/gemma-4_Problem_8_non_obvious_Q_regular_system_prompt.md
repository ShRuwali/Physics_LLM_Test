*   **Closest distance between centers ($d$):** $30 \times 10^9 \text{ meters}$

To calculate the gravitational force between the two bodies, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation:

$$F = G \frac{M \cdot m}{d^2}$$

### 1. Unit Conversions
First, we must convert all values into standard SI units (kilograms and meters).

*   **Mass of Planet A ($M$):**
    Since $1 \text{ nanogram} = 10^{-9} \text{ grams} = 10^{-12} \text{ kg}$:
    $$M = 6000 \times 10^{-12} \text{ kg} = 6 \times 10^{-9} \text{ kg}$$

*   **Mass of Moon ($m$):**
    $$m = 20 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg} = 2 \times 10^{23} \text{ kg}$$

*   **Distance ($d$):**
    $$d = 30 \times 10^9 \text{ m} = 3 \times 10^{10} \text{ m}$$

*   **Gravitational Constant ($G$):**
    $$G \approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$$

### 2. Calculation
Substitute the values into the formula:

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{23})}{(3 \times 10^{10})^2}$$

Simplify the numerator:
$$(6 \times 10^{-9}) \cdot (2 \times 10^{23}) = 12 \times 10^{14}$$

Simplify the denominator:
$$(3 \times 10^{10})^2 = 9 \times 10^{20}$$

Calculate the force:
$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{12 \times 10^{14}}{9 \times 10^{20}}$$
$$F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot (1.333 \times 10^{-6})$$
$$F \approx 8.898 \times 10^{-17} \text{ N}$$

The gravitational force between Planet A and the Moon at the given distance is approximately $8.9 \times 10^{-17} \text{ Newtons}$.
